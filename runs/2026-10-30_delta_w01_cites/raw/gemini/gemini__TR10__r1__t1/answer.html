What to Look for Before Buying Gemstone Jewelry Online:

  • Gemstone Certification: For high-value purchases (like a sapphire or emerald centerpiece), ensure the retailer provides a certificate of authenticity detailing the stone’s origin, treatment history (e.g., whether it is heat-treated), and grade.

  • Return & Resize Policies: Colored gemstone rings can be trickier to resize than plain gold bands, especially if they feature delicate side stones or custom prongs. Always check if the retailer offers free or low-cost initial resizing.
