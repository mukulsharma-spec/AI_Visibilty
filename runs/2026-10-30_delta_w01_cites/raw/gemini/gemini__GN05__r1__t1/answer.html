Tips for Buying Gemstones Online:

Check the Certification: For high-value precious stones (like a large sapphire, ruby, or emerald), look for independent lab reports (such as GIA or IGI) to verify if the stone is natural, heated, or treated.

Understand Treatments: Most colored gemstones undergo standard, accepted treatments (like heat treatment to enhance color). Reputable retailers will transparently disclose these treatments.

Review Return Policies: Ensure the retailer offers a secure, insured shipping method and a clear return window (typically 30 days) so you can evaluate the color accurately in natural daylight when it arrives.
