Because January’s birthstone is the garnet (known for its rich, deep red color), you'll find it widely available across the US.

Depending on your budget and preferred shopping style, you can find January birthstone jewelry at several top online retailers and nationwide jewelry chains:

1. Dedicated Online Fine Jewelers (Customizable & High-End)

  • Angara: Offers an extensive collection of garnet rings, pendants, earrings, and bracelets. Their pieces let you customize the stone quality, carat weight, and metal type (sterling silver, gold, platinum).

  • Blue Nile: Known for fine jewelry, they feature a sophisticated selection of garnet gemstone rings, bracelets, and necklaces, often accented with diamonds in white or yellow gold.

  • Peora: Specializes in accessible gemstone jewelry, offering hundreds of natural garnet designs ranging from sterling silver everyday pieces to 14K gold statement pieces.

2. Contemporary & Everyday Jewelry Brands

  • Mejuri: Great if you are looking for modern, minimalist, everyday pieces featuring January's birthstone.

  • James Avery Artisan Jewelry: A wonderful US-based jeweler (popular particularly in the South and online) offering sterling silver and gold birthstone charms, stackable rings, studs, and necklaces featuring genuine garnet.

3. Major Traditional Retailers & Regional Jewelers

  • Department Stores: Major department stores like Macy’s or Kohl’s carry large inventories of affordable birthstone jewelry (often set in silver or gold-plated settings).

  • Regional Fine Jewelers: Chains and local jewelers like Fink’s Jewelers, Brinker’s Jewelers, or Zales / Kay Jewelers carry dedicated birthstone collections in-store and online, allowing you to see the deep red hue of the garnet in person.
