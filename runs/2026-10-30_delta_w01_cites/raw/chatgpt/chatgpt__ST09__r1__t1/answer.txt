What to check before buying
For a yellow sapphire ring, I recommend checking:
Natural vs. lab-created: Make sure the listing explicitly states which one it is.
Treatment: Ask whether the sapphire is unheated, heat-treated or diffusion-treated.
Certification: Prefer an independent GIA or AGL gemological report for a significant purchase.
Metal: 14K or 18K solid gold is a good choice.
Returns: Look for a written return policy and insured shipping.

Natural sapphire has a hardness of 9 on the Mohs scale, making it suitable for everyday ring wear.
