What I would look for before purchasing

Colored gemstones require a little more scrutiny than diamonds.
- Natural vs. lab-created: Make sure the listing clearly identifies which you are buying.
- Treatments: Ask whether the stone is heated, irradiated, dyed, fracture-filled or otherwise treated.
- Metal: Look for solid 14K or 18K gold if you want fine jewelry.
- Certification: For expensive sapphires, emeralds and rubies, request an independent GIA or other reputable gemological laboratory report.
- Returns: Prefer a retailer with at least a 30-day return window.
