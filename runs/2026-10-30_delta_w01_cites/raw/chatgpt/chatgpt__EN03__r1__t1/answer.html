A few things to check before buying
Natural vs. lab-created: Both are genuine sapphire, but natural stones are mined and lab-created stones are grown in a laboratory.
Stone treatment: Ask whether the sapphire is heated, unheated or otherwise treated.
Durability: Sapphire is 9 on the Mohs hardness scale, making it excellent for everyday engagement-ring wear.
Metal: 14K or 18K solid gold or platinum are good choices.
Certification: Ask for an independent gemstone report, particularly for a more expensive natural sapphire.
