Strongest candidates for gemstone jewelry

Angara is the one that comes up most for colored gemstones. One independent review site rates it 4.5/5 in its gemstone jewelry category, and another guide lists it as best for colored gemstones at 4.7/5. Individual product pages on Angara’s own site show verified customer reviews, with many at five stars.

GemSelect sells loose stones and jewelry. Its Bizrate page lists 185 verified customer reviews, and the recent ones I saw were very positive on stone quality matching the photos, delivery and support.

Brilliant Earth, James Allen and Blue Nile are repeatedly named as consistently top-rated online jewelers, with praise for selection, quality and customer service. They lean toward diamonds and engagement rings, though they carry colored gemstones too.

Caveats

Most “best of” lists I found are affiliate sites. One discloses that it earns commissions from brands it lists, which influences how listings are presented. Their ratings are editorial opinions, not aggregated customer scores.
Brand-hosted reviews, like those on Angara’s product pages, tend to skew positive. Cross-check on independent platforms such as Trustpilot, Bizrate and the BBB.
A few stores that surfaced, such as Gem Bazaar (UK) and Shubh Gems (India), are good for reviews but are based outside the US, so customs, duties and return shipping would apply.

How to choose

Look for GIA or AGS certification on higher-value stones.
Check the return window, ideally 30 days or more, and who pays return shipping.
Ask whether stones are natural, lab-created or treated. Reputable sellers disclose this clearly.
