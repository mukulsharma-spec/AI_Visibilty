January’s birthstone is the garnet, usually deep red, though it also comes in orange, green, and other colors. Here are some good places to look, by budget and style.

Mainstream jewelry chains (easy to find in person or online, frequent sales)

Kay, Zales, and Jared have garnet rings, pendants, and earrings, usually in sterling silver or gold.

Macy’s and Nordstrom carry garnet pieces from fine and mid-range brands.

Costco sometimes has garnet jewelry, often at good prices.

Online specialists

Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth have higher-quality, more design-forward pieces, including some custom options.

Ross-Simons has lots of garnet jewelry at moderate prices, with frequent promotions.

Mejuri and Catbird are good for dainty, modern, everyday pieces.

Handmade and unique pieces

Etsy has everything from simple stud earrings to custom birthstone jewelry. Check seller reviews and whether the stone is natural or lab-created.

Budget-friendly

Amazon and Walmart have affordable garnet jewelry, but quality and stone authenticity vary a lot, so read reviews carefully.

Local jewelers are worth a visit if you want to see the stone’s color in person or have a piece resized or customized.

A few tips: deeper, richer red stones are generally more valued than very dark or brownish ones. Look for “genuine” or “natural” garnet in the description, since some cheaper pieces use glass or synthetic stones. Sterling silver is usually the best value, while 14k gold costs more but lasts longer.
