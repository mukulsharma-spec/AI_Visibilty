There isn’t one best place. It depends on your budget and whether you want a ready-made piece or a custom one. These are the retailers that come up most often for US shoppers.

Brilliant Earth is a good all-around choice, with a wide range of colored stones, transparent sourcing and strong customer service. Their own buying guide ranks them first, so take that ranking with a grain of salt. They describe themselves as the best all-around retailer in 2026.
James Allen lets you inspect stones closely before buying. One reviewer notes that few online jewelers let you examine a colored center stone in such detail, and their purchases include a free lifetime warranty.
Blue Nile is reliable, but colored stones are a sideline for them. One guide notes their selection is mostly limited to sapphires, rubies and emeralds.
Leibish & Co. specializes in colored diamonds and colored gemstones. Most people go there for the fancy colored diamonds, but they also have plenty of other colored stones.
Angara (from my own knowledge, not these search results) has a very wide range of gemstones and settings at moderate prices. It’s good for birthstone pieces and custom settings.
Etsy has independent jewelers and lapidaries for one-of-a-kind or custom work. Quality varies a lot, so check seller reviews and treatment disclosures.

Whichever you choose, a few things protect you:

Look for clear disclosure of whether the stone is natural or lab-created, and whether it’s been heated or otherwise treated.
For pricier pieces ($1,000 or more), ask for a report from a respected lab such as GIA or AGL.
Check the return policy, since color looks different in photos than in person.
