Here are some solid options in the US, grouped by what you’re after.

Well-known online jewelers with good return policies

Angara has one of the biggest selections of colored gemstone rings, with both natural and lab-created yellow sapphires in many settings.
Brilliant Earth has a customizable ring builder and emphasizes ethically sourced stones.
Blue Nile and James Allen offer sapphires with detailed photos and video. Their colored stone selection is smaller than their diamond selection.
Ross-Simons and Jewelry Television (JTV) are good for lower-priced natural stones.

Gem specialists and marketplaces

Etsy has independent jewelers and custom work, and often better prices. Check seller reviews carefully.
Gem Rock Auctions and Danny Gems sell loose stones, which you can have set in a ring you design.
Amazon has lots of listings, but quality and disclosure vary widely, so I’d be cautious there.

What to check before buying

Treatment: Most yellow sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal and accepted, but sellers should disclose it. Unheated stones cost significantly more.
Certification: For anything over a few hundred dollars, ask for a report from GIA, AGL, or a similar reputable lab, especially for unheated stones.
Natural vs. lab-created: Lab-created stones are chemically identical and much cheaper, but they should be labeled clearly.
Return policy: Look for at least 30 days, since color looks different in person than in photos.
Color: Pure, vivid yellow is the most valued. Stones leaning brownish or greenish are cheaper.
