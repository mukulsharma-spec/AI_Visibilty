Treating yourself to a gemstone ring is such a fun milestone! Where you should shop depends a bit on your style (classic, modern, or vintage-inspired) and whether you prefer browsing online or trying things on in person.

Here are some great spots to check out, broken down by what they do best:

1. Online Customization & Wide Selection

Angara: If you want a specific stone (like a sapphire, emerald, ruby, tanzanite, or aquamarine) and want to customize the metal type, carat size, and quality grade, Angara is a go-to gemstone specialist with massive inventory.

Blue Nile & James Allen: While famous for diamonds, both feature brilliant interactive ring builders and expansive collections of colored gemstone rings with high-definition imagery or 360-degree views.

2. Ethical Sourcing & Modern Classics

Brilliant Earth: Known for heavily emphasizing sustainability, recycled precious metals, and conflict-free or traceable gemstones. Their designs lean sleek, elegant, and modern.

Ingle & Rhode: A fantastic option if you are based in the UK and prioritize ethically sourced, fairtrade precious metals and traceable gemstones.

3. Dainty, Indie, and Everyday Styles

Catbird: Based out of Brooklyn with a cult following online, Catbird is brilliant if you love delicate, stackable fine jewelry, unique vintage-leaning designs, and lower-profile gemstone rings you can wear every day.

Mejuri: Great for modern, minimalist everyday pieces if you're looking for something sleek and understated rather than a massive statement stone.

4. Local Independent Jewelers & Antique Shops

If you want something truly one-of-a-kind, don't overlook local independent boutiques or antique/vintage dealers in your area. Estate jewelry often features gorgeous, unusual gemstone cuts and settings with a lot of character that you simply won't find at mainstream commercial retailers.
