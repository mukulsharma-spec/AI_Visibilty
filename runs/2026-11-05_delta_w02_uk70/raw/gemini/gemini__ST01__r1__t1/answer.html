Finding a garnet ring is a wonderful gift idea! Because garnets range from deep classic red to gorgeous mandarin orange, green, and pink varieties, you have some great options depending on her style.

Here are some of the best places to look for a garnet ring, ranging from handmade artisan platforms to well-known jewelers:

Online Artisan & Independent Makers

Etsy: If you are looking for something unique, vintage, or handmade, Etsy is one of the best spots. You can find independent jewelers who work with recycled metals, unique vintage settings (like Victorian or Art Deco garnet clusters), or custom designs.

Azza Fahmy or Local Independent Jewelers: For high-end artisan craftsmanship, smaller local jewelry stores often carry unique gemstone pieces that you won't find in big-box mall stores.

Contemporary & Fine Jewelers

Blue Nile / James Allen: These major online custom jewelers allow you to select your gemstone (including natural garnets) and pair it with a ring setting of your choice (such as sterling silver, yellow gold, white gold, or platinum) so you can build a custom piece.

Brilliant Earth: Known for ethical sourcing and sustainable fine jewelry, they frequently offer gemstone rings and customizable colored stone options.

Zales / Kay Jewelers / Jared: Good mainstream options if you want to visit a physical storefront in a local mall to see the ring in person before buying. They often carry gemstone fashion rings or custom gemstone options.

Tips Before You Buy

Check the Metal: Garnet is a durable stone (around 6.5 to 7.5 on the Mohs scale), but everyday wear is best supported by sturdy metals like gold or platinum if she plans to wear it daily. Sterling silver is great for more occasional wear or budget-friendly options.

Know Her Ring Size: If you aren't sure of her size, try borrowing one of her rings that fits that specific finger and tracing the inside of it on a piece of paper, or use a ring sizer tool available online.
