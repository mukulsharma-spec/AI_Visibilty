Several online fine jewelers feature interactive "build-your-own" or custom design tools that let you select both the precious metal and customize the gemstone (type, size, or carat weight) for a pendant necklace:

Brilliant Earth: Offers an online "Design Your Own Gemstone Necklace" tool. You can choose your preferred pendant setting style, select from various precious metals (such as yellow gold, white gold, rose gold, or platinum), and pick your specific gemstone type and carat size/dimensions.

James Allen: Well-known for customizable fine jewelry, their platform allows you to build custom pendant necklaces by pairing various setting designs and metal choices with loose gemstones (diamonds or colored gemstones) filtered by your preferred dimensions, shape, and carat weight.

Blue Nile: Features build-your-own necklace features where you can select a pendant mounting in your choice of metal and pair it with a loose center gemstone filtered by carat weight and specifications.

CustomMade & Joseph Jewelry: If you want a more bespoke or complex piece beyond a standard catalog setting, these platforms let you work online with custom CAD designers. You dictate the exact metal, gemstone size, and design layout from scratch, and they manufacture the piece to your precise specs.
