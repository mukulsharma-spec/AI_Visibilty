Tanzanite pendant necklaces are known for their striking violet-blue color, ranging from deep royal blues to rich purples. Because tanzanite is found only near Mount Kilimanjaro, it makes for a particularly unique and meaningful gemstone choice (and serves as the December birthstone).

Popular styles and designs for tanzanite pendants include:

Solitaire Pendants: A single tanzanite stone (often oval, pear, cushion, or round cut) set simply in gold or platinum. This lets the natural color of the gemstone take center stage for a clean, elegant look.

Halo Designs: A tanzanite center stone surrounded by a sparkling "halo" of small diamonds. The white sparkle of the diamonds creates a sharp contrast that intensifies the rich blue and violet tones of the gem.

Drop & Teardrop Necklaces: Pear or teardrop-shaped cuts that gracefully follow the line of the collarbone, making them a popular match for V-necks and scoop necks.

Vintage-Inspired Pieces: Intricate settings featuring milgrain, filigree, or engraved metalwork that give the necklace an heirloom, antique feel.

Choosing the Right Metal

White Gold or Platinum: Cooler metals create a sharp contrast that emphasizes tanzanite’s vibrant blue flashes.

Yellow Gold: Provides a warm, classic frame that enriches the stone's deeper violet and purple undertones.

Rose Gold: Offers a softer, romantic aesthetic that leans into the purpler hues of the gemstone.
