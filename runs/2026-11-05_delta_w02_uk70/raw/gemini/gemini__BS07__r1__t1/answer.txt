Tips for Buying Tanzanite Online:

Check Certification: Always buy tanzanite that comes with a certificate from a recognized gemological lab (like GIA or an independent certified gemologist) verifying its grade and authenticity.

Understand the Grading Scale: Tanzanite is graded on color saturation from violet-blue to deep royal blue. AAAA or AAA grades represent top-tier color intensity and clarity.

Consider Durability: Tanzanite ranks around 6 to 7 on the Mohs hardness scale (softer than diamonds or sapphires). For an everyday engagement ring, look for protective settings like bezels or halos, or opt for a slightly thicker band to protect the stone.
