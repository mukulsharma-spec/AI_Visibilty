When shopping for a lab-grown ruby ring, the best place depends on whether you are looking for loose stones crafted by premier gemstone creators, or fully set fine jewelry pieces with custom metalwork.

Top brands and retailers specializing in high-quality lab-grown rubies include:

1. Charles & Colvard

Best for: Sustainable luxury and certified AAA-quality created gemstones.

Why buy here: Known widely for pioneering lab-created fine jewelry, Charles & Colvard curates a dedicated "Conscious Color" collection. Their lab-grown rubies match the durability (Mohs hardness of 9) and fiery brilliance of mined stones while offering a completely ethical, conflict-free alternative paired with recycled precious metals.

2. Lily Arkwright

Best for: Designer settings and European-crafted engagement rings (with showrooms in the UK).

Why buy here: They specialize in ethically sourced lab-grown coloured gemstones and lab diamonds. Their extensive collection features custom ruby cuts set in everything from classic solitaires and vintage styles to intricate hidden halos and pavé bands, crafted in platinum or 18k gold.

3. Chatham

Best for: Industry-leading loose lab-grown stones and custom cuts.

Why buy here: Chatham is a legendary name in lab-created gemstones, producing virtually flawless, eye-clean rubies with zero enhancement treatments. While they primarily supply authorized retailers, buying a Chatham-created ruby ensures maximum color saturation and top-tier crystal clarity. You can work with their concierge service or find authorized jewelers to build a custom ring.

4. LUO Jewelry

Best for: Bold, contemporary statement pieces and modern ring architecture.

Why buy here: They offer a wide array of lab-created ruby designs that blend classic claw settings with modern geometric bands, making them a great option if you want a distinct, eye-catching cocktail or engagement ring.

Tips for Buying a Lab-Grown Ruby:

Growth Method: Most high-grade lab rubies are created using either the flame-fusion (Verneuil) or flux/hydrothermal method. Hydrothermal rubies (like Chatham's) tend to mimic natural crystal growth characteristics closely, while flame-fusion is a reliable, cost-effective classic.

Check the Metal: Because rubies are deeply hued, consider pairing them with 18k yellow gold or rose gold to accentuate the rich, warm red tones, or platinum/white gold for a high-contrast, brilliant look.
