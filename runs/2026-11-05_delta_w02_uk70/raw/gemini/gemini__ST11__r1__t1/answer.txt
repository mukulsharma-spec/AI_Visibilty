You can find ruby pendant necklaces at a variety of online jewellers and high street stores, depending on your budget, style preferences, and whether you are looking for fine luxury or customisable designs.

Online Specialist Jewellers

Angara UK: Offers a wide selection of made-to-order ruby pendants ranging from simple solitaires to diamond-accented halo and vintage styles. You can customize the metal type (white, yellow, or rose gold) and gemstone quality grade.

GLAMIRA UK: Features fully customisable ruby necklaces and pendants where you can pick the gold karat, stone size, and extra design elements like lab-grown diamonds.

TJC (The Jewellery Channel): A great option if you are looking for a diverse range of gold, silver, and gemstone styles spanning different price points.

Ruby & Oscar: Specialises in fine gemstone jewellery, showcasing various cuts and contemporary ruby necklace designs.

High Street & Traditional Jewellers

Goldsmiths: One of the UK's leading quality jewellers, carrying fine jewellery including precious gemstone pendants.

F.Hinds / H. Samuel: Well-known UK high street jewellers that frequently carry classic ruby and diamond pendant options both online and across their physical stores.
