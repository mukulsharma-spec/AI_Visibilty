Finding a ruby gift for a 40th wedding anniversary is a wonderful milestone to shop for. Depending on whether you are looking for fine jewelry, traditional ruby-themed homeware, or unique personalised keepsakes, several top options and retailers can help:

1. Fine Jewellery & Precious Stones

If you want traditional ruby jewellery (rings, necklaces, earrings, or cufflinks):

High Street Jewellers: Retailers like H.Samuel, Er Ernest Jones, and Goldsmiths feature specific gemstone and milestone anniversary collections.

Department Stores: Stores such as John Lewis & Partners carry a wide array of fine ruby jewellery set in gold and silver.

2. Personalised & Creative Keepsakes

For custom gifts like engraved glassware, photo albums, prints, or memory books:

Not On The High Street: A fantastic marketplace featuring hundreds of independent UK creators offering items like personalised ruby anniversary prints, memory boxes, cushions, and custom glassware.

Etsy: Excellent for bespoke, handmade ruby-themed gifts, custom portrait illustrations, and engraved keepsakes.

Specialist Sites: Sites like My Happy Moments and UK Gift Store Online specialise in custom-engraved crystal clocks, photo frames, and keepsake plaques.

3. Luxury Crystal & Glassware

If you are looking for high-end crystal glassware with ruby coloring or etching:

Royal Scot Crystal: Offers luxury presentation-boxed ruby crystal wine glasses, tumblers, champagne flutes, and decanters specifically curated for 40th wedding anniversaries.

4. Unique Nature Gifts

Tree2mydoor: A popular option if you want to gift a living plant or tree (such as a potted fruit tree or decorative garden plant) themed around a ruby anniversary to plant in the garden.
