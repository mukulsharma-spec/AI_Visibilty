An oval blue sapphire pendant with a diamond halo is a classic, elegant piece that is widely available from both independent fine jewelers and major fine jewelry retailers.

Where to Shop

Independent & Designer Jewelers: Boutiques like King Jewelers, Abhika Jewels, and Embark Fine Jewelry frequently feature natural oval sapphire and diamond halo pendants set in 14K or 18K white, yellow, or rose gold.

Specialty Online Jewelers: Sites like Jewelry Point, Diamond and Gold Warehouse, and custom design platforms allow you to choose your exact sapphire carat weight, gemstone grade (natural vs. lab-grown), and metal type.

Major Department Stores & Legacy Retailers: High-end department stores (such as Macy's or Nordstrom) and traditional brick-and-mortar jewelers (like Zales, Kay Jewelers, or Blue Nile) regularly stock sapphire halo necklaces, both online and in-store.

What to Look For When Buying

Gemstone Type: Decide whether you prefer a natural earth-mined sapphire or a lab-grown sapphire. Lab-grown options offer the exact same chemical and physical properties at a more budget-friendly price point.

Metal Choice: White gold and platinum enhance the deep royal blue of the sapphire and the brilliance of the diamonds, while yellow or rose gold gives the pendant a warmer, vintage or antique aesthetic.

Chain Inclusion: Check product listings carefully to see if a solid gold chain is included in the listed price or if it needs to be purchased separately.
