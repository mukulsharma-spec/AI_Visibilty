Aquamarine halo stud earrings combine the serene, pale blue sparkle of aquamarine with a brilliant border of smaller accent diamonds or crystals. Because aquamarine is the March birthstone and a popular alternative gemstone, you have plenty of great options depending on your budget and style preference.

Here are some of the best places to find them, ranging from online jewelry specialists to department stores and artisan marketplaces:

Online Jewelry Retailers

Blue Nile & James Allen: Excellent choices if you are looking for fine jewelry featuring genuine aquamarine set in precious metals like 14k white gold, yellow gold, or platinum. They allow you to customize or select the specific gemstone grade and metal type.

Brilliant Earth: Known for ethically sourced gemstones and recycled precious metals. They carry elegant halo designs with high craftsmanship standards.

Department Stores & Traditional Jewelers

Kohl’s & Macy’s: Great mid-tier options if you are looking for sterling silver or gold-plated aquamarine halo studs that are more budget-friendly. They frequently run sales and promotions.

Kay Jewelers or Zales: Major mall jewelers that carry a wide selection of gemstone fashion earrings, including classic halo styles featuring aquamarine paired with diamonds.

Artisan & Handcrafted Options

Etsy: Perfect if you want something unique, vintage-inspired, or handmade. You can find independent jewelry designers who offer custom metal options (such as rose gold) or unique gemstone cuts.
