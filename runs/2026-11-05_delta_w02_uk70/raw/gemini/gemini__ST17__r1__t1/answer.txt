You can find a wide variety of emerald earrings online depending on your budget, style, and whether you are looking for natural or lab-created stones. Here are some popular and trusted online retailers to check out:

Fine Jewelry Retailers (Natural Emeralds):

Blue Nile and James Allen: Great for classic, high-end fine jewelry where you can customize the metal type (gold or platinum) and stone quality.

Brilliant Earth: Known for ethically sourced gemstones and conflict-free fine jewelry, offering various modern and vintage-inspired emerald designs.

Angara: Offers a massive selection of emerald earrings ranging from simple studs to elaborate halo drops, often with options to choose the gemstone quality grade.

Accessible & Trendy Jewelry:

Mejuri: Excellent if you are looking for minimalist, everyday pieces in solid gold or gold vermeil.

Kendra Scott: Known for trendy, fashion-forward designs featuring colored stones at accessible price points.

Catbird: Features delicate, beautifully crafted fine jewelry with a whimsical, indie aesthetic.

Department Stores:

Nordstrom and Macy's: Both carry a broad selection of fine jewelry brands, ranging from affordable sterling silver options to luxury gold pieces.
