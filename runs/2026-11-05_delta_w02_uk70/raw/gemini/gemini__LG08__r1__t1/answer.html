Many prominent online jewelers offer both natural and lab-grown options—primarily for diamonds, and frequently for major colored gemstones as well.

The top online jewelry retailers that carry both categories include:

1. Brilliant Earth

What they offer: A massive selection of both natural earth-mined diamonds and lab-grown diamonds, alongside a variety of fine gemstone jewelry (such as sapphires, emeralds, and rubies).

Key highlight: Known for an emphasis on ethical and responsibly sourced materials, allowing you to filter your search specifically by stone origin.

2. Blue Nile

What they offer: One of the largest online retailers for custom fine jewelry, featuring extensive, side-by-side inventories of both natural and lab-grown diamonds as well as colored gemstone rings, earrings, and necklaces.

Key highlight: Offers robust educational tools, transparent grading reports, and a price-match guarantee.

3. Angara

What they offer: Highly specialized in customizable gemstone jewelry. Angara allows shoppers to pick their preferred jewelry setting and choose between natural/earth-mined gemstones (like rubies, sapphires, emeralds, tanzanite) and lab-created alternatives or lab-grown diamonds.

Key highlight: Exceptional customization options where you can often select gemstone quality grades, carat sizes, and metal types.

4. James Allen

What they offer: Famous for their high-definition 360-degree video technology for loose stones, James Allen provides a vast inventory categorized cleanly into natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds, and a selection of colored gemstones.

Key highlight: Great interactive ring-builder platform that lets you mix and match settings with either natural or lab-created center stones.

Tips for Shopping Both Options Online:

Check the Terminology: Sites will typically label stones as "Natural / Earth-Mined" versus "Lab-Grown / Lab-Created". Be sure to double-check product specifications, as lab-grown stones are chemically identical to natural ones but usually retail at a significantly lower price point.

Colored Gemstones vs. Diamonds: While finding lab-grown diamonds is universally standard across these sites, if you are looking for specific colored gemstones (like lab-created alexandrite or sapphires alongside natural ones), specialty custom jewelers like Angara or brands like Chatham provide the widest direct selection.
