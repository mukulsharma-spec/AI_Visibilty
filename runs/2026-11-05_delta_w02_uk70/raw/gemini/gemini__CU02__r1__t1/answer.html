Finding a store that offers free engraving on a gemstone ring depends on whether you are looking for online custom jewellers or high street options. While many jewelers offer complimentary engraving for simple metal wedding bands or engagement rings, rings set with gemstones require extra care because the stones can limit how much space is available on the band (such as the inner shank).

Several stores and brands provide free or complimentary engraving services that can apply to custom or fine rings:

Orla James: Specialises in custom and wedding rings, offering a free engraving service across their product lines with options to engrave the inside of the band.

MSBLUE Jewelry: Offers free custom engraving on a wide range of fine jewelry, including engagement and gemstone rings, using laser or hand engraving methods across various fonts.

Holts Gems: Provides free engraving options online for specific ring categories like engagement rings and wedding bands when conditions or subscriptions apply.

H.Samuel: Offers complimentary engraving on selected personalised and engravable collections using laser or diamond-tip technology, though you will want to check if the specific gemstone ring style you choose qualifies.

Independent High Street Jewellers: If you already own the ring or are buying locally, many independent local jewellers will throw in free engraving as a courtesy if you purchase the piece through them or pay for sizing.

A few tips before you buy:

Check the Band Width: Gemstone rings often feature delicate or twisted bands. Ensure the metal band has enough flat surface area on the inner shank for text.

Return Policies: Be aware that once a ring is custom-engraved, many stores consider it final sale and won't accept returns or exchanges. It is usually best to confirm your ring size first.
