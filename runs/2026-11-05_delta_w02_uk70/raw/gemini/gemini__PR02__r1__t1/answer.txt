A budget of up to £750 gives you plenty of great options for real gemstone earrings in the UK, ranging from contemporary designer demi-fine silver/gold-vermeil pieces to solid 9ct or 18ct gold fine jewelry featuring precious gems (like sapphires, rubies, and emeralds).

Where you should shop depends on the style of metal and stones you are looking for:

1. Fine Jewelers & Multibrand Retailers (Solid Gold & Precious Stones)

If you want real precious gemstones (sapphire, ruby, emerald) set in solid 9ct or 18ct gold, your budget sits comfortably in the mid-range tier:

The Jewel Hut: Excellent for traditional fine jewelry brands. You can frequently find solid 9ct white or yellow gold earrings featuring genuine oval sapphires, rubies, or tanzanites paired with small diamonds well within a £150 to £600 range.

EC One (London-based contemporary fine jewelry): Offers incredible contemporary designer pieces, drop earrings, and unique studs featuring rubies, emeralds, tourmalines, and tanzanites crafted by independent designers, with many staple pieces falling under £750.

2. Independent British Designer Brands (Artisan & Semi-Precious)

If you prefer unique artistic designs, raw/uncut stones, or heavy gold vermeil/sterling silver work:

Ottoman Hands: Known for 21ct gold-plated recycled brass and sterling silver statement gemstone jewelry, featuring genuine semi-precious stones like emerald, turquoise, lapis lazuli, and chalcedony. Most pairs range from £50 to £150.

Carrie Elizabeth Jewellery: Great for celestial and vintage-inspired pieces, offering genuine gemstone drops and stackable styles usually priced between £50 and £200.

Gem Bazaar & Earth and Elements: Perfect if you love vibrant, natural, ethical gemstone drops (like moonstone, amethyst, topaz, and tourmalines) set in sterling silver or gold vermeil, mostly priced accessibly under £100–£300.

Tips Before You Buy:

Check the Metal Specification: At £750, you can easily secure solid gold (9ct or 18ct), but many fashion-forward brands use gold vermeil (thick gold plating over sterling silver). Always double-check the product description to ensure you are getting the metal type you prefer.

Treatment & Enhancement: Many natural gemstones (like rubies, sapphires, and emeralds) undergo standard heat treatments or minor clarity enhancements to bring out their best color. High-end independent retailers will typically note these details in their product specifications.
