Several reputable online jewelry brands and specialized gemstone creators offer lab-grown (or created) sapphire jewelry, ranging from everyday pieces to fine engagement rings.

Popular Online Retailers for Lab-Grown Sapphires

Chatham: Known as a pioneer in lab-grown gemstones, Chatham offers high-quality lab-grown sapphires (available in various shades of blue as well as other colors). While they primarily wholesale loose stones, their site features a store locator and concierge service to help connect you with authorized fine jewelry retailers.

Mejuri: Great for modern, minimalist everyday wear, Mejuri features a collection of lab-grown gemstone jewelry—including earrings, rings, and bracelets—crafted with sustainable lab-grown sapphires (both blue and white) set in sterling silver or gold vermeil.

Lily Arkwright: If you are based in the UK or looking internationally, they specialize in ethical fine jewelry, including a gorgeous collection of lab-grown blue sapphire engagement rings and fine pieces set in gold and platinum.

Created Brilliance: Another great option (particularly for UK shoppers) offering a wide range of created sapphire and lab-grown diamond hybrid pieces, from classic Toi et Moi rings to elegant necklaces and earrings.

Custom Jewelers (e.g., Krikawa): If you are looking for a bespoke or fully custom engagement ring or specialized setting using a lab-grown sapphire center stone, custom design houses offer tailored online consultation services.
