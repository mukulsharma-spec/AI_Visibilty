You can find a three-stone (trilogy) sapphire and diamond ring at a variety of high street jewellers, specialist fine jewellery designers, and online retailers. Many stores allow you to customise your choice by selecting the metal type (such as platinum, 18ct white, or yellow gold) and the cut of the sapphire and diamonds.

Where to look depends on your budget and whether you prefer shopping in-store or online:

High Street & Specialist Jewellers

Beaverbrooks: Offers a range of traditional 18ct gold and platinum sapphire and diamond three-stone rings both online and in stores across the UK.

Pravins: Features high-end bespoke and ready-to-wear platinum and gold trilogy rings featuring sapphires and diamonds.

Chisholm Hunter / Hancocks Jewellers: Excellent options if you are looking for luxury, certified gemstones, or vintage and antique estate pieces.

Custom & Made-to-Order Online Retailers

Austen & Blake: Specialises in customisable made-to-order jewellery where you can choose a three-stone setting and pick a sapphire as a centre or side stone paired with diamonds.

Independent Bespoke Jewellers: Local jewellers in major jewellery quarters (such as Hatton Garden in London or the Jewellery Quarter in Birmingham) can source loose sapphires and diamonds to custom-build a ring to your exact specifications.
