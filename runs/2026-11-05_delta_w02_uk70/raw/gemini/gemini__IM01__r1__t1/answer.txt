What to Look For When Shopping

If you are browsing or shopping for this style of ring, here are a few key details to keep in mind:

The Sapphire: Look for a rich, velvety royal blue. Sapphires are exceptionally durable (9 on the Mohs scale, just below diamonds), making them ideal for everyday wear.

The Halo & Setting: White gold (often 14K or 18K) provides a bright, cool-toned metal that complements both the deep blue of the sapphire and the sparkling white of the diamond halo. A halo of brilliant-cut diamonds will make the center stone look larger and add extra brilliance.

Claw vs. Prong Settings: Oval stones look stunning with either delicate double prongs or secure 4-prong settings that highlight the curved shape of the gemstone.
