The January birthstone is garnet, which is typically known for its deep, rich red color (though it can also be found in other shades like green or orange).

You can find January birthstone jewelry at a wide variety of jewelry stores, both online and in physical shopping centers:

Major Fine Jewelry Retailers (Online & In-Store)

Reeds Jewelers: Offers dedicated birthstone collections featuring garnet rings, necklaces, and earrings with options for in-store pickup.

Blue Nile: Great for high-end gemstone jewelry, offering customizable pieces and fine settings featuring garnet.

Brilliant Earth: Specializes in ethically sourced fine jewelry, featuring a dedicated selection of garnet rings, necklaces, earrings, and bracelets.

Local Mall Jewelers: Major nationwide chains (such as Kay Jewelers, Zales, or Jared) typically carry extensive lines of birthstone jewelry, including garnet rings and pendants.

Specialized & Independent Options

Jewelry by Johan: Known for unique and custom designs, including alternative materials like meteorite or wood paired with natural garnet birthstones.

Etsy: Perfect if you are looking for handmade, vintage, or personalized garnet pieces from independent artisan jewelers.

Local Independent Jewelers: Visiting a local family-owned jeweler in your area is a great way to view garnet pieces firsthand or have a custom birthstone piece made.
