Finding the right place to buy ruby and diamond halo stud earrings depends on whether you are looking for trusted online fine jewelry specialists, custom jewelers, or high-end high street retailers. Because rubies vary heavily in color, clarity, and treatment, choosing a reputable vendor with clear certification and return policies is essential.

Here are some of the best places and types of retailers to look into:

1. Specialized Online Fine Jewelers

Online retailers often offer a wider selection of gemstone options, customizable metal types (white gold, yellow gold, rose gold, or platinum), and competitive pricing compared to traditional brick-and-mortar stores.

The Diamond Store: Known for a vast collection of precious gemstone and diamond jewelry with clear guarantees and customer support.

Diamonds Factory: Offers customizable gemstone earrings where you can often select your preferred metal purity and gemstone specifications.

Klenota: A jeweler specializing in fine gold and gemstone pieces, known for classic halo designs that highlight the rich red color of natural rubies.

2. Independent Fine Jewelry Boutiques & Custom Jewelers

If you want a specific shade of ruby (such as deep "pigeon’s blood" red) or want to ensure superior diamond cut quality in the halo, local or specialized independent fine jewelers (such as Soho Gem or regional family-owned jewelers) are a great choice. They allow you to inspect the stones in person and often provide better craftsmanship warranties, appraisals, and bespoke options.

What to Look For When Buying:

Ruby Quality & Treatment: Most natural rubies undergo heat treatment to enhance color and clarity, which is standard industry practice. Always check if the retailer discloses treatments.

Metal Choice: Ruby’s deep red contrast looks striking against yellow gold (for a vintage, warm aesthetic) or white gold/platinum (which makes the surrounding diamonds pop and accentuates a crisp halo).

Setting Security: Halo studs feature many small accent stones (pave or micro-pave). Ensure the retailer offers a warranty or secure claw/prong settings so you don't lose the tiny surrounding diamonds.
