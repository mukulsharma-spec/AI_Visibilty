Finding a great gemstone tennis bracelet depends on whether you are looking for genuine precious gemstones (like sapphires, rubies, or emeralds set in fine gold or platinum) or fashion-forward crystal pieces.

Several reputable options stand out across different styles and price points:

1. Fine Jewelry & Custom Jewelers (Precious Gemstones)

If you are looking for authentic, high-quality colored gemstones set in solid gold or platinum:

Brilliant Earth: Known for ethical sourcing, they offer customizable fine jewelry options where you can find or build tennis bracelets featuring alternating diamonds and vibrant precious gemstones (like emeralds, sapphires, and rubies).

Allurez Jewelers: They specialize in fine gemstone jewelry and carry a wide selection of tennis and bangle bracelets featuring various colored stones and metal choices.

Local Fine Jewelers / Hatton Garden (UK): If you prefer to see pieces in person, visiting established jewelry districts (such as Hatton Garden in London) or reputable local independent jewelers allows you to inspect stone clarity, color matching, and clasp security firsthand.

2. Department Stores & Established High Street Brands

Ernest Jones: A trusted high street jeweler offering a variety of tennis bracelets, ranging from traditional diamond styles to vibrant colored gemstone and precious metal designs.

Tiffany & Co.: If you are looking for luxury designer craftsmanship, they offer timeless, exceptionally crafted delicate bracelet designs.

3. Contemporary & Fashion Crystal Options

Swarovski: If you want a brilliant look at an accessible fashion-jewelry price point, their Matrix and Imber lines feature precision-cut colored crystals set in gold-tone, rose gold-tone, or rhodium plating.

What to Look For Before Buying:

Setting Security: Because tennis bracelets are flexible and worn around the wrist, make sure the stones are held securely (such as prong or bezel settings) and that the clasp features a safety latch so it doesn't accidentally slip off.

Stone Matching: For gemstone tennis bracelets, consistency in color saturation and hue across all the stones is the hallmark of a well-made piece.
