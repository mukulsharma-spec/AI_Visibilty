With a budget of up to £2,000, you have plenty of great options for a tanzanite ring, whether you are looking for a fine 9ct or 18ct gold setting, a vintage-style piece, or a vibrant cluster/halo design.

Several reputable UK jewellers and online specialists offer strong selections within this price range:

1. Dedicated Online Fine Jewellers

The Diamond Store: They feature an extensive online collection of natural tanzanite and diamond rings (often paired with white gold or yellow gold) ranging heavily from £200 up to £1,500+, giving you room to find a good-sized stone with accent diamonds well under your £2,000 cap.

Gemporia / TJC (The Jewellery Channel): Excellent if you want to stretch your budget or look for specific gemstone grading (like AAA or AAAA tanzanite grades). They frequently offer unique gold and silver settings featuring substantial tanzanite carat weights well below £1,000 to £1,500.

2. High Street & Independent Jewellers

F. Hinds: A trusted UK high street name with a dedicated selection of tanzanite and diamond rings. Most of their pieces (typically set in 9ct white or yellow gold) sit comfortably between £300 and £1,000, making it easy to view pieces in person if you prefer shopping on the high street.

Independent Antique & Vintage Specialists (e.g., Philip Lloyd Jewellers or local vintage shops): If you love unique character, you can often find pre-owned Art Deco or Edwardian-style platinum and tanzanite rings right around the £1,200 to £1,950 mark.

Quick Tips for Buying Tanzanite:

Check the Setting: Tanzanite is a slightly softer gemstone (around 6 to 7 on the Mohs scale) compared to diamonds or sapphires. For everyday wear, look for settings like bezels or secure claws that protect the corners of the stone.

Colour & Clarity: Tanzanite is prized for its rich violet-blue hue. Deep, saturated stones usually command higher prices, but £2,000 is a very healthy budget to secure a vibrant, eye-clean centre stone.
