Several online jewelers and fine jewelry retailers feature advanced virtual try-on technology that lets you preview gemstone and diamond rings. Many of these platforms allow you to customize center stones (such as sapphires, emeralds, rubies, and colored or lab-grown diamonds) and metal types to see how they look before buying.

Popular Online Jewelers with Virtual Ring Try-On

Brilliant Earth: Known for extensive ethical and colored gemstone options (including sapphires, emeralds, and aquamarines). Their virtual try-on tool lets you upload a photo of your hand, position and resize the ring, adjust carat weights or stone shapes, and even preview ring stacks.

Abelini: Offers an interactive, web-based virtual try-on tool powered by AR and AI. You can either snap a live photo of your hand or use an adjustable pre-set model hand to test out different gemstone ring designs, switch fingers, and zoom in.

Kavalri: Features a virtual reality try-on tool across their ring collections. It allows you to visualize various center stone shapes, sizes, and custom designs either by using a model hand or uploading a photo via your phone.

Zales: Offers a virtual try-on feature on a wide variety of custom, natural, and lab-grown gemstone and diamond pieces. Their tool allows you to see how different styles look on a hand model and test out matching wedding band stacks.

Shane Co.: Features a user-friendly try-on experience where you can upload a photo of your hand to preview different stone shapes, colors, carat sizes, and metal types.
