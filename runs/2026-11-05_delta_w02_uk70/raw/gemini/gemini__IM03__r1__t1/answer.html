Popular Design Options

Solitaire Studs: Available in traditional round, oval, or pear shapes. They can be mounted in simple 3-prong or 4-prong basket settings to maximize light exposure through the stone.

Halo Settings: Features a central ruby surrounded by a sparkling ring of small accent diamonds, which enhances the visual size and brilliance of the center stone.

Bezel or Rubover Settings: Encases the gemstone securely in a thin rim of yellow gold, offering a sleek, modern, and snag-free profile.

Key Considerations When Shopping

Metal Purity: You will typically find these studs in 10K, 14K, or 18K yellow gold. Higher karat weights (like 18K) feature a richer, deeper yellow hue, while 10K or 14K offer greater durability for everyday wear.

Stone Origin: Options often span natural mined rubies (valued for unique inclusions and classic heritage) as well as lab-created or lab-grown rubies, which provide exceptional clarity and vibrant color at a more accessible price point.

Birthstone Significance: Rubies are the traditional birthstone for July, making them a popular choice for personalized gifts.
