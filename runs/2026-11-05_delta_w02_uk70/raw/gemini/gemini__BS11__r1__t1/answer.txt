There are several reputable online platforms and fine jewellers where you can buy an opal pendant necklace, ranging from specialized Australian opal exporters to mainstream and independent jewellery stores.

1. Specialized Opal Specialists (Authentic Australian Opals)

If you are looking for genuine, high-quality Australian opals with certification and direct-from-mine sourcing:

Opals Down Under: Offers an extensive range of solid, doublet, and triplet opal pendants set in sterling silver or gold, complete with certificates of authenticity and worldwide shipping.

Australian Opal Direct: Known for gold and silver opal necklaces sourced directly from Australian mines.

Opal Auctions: A massive online marketplace featuring verified individual opal sellers and independent stores offering everything from budget-friendly pieces to investment-grade stones.

Burton's Gems & Opals: Custom-designed opal pendants and necklaces featuring natural Australian opals.

2. UK & International Retailers

If you prefer shopping from well-established regional or online department jewellers that offer reliable delivery and customer service:

TJC (The Jewellery Channel): A budget-friendly option offering a wide variety of silver, gold, and gemstone opal pendant styles and chains.

Ramsdens Jewellery: A great place to look if you are interested in unique, pre-owned, or vintage opal pendant necklaces.

Tips Before Buying Online:

Check the Opal Type: Opals can be solid (a single piece of natural solid opal), doublet (a layer of opal backed by another material), or triplet (opal with a dark backing and a protective crystal cap). Solid opals are generally more valuable and durable, while doublets and triplets offer vibrant color at a more accessible price point.

Look at the Setting: Because opals have some water content and can be softer than diamonds or sapphires (ranking around 5.5–6.5 on the Mohs hardness scale), look for protective settings like bezels rather than exposed prongs if you plan to wear the necklace every day.
