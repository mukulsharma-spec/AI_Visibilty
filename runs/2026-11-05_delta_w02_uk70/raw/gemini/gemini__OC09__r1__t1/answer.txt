Celebrating 55 years of marriage is a wonderful milestone! Because emerald is the traditional gemstone for a 55th anniversary, many jewellers and specialty boutiques offer dedicated collections.

Where you should look depends on whether you are looking for fine jewellery, personalized keepsakes, or unique curated items:

1. Fine Jewellery Specialists (UK & Online)

If you are looking for traditional emerald rings, necklaces, earrings, or bracelets:

Avanti Jewellers: Offers a dedicated 55th Anniversary Emerald collection featuring diamond and emerald cluster rings, eternity rings, and gold earrings.

Catherine Best: Known for bespoke craftsmanship, they feature a specialized line of high-end emerald anniversary rings, bangles, and necklaces.

Golden Snowdrop & Lily Gardner: Both offer curated selections of emerald-themed jewellery specifically chosen to mark 55 years of marriage.

High Street & Local Independent Jewellers: Most high street names (such as Beaverbrooks or Ernest Jones) or local independent jewellers carry custom gemstone pieces or can source emerald settings.

2. Personalised Keepsakes & Crafts

If you are looking for custom-made memory books, framed art, or emerald-themed homeware:

Not On The High Street (NOTHS): An excellent marketplace featuring independent British creators offering personalized 55th anniversary gifts, ceramic keepsakes, and emerald-accented art.

Create Gift Love: Specialises in personalised wooden and leather gifts, offering custom emerald-themed photo blocks and anniversary tokens.

3. Luxury Cards & Accompaniments

The Luxe Co: Specialises in luxury, handmade, and custom-scented 55th emerald anniversary cards and keepsake boxes if you want a premium accompaniment to your main gift.
