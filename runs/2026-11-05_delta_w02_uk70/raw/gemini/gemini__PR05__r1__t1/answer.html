Tips for Buying a Real Ruby:

Check the Treatment Disclosure: Most natural rubies undergo standard heat treatment to enhance color and clarity—this is standard industry practice. Ensure the retailer explicitly states the stone is a natural ruby rather than synthetic/lab-created (unless you specifically want a lab-grown stone, which will cost significantly less).

Gold Purity: At £1,500, look closely at whether the piece is crafted in 9ct or 18ct gold. 18ct gold has a richer gold content, while 9ct is more durable for everyday wear and often allows a larger or higher-quality ruby center stone within a strict budget.
