Several reputable online jewelry stores and direct-to-consumer brands offer a combination of a lifetime warranty (typically covering manufacturing defects) and ring resizing options.

Popular online retailers that feature these services include:

Blue Nile: One of the largest online fine jewelry and engagement ring retailers. They offer a limited lifetime warranty protecting against manufacturing defects, alongside complimentary ring resizing on eligible styles (though certain alternative metals or eternity bands are excluded due to design limitations).

Grown Brilliance: Specializing in lab-grown diamond jewelry, they back their craftsmanship with a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects and provide robust customer service support for alterations and resizing.

Brilliance: They provide a lifetime warranty on items alongside a free ring resizing window (typically within an initial timeframe like 90 days post-shipment, depending on the specific product line and design).

Frank Darling: Known for custom and semi-custom engagement rings, they offer a lifetime warranty covering structural defects and provide support services for adjustments.

Important Caveats to Keep in Mind:

Exclusions for Alternative Metals: Rings made from alternative or heavy-duty industrial metals (such as tungsten, titanium, or stainless steel) usually cannot be resized by traditional methods.

Eternity Bands: Rings featuring diamonds or gemstones set all the way around the band (eternity rings) generally cannot be resized without completely remaking the piece, so they are typically excluded from free resizing policies.

Manufacturing Defects vs. Normal Wear: Lifetime warranties almost universally cover structural or manufacturing flaws rather than accidental damage, loss, theft, or regular wear-and-tear (such as scratches or prongs worn down over years of use). Separate jewelry insurance is usually recommended to cover loss or theft.
