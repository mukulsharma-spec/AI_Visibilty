With a budget of up to £1,000, you actually have a wonderful amount of flexibility to get real, natural sapphires (or lab-created sapphires, which are physically and chemically identical real gemstones) set in solid precious metals like 9ct or 18ct gold.

When looking for "real" earrings, the key is ensuring you check two things: the gemstone isn't cheap glass or plastic (look for natural or lab-created/synthetic sapphire) and the setting is solid gold or sterling silver rather than plated mystery metal.

2. What "Real" Means for Sapphires

When shopping under £1,000, you will generally encounter two types of real sapphires:

Natural Mined Sapphires: Formed in the earth over millions of years. At lower price points, they may be smaller carat weights, treated/heated to enhance colour, or have natural inclusions, but they are 100% genuine stones.

Lab-Created (Synthetic) Sapphires: Grown in a laboratory. Chemically, physically, and optically, they are real sapphires (same crystal structure and hardness), but they cost a fraction of the price and are usually flawless. If a brand offers vibrant, large stones in solid 18ct gold for a few hundred pounds, they are likely lab-created.
