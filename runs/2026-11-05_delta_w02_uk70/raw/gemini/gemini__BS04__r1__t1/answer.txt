Finding a pear-shaped emerald engagement ring offers a stunning blend of vintage charm and modern elegance. Because emeralds are a softer gemstone than diamonds (ranking 7.5 to 8 on the Mohs scale), buying from a reputable jeweler that specializes in custom settings or high-end colored gemstones is essential to ensure proper protection (like a V-prong at the pear's point) and clarity.

Tips for Buying a Pear-Shaped Emerald:

Secure the Tip: Pear cuts taper to a sharp point, which is vulnerable to chipping. Always verify that the setting features a protective V-prong over the point of the pear.

Inquire About Treatments: Most emeralds undergo minor oiling or clarity enhancements to fill surface-fractions—this is an industry standard, but always ask for certification details regarding treatment levels.

Natural vs. Lab-Grown: Decide ahead of time whether you prefer a 100% natural Earth-mined stone (which often features unique internal inclusions called jardin) or a lab-grown emerald, which typically offers higher clarity at a more accessible price point.
