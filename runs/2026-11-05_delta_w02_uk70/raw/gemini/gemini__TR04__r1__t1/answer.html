Several reputable online jewelers offer 30-day return policies on fine jewelry, including gemstone rings. Here are some options:

Brilliant Earth: Known for ethically sourced fine jewelry, they offer a free 30-day return policy on standard ring orders for a full refund or exchange.

77 Diamonds: Providing bespoke rings and fine jewelry, they feature an easy returns policy allowing customers to return items within 30 days.

Austen & Blake: Specialising in custom diamond and gemstone jewellery, they offer secure delivery alongside free returns.

Flawless Fine Jewellery: Based in London's Hatton Garden, they provide a 30-day return policy for online and non-bespoke orders.

Note: Custom-engraved items, custom-designed bespoke pieces, or items resized outside of standard parameters are often excluded from standard free return windows, so it is always a good idea to check the specific retailer's terms before purchasing.
