Finding amethyst jewelry for a February birthday is a wonderful idea, as the rich purple stone makes for a striking and meaningful gift. Depending on your budget and style preference, you have some great options available across well-known UK high street jewellers, independent designers, and online specialists:

High Street & Established Jewellers

Beaverbrooks: They carry a wide-ranging collection of amethyst rings, pendants, and earrings set in sterling silver as well as 9ct white and yellow gold, often paired with diamonds.

Laings: If you are looking for fine, luxury jewellery, Laings offers exquisite handcrafted amethyst necklaces, bracelets, and rings set in 18ct gold.

C W Sellors: A fantastic option for British-crafted fine jewellery featuring vibrant coloured gemstones, including a diverse selection of amethyst pieces.

Independent & Contemporary Brands

Auree Jewellery: Known for elegant, wearable everyday pieces, Auree offers handcrafted amethyst birthstone necklaces, rings, and earrings in both gold and silver.

Augustine Jewels: Based in London, they feature striking luxury collections (such as cluster rings and drop earrings) that combine amethyst with gold, diamonds, or other complementary stones.

Pippa Small or Kiki McDonough: Excellent choices if you are looking for high-end designer pieces with unique, artistic settings.

Budget-Friendly & Customisable Options

TJC (The Jeweller's Channel): A great destination if you are looking for a wide variety of affordable silver and gold amethyst rings or tennis bracelets.

GLDN: Perfect if she prefers delicate, minimalist everyday birthstone pieces or personalized touches.
