Choosing a ring
- Full vs. half eternity: A full eternity can't easily be resized. A half or three-quarter eternity is more forgiving if your finger size changes.
- Metal: Platinum and 18ct gold are the most durable for a ring worn every day. 9ct is cheaper but a bit less refined.
- Setting: Claw settings show more of the stones, while channel settings protect them better.
- Sapphire quality: Ask whether the sapphires are natural and whether they're heat-treated. Heat treatment is common and normally fine, but it should be disclosed. For pricier pieces, look for a certificate or hallmark.
- Returns: Check the policy, especially for made-to-order rings, which are often non-returnable.
