A few things help when choosing at this budget:

Stone size: Under £500, sapphires, rubies, emeralds and diamonds are usually small accent stones. Pendants and earrings tend to give the most visible sparkle for the money. Rings and bracelets are better if the colour or design matters more than size.

Gold purity: 9ct is harder-wearing and cheaper, while 18ct is richer in colour and more valuable. At the same price you generally get a larger or more elaborate piece in 9ct.

Treatments and origin: Many sapphires, rubies and emeralds are heat- or oil-treated. This is normal and should be disclosed. Some listings also use dyed stones, such as dyed jade, so read the description. Lab-grown diamonds are also common in this range, so check whether a stone is natural or lab-grown.

Birthstones: A birthstone piece is a safe, personal choice. For example, citrine is November’s and sapphire is September’s.
