Choosing one
- Antique vs new: Antique rings often give you a larger, finer stone for the money and can be resized, but check the condition of the claws and shank. New rings come with a warranty and can be customised.
- Sapphire quality: Most sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal. Untreated stones cost more, so ask for a lab report (GIA, SSEF or Gübelin) on anything expensive.
- Setting: With a three-stone ring, check that the side diamonds are well matched with each other. The metal matters too: platinum is more durable, while 18ct gold is warmer in tone.
