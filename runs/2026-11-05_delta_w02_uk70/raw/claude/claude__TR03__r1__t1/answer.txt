Before you buy, check a few things:

Free resizing is usually time-limited (30 to 90 days, or one year) or limited to one resize.
Warranties often cover maintenance and defects, not accidental damage or loss, and many are non-transferable.
Exclusions apply. For example, Unicoring excludes solid gold, platinum, custom, and engraved pieces from free resizing, and says third-party repairs void its warranty.
Some stores want original documentation or lab reports kept for service.
