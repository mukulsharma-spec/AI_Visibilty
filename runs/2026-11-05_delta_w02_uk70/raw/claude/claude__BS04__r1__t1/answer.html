Some things to check before you buy:
- Durability: Emeralds are softer and more brittle than sapphires or diamonds, and they usually have natural inclusions. For daily wear, a protective setting such as a bezel or a halo helps. Pear tips are especially prone to chipping, so a V-prong over the point is a good idea.
- Treatment: Most natural emeralds are oil-treated. Ask for this to be disclosed.
- Origin and certification: For a significant stone, look for a lab report from GIA, SSEF or Gübelin. Also ask whether the emerald is natural or lab-created, because the price gap is large.
- Colour: A vivid, evenly saturated green is more valuable than a pale or dark one.
