What to check before buying

  • Make sure it’s a real lab-created emerald, not a simulant. Lab-created emeralds have the same chemistry as natural ones. Green glass or CZ doesn’t, and some listings blur the two.
  • Ask about the growth method. Flux-grown and hydrothermal stones differ slightly in look and inclusions, and some are more durable than others.
  • Look for a certificate or clear disclosure. Reputable sellers will state the stone is lab-created, its size, and its treatment (oil or resin filling is common even in lab stones).
  • Consider the setting. Emeralds are softer than diamonds and chip more easily, so a bezel or protective setting suits everyday wear better than a prong-heavy one.
  • Check the return policy. Colour varies a lot, and it’s worth seeing the stone in person or being able to return it.
