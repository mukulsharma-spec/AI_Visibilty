Several retailers let you pick the quality grade of the gemstone yourself.

Angara is probably the closest match to what you describe. Their gemstone rings are built around quality tiers, so you pick the stone type, then choose a grade (they use labels like AA, AAA, and AAAA, with higher grades meaning better color, clarity, and cut), then the metal and setting. The price updates as you change the grade, so you can see what each step up costs. They ship internationally, though I’d check UK delivery, duties, and returns at checkout.

Other options:

- Blue Nile and James Allen let you search loose stones by specs and then set them in a ring. They’re strongest for diamonds, with a more limited range of colored stones.

- Brilliant Earth offers colored gemstones, including sapphires, in customizable settings.

- Independent UK jewellers are an option if you’d like to see stones in person. Hatton Garden in London has many dealers who can source a specific stone and quality and set it into a ring you design.

Tips for choosing a grade:

- For colored gemstones, color matters most, followed by clarity and then cut. Mid-tier grades often look nearly identical to top grades at a much lower price.

- Gem type affects what’s worth paying for. Emeralds almost always have visible inclusions, so don’t expect perfect clarity, while aquamarine and topaz are typically very clean.

- Ask whether the stone is natural or lab-created, and whether it’s been heated or treated. This affects value more than people expect.

- For expensive stones, ask for an independent lab certificate (GIA, for example).
