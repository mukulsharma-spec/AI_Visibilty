What to check before buying

Treatment: Almost all sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal and stable. Beryllium or diffusion treatment is a different matter and should be disclosed, and it’s worth asking about on antique or pre-owned pieces.

Natural or lab-grown: Lab-grown sapphires are chemically identical and noticeably cheaper per carat, so they can get you a bigger, richer stone within £2,000. Make sure the listing says which one you’re buying.

Colour over size: A vivid, medium-dark blue looks better than a larger stone that’s pale or very dark. Compare the two stones in a pair, since mismatched tone is common at this price.

Metal: 9ct is the most hard-wearing for everyday use. 18ct has a richer colour but is softer.

Backs and settings: Screw-backs or secure friction backs are worth having on earrings you’ll wear daily.
