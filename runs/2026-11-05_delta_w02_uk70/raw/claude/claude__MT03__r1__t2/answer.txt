I'm not sure which site you mean, so here's a quick take on the four I named. Blue Nile, James Allen, Brilliant Earth and Angara are all long-established retailers with real return policies and gemstone documentation, so they're generally safe choices. Blue Nile and James Allen are now owned by Signet, the parent of Kay, Zales and H. Samuel.

Whichever one you're considering, a few checks apply to any jewellery site:

Return window: 30 days or more, with the terms in writing, and ideally free returns.
Treatment disclosure: it should state whether the ruby is heated or untreated on the listing itself.
Lab reports: pricier stones should come with a GIA or similar certificate, not just the seller's own grading.
Independent reviews: check Trustpilot and Reddit's r/jewelry or r/EngagementRings rather than only the testimonials on the retailer's own site.
Payment: pay by credit card, which gives you extra protection under Section 75 in the UK, and avoid sites that push bank transfers.
Too-good-to-be-true pricing: a large, vivid ruby at a few hundred pounds is almost certainly glass-filled or synthetic.
