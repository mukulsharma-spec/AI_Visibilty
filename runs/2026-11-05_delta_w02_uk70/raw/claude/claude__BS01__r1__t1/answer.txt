Tips for choosing

Natural, lab-grown, and “created” sapphires differ a lot in price, so check which one a listing offers.
Look for the treatment disclosure. Most sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal, but one listing I found mentioned irradiated stones, which is less common.
Check the return window and whether the earrings have screw backs or push backs. Screw backs are more secure for pricier pairs.
Compare carat weight per earring against total carat weight, since listings often quote the pair total.
