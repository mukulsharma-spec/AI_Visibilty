Things to check before buying
Durability: Tanzanite is softer than sapphire or diamond and can chip or scratch. If you’ll wear it daily, choose a bezel or a halo setting that protects the edges.
Treatment and certification: Almost all tanzanite is heat-treated to get its blue-violet colour, which is normal. Ask the seller to confirm it’s natural, and whether it comes with a gemstone report for larger or pricier stones.
Colour over carat: A deeper, more saturated blue-violet is worth more than extra size. Look at the stone in person or in video if you can.
Metal and hallmark: Solid gold (9ct, 14ct or 18ct) should be hallmarked. Silver is much cheaper but wears differently.
Returns: Check the return window and whether resizing is free.
