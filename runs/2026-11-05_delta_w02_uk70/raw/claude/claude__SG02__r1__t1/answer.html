A few good routes, depending on what you want:

Online retailers with men’s sapphire bands

Brilliant Earth has a men’s collection with sapphire-set bands and a focus on ethically sourced stones.
Angara specializes in colored gemstones, and has sapphire bands in a range of metals and stone cuts.
Blue Nile offers a good selection of men’s bands, including some with sapphire accents.
Manly Bands specializes in men’s rings and has sapphire inlay and channel-set options in alternative metals like tungsten, titanium, and cobalt.

Custom or one-of-a-kind

Etsy has independent jewelers who make bespoke sapphire bands, often at lower prices than big brands. Check reviews and return policies carefully.
A local independent jeweler can design a band around a sapphire you choose. In London, Hatton Garden is full of workshops that do this.

High street (UK)

Goldsmiths, Ernest Jones, and Beaverbrooks carry some men’s gemstone bands, though selection is usually more limited than online.

What to look for

Setting style: Flush or bezel settings suit men’s bands best, since the stone sits low and resists snagging and knocks.
Stone quality: Ask whether the sapphire is natural, lab-grown, or heat-treated. Heat treatment is common and fine, but it should be disclosed.
Metal: Platinum, palladium, and titanium hold up well to daily wear.
