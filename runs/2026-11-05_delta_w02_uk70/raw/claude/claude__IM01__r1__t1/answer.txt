Here are some oval blue sapphire rings with diamond halos in white gold. They show a few different takes on the style:

Classic single halo: A smaller oval sapphire (around 0.7ct) circled by one row of round diamonds. It’s the most traditional version of the look and suits everyday wear.
Baguette halo: The sapphire is surrounded by baguette-cut diamonds instead of round ones. This gives a more structured, cocktail-ring look.
Double halo: A larger stone (around 2.5ct) with two rings of diamonds. It has the most sparkle and makes the center stone look bigger.
Larger center stone (2ct+): A bolder single-halo option where the sapphire is the focus.

White gold is a good match for blue sapphire. The cool metal keeps the stone’s color looking clean, and it blends with the diamonds so the halo looks brighter.

Things to look at when comparing rings:

Sapphire color: Look for a medium-to-vivid royal or cornflower blue. Stones that are too dark can look inky, and very pale ones can look washed out.
Setting height: A low-profile setting is more practical if you want to wear the ring every day.
Halo width: Thin halos look delicate and a wider halo makes more of a statement.
