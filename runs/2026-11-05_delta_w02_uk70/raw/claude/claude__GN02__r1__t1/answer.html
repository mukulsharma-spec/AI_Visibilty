Deciding whether to search or answer directly

The best place depends on what you’re after.

Diamonds and engagement or wedding jewelry: Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, and James Allen let you filter by cut, color, and clarity, and they offer lab-grown options too. Their certification and return policies are generally solid.

Colored gemstones: Angara specializes in sapphires, emeralds, rubies, and other colored stones, with lots of customization.

Everyday fine jewelry (14k and 18k gold): Mejuri, Catbird, and Monica Vinader are popular for delicate, wearable pieces at more approachable prices. Some of these lean “demi-fine,” so check whether a piece is solid gold or gold-plated.

Luxury houses: Tiffany & Co., Cartier, Bulgari, and Van Cleef & Arpels sell directly online. Net-a-Porter, Mytheresa, and Farfetch carry curated designer fine jewelry from many brands.

Vintage and antique: 1stDibs, Ruby Lane, and Chairish are good for estate and one-of-a-kind pieces, but read seller reviews and return terms closely.

Whichever you choose, look for independent certification on diamonds and gemstones (GIA, IGI, or similar), clear metal purity markings, a generous return window (30 days or more), and insured shipping. Be wary of prices that seem too good to be true.
