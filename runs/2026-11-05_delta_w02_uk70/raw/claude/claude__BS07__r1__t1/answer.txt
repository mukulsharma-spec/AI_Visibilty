Before you buy
- Durability: Tanzanite is softer than sapphire and fairly brittle, which makes it more prone to scratches and chips. A halo or bezel setting protects it better than an open solitaire. One retailer also advises halo settings for buyers who want both visual impact and durability. It is best suited to someone who will take the ring off for heavy work, and it is not ideal for constant daily wear. [aquamarise]
- Treatment: Almost all tanzanite is heat-treated, which is normal and stable, but it should be disclosed.
- Colour: The most valuable stones are a strong, saturated violet-blue. Pale or grey-toned stones are cheaper.
- Origin: Real tanzanite comes only from Tanzania. Be wary of unusually cheap listings, and ask whether the stone is natural or lab-made.
- Check the stone itself: One buying guide suggests checking that the stone does not have fractured or chipped corners. [theknot]
