Cheapest route: eBay and Etsy
You'll find lab-created sapphire tennis bracelets for roughly $214 to $237, and some even lower. Many of these are only gold-plated, often over sterling silver or base metal. Some listings use cubic zirconia as the accent stone, so read the listing details closely. These are fine as fashion jewelry but won't last like solid gold. [ebay]

Things to check before buying

- Whether it's solid 14k gold or just "gold plated" (the plated ones are the ones that tarnish and wear through).
- Whether the sapphires are natural or lab-created. Both are real sapphire, but lab-created stones cost far less.
- The accent stones: lab diamonds, natural diamonds, or CZ.
- The return policy, since the safety clasp and stone security matter on a bracelet you'll wear daily.

If you'd rather have natural sapphires but at a lower price, a thinner bracelet with smaller stones, or all sapphire with no diamonds, will cut the price more than switching retailers will.
