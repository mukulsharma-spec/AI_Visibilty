Tips

Metal: Peridot’s yellow-green color looks warm in yellow gold and crisp in white gold or silver.
Style: Studs are the safest choice for everyday wear. Drops and halos suit special occasions.
Durability: Peridot is about 6.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale, like tanzanite, so earrings are a better fit than rings.
Returns: Check each retailer’s return policy before buying, since most only accept items in as-new condition.
