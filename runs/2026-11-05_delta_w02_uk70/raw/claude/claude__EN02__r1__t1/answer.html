The “best of” lists that come up for this are mostly written by the retailers themselves, so I’ve leaned on the more neutral ones and my own knowledge. I’ve also assumed you’re in the UK, so tell me if not.

Online-first retailers with good reputations Brilliant Earth, James Allen, Blue Nile, Clean Origin and VRAI appear in a round-up of the best places to buy engagement rings online. Brilliant Earth and VRAI are known for ethical sourcing and lab-grown options. James Allen and Blue Nile are known for large searchable diamond inventories with 360° video. Check that each ships to the UK, and ask about import duty and VAT.

Taylor & Hart is a UK brand that appears in a top 9 list of UK engagement ring retailers. It’s a good place to start if you want a bespoke or customisable design from a UK retailer.

Whiteflash is on a list of designer engagement ring retailers alongside luxury names. It’s best known among diamond enthusiasts for very well-cut stones.

Hybrid options (browse online, buy with an appointment) London’s Hatton Garden is a well-known jewellery district. Many of its workshops also sell online, so you can look at a ring in person and still compare prices. This is a good route if you want to see the stone before committing.

Tips whichever you choose Ask for an independent grading certificate (GIA or IGI for lab-grown, GIA for natural) and check it matches the stone. Check the returns policy, resizing terms and insurance, because engagement rings are rarely a “try it and see” purchase. Compare the same specification (carat, cut, colour, clarity) across two or three retailers. Prices vary a lot for identical stones. Prioritise cut quality over carat size. It affects sparkle more than anything else.
