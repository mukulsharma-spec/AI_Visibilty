Here are the main styles you’ll see in pink sapphire engagement rings:

Solitaire: A single oval, round, or cushion-cut pink sapphire on a plain band. It’s simple and lets the color stand out.
Halo: Diamonds surround the center stone, which adds sparkle and makes the sapphire look larger. Pink sapphire pairs especially well with this style.
Three-stone: A pink sapphire flanked by diamonds or pear-shaped side stones.
Shapes: Oval is the most popular. Pear and heart shapes feel more romantic, and cushion cuts have a softer, vintage look.

Color: Pink sapphires range from pale blush to hot pink and deeper raspberry. Richer, more saturated tones generally cost more, while soft pastels look delicate and are usually more affordable.

Metal: Rose gold gives a warm, monochrome look. White gold or platinum makes the pink appear cooler and brighter. Yellow gold gives a vintage feel.

Durability: Sapphire is a 9 on the Mohs scale, second only to diamond. That makes it a good choice for a ring worn every day, and a tougher one than tanzanite.
