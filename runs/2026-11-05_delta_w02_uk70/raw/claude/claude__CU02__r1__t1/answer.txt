Free engraving usually comes with buying the ring from that retailer, so it’s mostly a perk of purchase rather than a standalone service. Here’s what I found, and some of it may be dated or promotional, so check before you buy.

- John Greed Jewellery says it offers free engraving, and you can order online. [trustpilot]
- Van Bruun states that it offers free engraving on all its jewelry at the time of purchase. [vanbruun]
- Joshua James says its free engraving is carried out in-house and is now offered in-store as well as online. Its store is in Hessle, near Hull, so it’s likely only handy if you’re up north or ordering online. [joshuajames]
- Holts (London, Hatton Garden) advertises free engraving on engagement rings and wedding bands when you subscribe, online only, with terms and conditions. [holtsgems]
- Lunn’s the Jewellers has run free engraving promotions when you purchase a ring. These look seasonal, so ask whether one is running now. [lunns]
- Kaanaanmaa in Wokingham offers a free engraving service of up to 12 characters, if you tell them when ordering. [bridebook]

Astrid & Miyu offers in-store engraving at Notting Hill, Carnaby, Kings Road and Coal Drops Yard as a walk-in service. I couldn’t confirm whether it’s free, so ask first. Others charge, for example £40 per ring at Purely Diamonds. [astridandmiyu] [purelydiamonds]

Some practical points:
- Many of these offers cover only wedding and engagement rings, or only certain ranges, so confirm that a gemstone ring qualifies.
- Engraving usually has to be requested when you order, and personalised pieces are often non-returnable.
- If you already own the ring, free engraving is much rarer. Your best bet there is asking a local independent jeweller, who sometimes does short engravings free or for a small fee.
