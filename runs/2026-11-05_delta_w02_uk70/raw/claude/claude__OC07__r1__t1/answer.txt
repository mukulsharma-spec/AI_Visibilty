Congratulations on 45 years! Sapphire is the traditional gem for this anniversary, so it’s a lovely choice. Where to shop depends on your budget and what kind of piece you have in mind.

Online specialists (best selection and price transparency)


Angara focuses heavily on sapphires and offers lots of customization, with good options at moderate prices.

Brilliant Earth and Blue Nile have large catalogs, solid return policies, and independent certification on many stones.


Independent jewelers and gem dealers

Buying in person lets you judge color in real light, which matters a lot with sapphires. If you’re in or near London, Hatton Garden has many independent dealers who can show you stones and make custom pieces. Look for established shops with good reviews, and don’t be shy about asking questions.

Luxury houses (premium for the name and design)

Tiffany, Cartier, Graff, and in the UK, Garrard, are worth a look if you want a statement piece and your budget allows.

Antique and estate jewelry

Auction houses and reputable estate dealers often have beautiful vintage sapphire pieces, which can be a nice fit for a long marriage. Just make sure the seller provides documentation.

What to check wherever you buy


Certification: a report from GIA, AGL, or SSEF (for high-end stones) confirms what you’re getting.

Treatment: most sapphires are heated, which is normal and accepted, but it should be disclosed. Unheated stones cost significantly more.

Origin: Sri Lanka (Ceylon), Madagascar, Burma, and Kashmir sapphires have different looks and price premiums.

Color: a rich, vivid blue that isn’t too dark or too pale is generally the most prized, but go with what your wife would love.

Return policy: especially important online, so you can see the piece in person before committing.
