A few things to check before you buy:

Sale pricing: The Shining Diamonds pairs are shown with a "from" price roughly double the sale price. That kind of permanent-sale pricing is common, so judge them on the price you'd actually pay.
Natural or lab-grown: The listings I found didn't say, so confirm with the seller. It matters a lot for value.
Stone quality: Ask whether the emeralds are treated (oiling is standard) and for the colour and clarity grade. The Shining Diamonds listings give an "SI2/G" grade, but I wouldn't rely on that alone for coloured stones.

You skipped the style question, so I leaned towards studs. If you'd prefer drops or hoops, or want me to look at something specific like vintage or pre-owned pairs, let me know.
