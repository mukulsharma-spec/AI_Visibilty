Here are the ones I found with a 30-day window, sorted by whether the return shipping is actually free.

Free returns

  • Azeera advertises free returns on its gemstone rings. Its policy is a 30-day return with free FedEx return shipping and a full refund to the original payment method. Items must come back unworn and with all original documentation. Many of its rings are customizable, so check whether your specific piece is excluded. azeeraazeera
  • Gem Noir has a similar policy. It offers free US returns within a 30-day window, with a prepaid, insured return label (one per order). International returns are handled differently, so check those terms if you’re outside the US. gem-noir

30-day window, but not fully free

  • Fire & Brilliance: It allows returns or exchanges within 30 days, but the shipping and handling fee is deducted from returns, and refunds come as 15% store credit. Gemstone full eternity rings and bands are final sale. fireandbrilliancefireandbrilliance
  • Gemstone King: The return window is 30 days, but you pay return shipping, and it isn’t refunded. gemstoneking
  • Day’s Jewelers: Its 30-day policy covers returns in store or by mail, but mailed returns are at your expense. Rings that were sized in-house or special ordered are excluded. daysjewelers

If you’re ordering a custom or resized ring, read the exclusions closely. Most retailers treat made-to-order and resized pieces as final sale, even when the headline policy says “free returns.”
