Here are the ones I found with a 30-day window, sorted by whether the return shipping is actually free.

Free returns

Azeera advertises free returns on its gemstone rings. Its policy is a 30-day return with free FedEx return shipping and a full refund to the original payment method. Items must come back unworn and with all original documentation. Many of its rings are customizable, so check whether your specific piece is excluded.
