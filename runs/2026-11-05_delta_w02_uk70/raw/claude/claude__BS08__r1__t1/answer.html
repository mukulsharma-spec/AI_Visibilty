Things to check before buying
- Colour: Aquamarine ranges from pale to a richer sea-blue. Richer colour costs more, so compare photos in daylight if you can.
- Metal and karat: 18ct is more common in UK listings, while US retailers lean towards 14k.
- Natural or lab-created: Look for a clear statement, and avoid listings that only say "aquamarine" at a suspiciously low price.
- Returns policy: This matters most with overseas sellers.
