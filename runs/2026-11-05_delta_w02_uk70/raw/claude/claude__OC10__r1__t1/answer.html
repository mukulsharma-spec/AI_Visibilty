Congratulations to your daughter! The best place depends on your budget and the kind of piece you want, but here are some good options.

Online jewellers that specialise in gemstones

Angara has a huge range of gemstones, settings and birthstones, and lets you customise the stone, metal and size. It’s a good fit if you want something personal. Monica Vinader, Astrid & Miyu and Mejuri are popular for delicate, everyday pieces that a young graduate is likely to wear often.

High street and department stores (UK)

Goldsmiths, Ernest Jones and F. Hinds let you see the piece in person and are easy to return from. John Lewis also has a decent fine-jewellery selection.

Independent jewellers

Hatton Garden in London has dozens of independent jewellers, and you can often get something custom or better value for the quality. Etsy has independent makers if you want something handmade or one of a kind. Check the seller’s reviews and whether they state the metal and gemstone type clearly.

A few tips whichever route you choose:

Choose a stone with meaning. Her birthstone is classic, and a stone in her school or university colours also works well. Check whether the stone is natural, lab-created or simulated, as this affects the price a lot. Lab-created stones can look identical and cost far less. Look for a clear returns policy, and ask for a gift receipt in case the length or style isn’t quite right.
