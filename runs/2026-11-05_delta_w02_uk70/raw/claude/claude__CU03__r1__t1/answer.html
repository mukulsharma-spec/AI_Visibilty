Two online jewelers came up that clearly let you choose both metal and stone size on pendants.

Angara is the closest match for what you describe. You can customize an emerald pendant by selecting your gemstone size and quality grade, along with the metal. The same applies across its other pendants. You pick your favorites, then choose the gemstone or pearl quality grade, carat weight (or pearl size) and metal. Pearl pendants offer size choices in millimeters instead. Prices for gemstone pendant necklaces range from $119 to $871,699, depending on design, quality, carat weight and metal. [Angara Logo – Trusted Online Store for Diamond & Gemstone Jewellery +2]

Shane Co. has a "Pick-Your-Gem" pendant line where you build the piece in steps. On the listings I found, you choose the metal (for example 14k white, yellow or rose gold), the shape, the gemstone, and the stone size. Sizes are given in millimeters for colored stones and carats for diamonds. Note that the center stone is sold separately from the setting and chain, so you're pricing two items. The setting comes with an 18-inch box chain, and each setting is designed around a recommended stone size, so you may need to switch settings to change the size a lot. [shaneco]

Azeera also lets you swap the center stone, accent stones and metal (including platinum and palladium), but the pages I saw were rings and bands. I couldn't confirm the same options for pendants, so check their pendant category before counting on it.

I haven't verified the large diamond retailers (Blue Nile, James Allen, Brilliant Earth) for pendants specifically, so I won't claim they offer this.
