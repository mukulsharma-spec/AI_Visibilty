Amethyst is a lovely choice, and it’s a popular and affordable gem, so there are plenty of good places to look. Since you’re in London, here are some options.

Specialist gem and jewellery retailers

  • Angara is known for coloured gemstones and lets you customise the metal, stone shape, and size. It ships internationally.
  • Blue Nile and James Allen have online selection and good stone detail, though they’re more diamond-focused.
  • Astley Clarke and Monica Vinader are British brands with modern, wearable amethyst pieces in the mid-range.

High street and department stores

  • Goldsmiths, Ernest Jones, H. Samuel, and Beaverbrooks carry amethyst rings, pendants, and earrings, and you can see the piece in person.
  • John Lewis and Selfridges have a mix of fine and fashion jewellery.

Something more unique

  • Etsy has independent makers and handmade or custom pieces, and many will personalise or engrave.
  • Hatton Garden in London is the jewellery district. A local jeweller there can show you loose stones or make a bespoke piece.

Tips for choosing

  • Colour: Deeper, evenly saturated purple is generally more valuable than pale lilac.
  • Metal: Amethyst looks great in both yellow gold and silver or white gold, so match what she usually wears.
  • Durability: Amethyst is fairly hard (7 on the Mohs scale), but a pendant or earrings will hold up better than a ring she wears daily.
  • Authenticity: Look for retailers that state whether the stone is natural, and avoid anything that doesn’t say.
