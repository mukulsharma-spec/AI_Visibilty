Better value, more effort
Hatton Garden, London. One guide calls it the best starting point for value, choice and certified diamonds. Independent jewellers there can often show you loose stones and set them for you. [revediamonds]
Birmingham Jewellery Quarter. A Mumsnet user said they had sapphire platinum studs made there for under half of a £2,000 budget. [mumsnet]
Auction houses, antique shops and pawn shops. Another poster suggested these for good value and unusual stones, and secondhand is also better ethically. [mumsnet]

Things to check before buying
Natural or lab-grown. Both are “real”, but they are priced very differently, and lab-grown stones should be labelled as such.
Whether the stone is treated. Heat treatment of sapphires and rubies and oil treatment of emeralds are common, and reputable sellers disclose them.
Metal and hallmark. UK gold and silver should carry a hallmark, and 9ct gold is common at this budget.
Carat weight. Some listings give total carat weight (t.w.) across all stones, so a big number may not mean a big centre stone.
Returns. Look for a clear returns policy, since many retailers offer around 14 to 30 days.
