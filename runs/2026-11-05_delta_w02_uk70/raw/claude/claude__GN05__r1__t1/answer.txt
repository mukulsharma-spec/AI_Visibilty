There isn’t one best place. It depends on whether you want a ready-made piece, a custom setting, or a loose stone, and on your budget. The “best of” articles that come up in searches are mostly written by retailers or affiliate sites. For example, Brilliant Earth’s own guide names Brilliant Earth the best all-around retailer, so I’d treat these rankings with some skepticism. Here’s what I’d consider, drawing on both the search results and general knowledge:

Colored-gemstone specialists
Angara focuses on colored gemstones (sapphire, ruby, emerald, aquamarine, tanzanite and more) in many settings and price points, with lots of customization and lab-created options.
Leibish & Co. is known for fancy-colored diamonds and unusual loose stones, and specializes in high-quality fancy colored diamonds and colored gemstones.

Diamond-first retailers with colored stone options
James Allen is often praised for its selection. One reviewer says it has the largest selection of loose colored precious gemstones among the retailers they compared, and it offers 360° stone imagery.
Blue Nile is reliable, though colored gemstones are a secondary focus to their diamond business.
Brilliant Earth has a more limited colored range, mostly sapphires, rubies, and emeralds, but is strong on sourcing transparency.

Other routes
Etsy and independent jewelers can be good for one-of-a-kind or vintage pieces, but quality varies, so check seller reviews and return terms.
Mejuri or similar brands suit everyday, lower-cost gemstone pieces, though the stones are usually smaller and less “fine” in grade.

What matters more than the retailer
Treatment disclosure: most sapphires, rubies and emeralds are heated or oil-filled. Reputable sellers say so clearly.
Natural vs. lab-created: the price difference is huge, so make sure the listing is explicit.
Certification: for higher-value stones, look for a report from GIA, AGL, or a similar lab.
Return policy: color looks different on every screen, so a 30-day return window is worth a lot.
Real photos or video: these beat stock images for judging color.
