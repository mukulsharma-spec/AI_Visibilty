Several reputable online jewelers sell sapphire necklaces, depending on your budget and what you want.

Specialist online jewelers: Blue Nile, James Allen, Brilliant Earth, and Angara all have large sapphire selections, with detailed stone information and good return policies. Angara in particular focuses on colored gemstones and offers customization.

Established luxury and high-street names: Tiffany & Co., Cartier, Mappin & Webb, Goldsmiths, Ernest Jones, and Laings carry sapphire pieces at higher price points or with strong brand backing. Mappin & Webb and Goldsmiths are good if you’re in the UK.

Marketplaces: Etsy is good for handmade and vintage pieces, and 1stDibs for antique or designer jewelry. Quality varies by seller, so check reviews carefully.

What to check before buying:

Treatment disclosure: Most sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal and accepted, but the seller should say so. Untreated stones cost significantly more.

Certification: For stones over about 1 carat or pricier pieces, look for a report from GIA, AGL, or a similar lab.

Origin and color: Origin (Ceylon/Sri Lanka, Kashmir, Burma, Madagascar) affects price, but a vivid, evenly colored stone matters more than the label.

Return policy: Look for at least 30 days, plus free insured shipping.

Metal: Check whether it’s solid gold or platinum rather than plated or filled.
