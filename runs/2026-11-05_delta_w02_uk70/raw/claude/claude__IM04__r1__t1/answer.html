Tanzanite pendants usually come in a few main styles:

Solitaire: A single oval, pear, or cushion-cut stone on a simple chain. This is the classic choice, and it lets the violet-blue color stand out.

Diamond halo: The tanzanite is ringed with small diamonds, which adds sparkle and makes the stone look larger. Several of the pendants above use this style.

Three-stone or accent designs: Tanzanite paired with diamonds or other gemstones, often in a more elaborate or vintage-inspired setting.

Metal: White gold and platinum bring out the cool blue tones. Yellow gold gives a warmer contrast and makes the violet stand out more.

Quality: Look for deep, saturated blue-violet color, since paler stones are usually cheaper. Tanzanite is softer than sapphire (about 6.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale), so pendants suit it better than rings because they take less daily wear.
