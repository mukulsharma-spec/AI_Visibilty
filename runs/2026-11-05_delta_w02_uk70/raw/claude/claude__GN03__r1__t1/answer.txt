The best store depends on what you’re buying, and many “best of” lists are written by retailers or affiliate sites. One top-10 site says openly that it earns commissions from the brands listed, which influences how listings are presented. The same few names keep coming up, though.

Engagement rings and diamonds: Blue Nile, James Allen and Brilliant Earth appear most often. One guide rates James Allen for engagement rings and Blue Nile for loose diamonds. For lab-grown stones, the same guide picks Clean Origin for price, and another source calls lab-grown diamonds an excellent choice for online buyers in 2026.

Everyday fine jewelry: Mejuri is the usual reference point for dainty studs, thin bands and layering necklaces, according to a comparison that also lists smaller boutique alternatives.

Colored gemstones: Angara is named as best for variety.

Fashion and statement pieces: Kendra Scott is best for colorful, stone-forward fashion jewelry. Jenny Bird and BaubleBar also show up often for trend-led, lower-priced pieces.

Big retailers with physical stores: Zales suits you if you want pickup, resizing or repairs, plus financing plans. Ross-Simons is often cited for value.

Whichever store you choose, look for:
Product listings that state stone size, metal and dimensions clearly.
A clear return policy.
Real customer reviews.
