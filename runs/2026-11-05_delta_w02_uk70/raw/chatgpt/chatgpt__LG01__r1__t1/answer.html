What to check before buying

Gemstone: Make sure it says lab-grown, laboratory-created or synthetic sapphire, rather than simulated sapphire or glass.
Metal: Check whether it's solid 9ct/18ct gold, gold vermeil or gold-plated silver.
Certification: For an expensive sapphire, ask for an independent gem report, such as IGI.
Returns: Check the returns policy, particularly for made-to-order rings.
