If you're looking for real emerald jewellery without paying ridiculous luxury-brand markups, I'd start with specialist gemstone jewellers rather than designer brands.

I'd focus on sites that offer:

Natural emeralds with clear disclosure (or clearly labelled lab-grown emeralds).
Solid gold or sterling silver settings.
Proper gemstone descriptions and return policies.
Prices that reflect the stone and craftsmanship, not just the brand name.

What I'd recommend

Gemondo would be my first stop if you want genuine emeralds at reasonable prices. Gemporia would be my second.

One important thing: natural emeralds, lab-grown emeralds and green glass are three very different things. Always check which you're buying.
