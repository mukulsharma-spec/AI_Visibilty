Three things I'd insist on before buying

Ruby: Confirm whether it's natural or lab-grown, and whether it has been heat-treated.

Diamonds: Ask for the total diamond carat weight, colour and clarity.

Gold: I'd favour 18ct gold over 9ct for a special piece. Ask about secure screw-back fittings too.
