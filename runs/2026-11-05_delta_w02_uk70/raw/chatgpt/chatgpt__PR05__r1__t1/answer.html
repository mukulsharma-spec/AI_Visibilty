I need a real ruby necklace but my budget is £1,500. Where should I look?

With a budget of £1,500, you can buy a beautiful genuine ruby necklace, including some lovely options in solid 18ct gold.

I'd concentrate on independent fine jewellers, Hatton Garden, and reputable vintage jewellers rather than high-street fashion jewellery.

One important distinction: you want a natural ruby, not a synthetic or lab-grown ruby. Natural rubies can be heat-treated, which is normal, but the treatment should be disclosed.

What I'd insist on before buying

For a £1,500 purchase, ask the jeweller:

Is the ruby natural or lab-created?

Has it been heat-treated, glass-filled or fracture-filled?

Can you provide an independent gemmological certificate?

Is the chain solid gold as well as the pendant?

Is the piece hallmarked?

I'd be particularly cautious about glass-filled rubies, as they are significantly less desirable than conventional natural, heat-treated rubies.
