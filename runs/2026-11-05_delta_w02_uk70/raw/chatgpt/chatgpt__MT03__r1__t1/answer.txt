What I'd look for when buying a ruby

Natural vs lab-grown: Natural rubies are rarer; lab-grown rubies are more affordable.
Colour: Look for a rich, vivid red.
Treatment: Ask whether the ruby is heated, unheated or fracture-filled.
Certification: Request an independent gemmological report for a valuable natural ruby.
Gold: 18ct yellow gold is particularly beautiful with a deep red ruby.
