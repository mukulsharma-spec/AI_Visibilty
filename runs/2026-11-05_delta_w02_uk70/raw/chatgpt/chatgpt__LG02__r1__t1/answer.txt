A few things I'd look for

A genuine synthetic/lab-created emerald, not green cubic zirconia or glass.
9ct or 18ct solid gold if you want something long-lasting.
A certificate or written confirmation of the gemstone identity.
A protective setting, particularly if you'll wear it every day.
