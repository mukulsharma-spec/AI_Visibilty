Which online jewelers provide certification or grading reports for colored gemstones?

Several online jewellers and specialist gemstone retailers provide certificates of authenticity or independent gemological grading reports for coloured gemstones such as sapphires, rubies, emeralds, spinels and tourmalines.

If you're buying a valuable gemstone, I recommend prioritising retailers that provide reports from independent laboratories rather than relying solely on their own certificates.

What should a gemstone certificate contain?

Geographic origin

	

May establish whether it comes from a particular country




Weight and measurements

	

Confirms the stone's physical specifications




Report number

	

Allows independent verification

One important distinction: Coloured gemstones don't have a universally standardised grading system equivalent to the diamond 4Cs. A laboratory report primarily establishes identity, treatments and sometimes origin; it does not necessarily guarantee a particular quality or market value.
