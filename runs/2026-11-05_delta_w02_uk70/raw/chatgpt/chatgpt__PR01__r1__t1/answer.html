What I'd prioritise

For a ring you'll treasure for years, I'd look for:

Natural sapphire: Make sure it isn't lab-created unless that's what you want.

Colour: Rich cornflower or royal blue, rather than very dark navy.

Gold: Ideally 18ct, although 9ct is perfectly wearable.

Cut: Oval is particularly beautiful; cushion is a lovely alternative.

Treatment: Heat treatment is normal, but ask the jeweller to disclose it.
