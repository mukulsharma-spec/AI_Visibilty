Which gemstone should you choose?

Ruby

Emerald

Represents hope, growth and new beginnings. Gorgeous green colour, although it needs a little more care.

Amethyst

A beautiful purple gemstone. Often more affordable, making it a lovely option for a meaningful gift.

Aquamarine

A soft blue gemstone associated with calmness and lasting love. Elegant and understated.
