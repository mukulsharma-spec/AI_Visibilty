Where can I buy real gemstone earrings under £750?

You can find beautiful genuine gemstone earrings for under £750 from fine-jewellery retailers, independent jewellers and established gemstone specialists.

I'd concentrate on solid gold settings (9ct, 14ct or 18ct) with natural gemstones, rather than gold-plated fashion jewellery.

Some particularly lovely gemstones to consider:

Sapphire – timeless, elegant and available in blue, pink and yellow.

Emerald – rich green, especially beautiful in yellow gold.

Ruby – classic deep red.

Aquamarine – delicate, pale blue and sophisticated.

Tourmaline – wonderful range of colours.

Amethyst – beautiful purple, often excellent value.

A quick tip before buying

If you specifically want real gemstones, ask the jeweller:

Are the stones natural or laboratory-grown?

Have they been heat-treated, dyed or otherwise enhanced?

Is the setting solid gold, rather than gold vermeil or plating?

Can they provide gemstone details or a certificate where appropriate?
