A few things I'd recommend before buying

For an emerald engagement ring, check:

Natural vs lab-grown: Natural emeralds are rarer; lab-grown emeralds can be more affordable.

Colour: Look for a rich, even green rather than a pale or overly dark stone.

Clarity: Emeralds naturally have inclusions, so don't expect diamond-like clarity.

Setting: A protective bezel or secure claw setting is important because emeralds are relatively delicate.

Certification: Ask for independent gemmological documentation and disclosure of any treatments.
