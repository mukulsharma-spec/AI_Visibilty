A couple of things worth checking
Full vs half eternity: Full eternity has stones all the way around; half eternity is generally easier to resize and often more comfortable.

Natural vs lab-grown: Check whether both the sapphires and diamonds are natural or lab-grown.

Setting: Channel settings give a smooth, elegant finish; claw settings show more of each stone.

Metal: 18ct gold or platinum are lovely choices for a substantial, long-lasting piece.
