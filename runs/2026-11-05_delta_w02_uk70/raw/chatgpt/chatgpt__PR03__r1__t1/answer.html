What I'd prioritise when buying

For this budget, I'd look for:

Natural sapphires, with disclosure of any heat treatment.

Deep royal or cornflower blue rather than very dark navy.

Ideally 0.8–1.5ct combined sapphire weight.

18ct gold or platinum.

Screw-back or secure butterfly fittings.

A matching pair with similar colour and clarity.

My recommendation

I'd put the Katherine James (£1,995) and Fine Jewellery Company (£1,400) at the top of the shortlist. The first is more glamorous; the second is more understated and wearable.
