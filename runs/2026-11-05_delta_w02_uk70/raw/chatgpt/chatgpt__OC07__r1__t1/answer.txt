What I'd look for in the sapphire
 
 
9
 

For a special anniversary, I'd consider three styles:

A sapphire and diamond ring: A beautiful, substantial keepsake that she can wear every day.

A sapphire pendant: Elegant, timeless and avoids the difficulty of guessing her ring size.

Sapphire earrings: Particularly lovely if she already wears earrings regularly.

A few important buying tips:

Ask whether the sapphire is natural or lab-grown.

Ask whether it has been heat-treated. Heat treatment is common, but it should be disclosed.

For a significant purchase, request an independent gemmological report confirming the stone and its treatments. GCS, GIA, SSEF and Gübelin are names worth knowing.
