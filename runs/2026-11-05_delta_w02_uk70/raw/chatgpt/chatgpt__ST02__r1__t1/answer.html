My buying advice

If you want genuine natural peridot, I'd particularly look at Abiza and Henryka. Check that the listing explicitly says natural peridot rather than created or simulated peridot.

I'd also compare:

Sterling silver: lovely for everyday wear.

9ct gold: a more traditional fine-jewellery option.

Gold vermeil: a good middle ground if you prefer a golden finish.
