What I'd insist on before spending £5,000+

For a significant coloured gemstone purchase, I'd request:

Independent laboratory report: ideally GIA, SSEF, Gübelin, GRS or GCS, as appropriate.

Treatment disclosure: particularly important for rubies, sapphires and emeralds.

Origin information: if the seller is charging a premium for a particular origin.

Written valuation: suitable for insurance.

Return and aftercare terms: especially for bespoke or resized rings.

An independent laboratory such as 
GCS can assess gemstone identity, treatments and, where possible, geographical origin.
