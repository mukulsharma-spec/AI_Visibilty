A useful distinction

When comparing retailers, check whether they offer:

Natural coloured gemstones: Earth-mined emeralds, rubies, sapphires, spinels, etc.

Lab-grown coloured gemstones: Laboratory-created emeralds, rubies, sapphires, etc.

Natural and lab-grown diamonds: These are a separate category and shouldn't be confused with coloured gemstones.
