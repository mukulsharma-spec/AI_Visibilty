Which online jewelers disclose gemstone treatments like heating or filling?

Several online gemstone and jewellery retailers explicitly publish gemstone treatment policies, rather than simply describing stones as “natural.”

If you're buying natural rubies, sapphires, emeralds or other coloured gemstones, I'd prioritise sellers who disclose heating, oiling, resin filling, glass filling, diffusion, dyeing and irradiation.

Here are some worth investigating, particularly if you're buying from the UK.

One particularly important distinction: natural does not mean untreated.

For example, a natural sapphire can be heated, and a natural emerald can be oil-treated.

The UK National Association of Jewellers also publishes guidance on gemstone descriptions and disclosure, including the need to identify treatments clearly in online descriptions.

My recommendation

I would start with Apsara Gems and Topgem if you're shopping in the UK. Their published policies are unusually specific about treatments, including some treatments that are particularly important to avoid confusing with ordinary heating.

One additional precaution: for an expensive gemstone, request an independent report from a recognised laboratory such as GIA, SSEF or Gübelin. A retailer's own disclosure policy is not equivalent to independent verification.
