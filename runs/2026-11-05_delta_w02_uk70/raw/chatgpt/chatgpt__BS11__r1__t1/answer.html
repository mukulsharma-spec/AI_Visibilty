A quick tip before buying

Check whether the listing says:

Natural opal: Genuine opal gemstone.

Opal doublet or triplet: Genuine opal bonded to backing layers.

Synthetic opal: Lab-created material, rather than naturally mined opal.

Opal effect or imitation: Not genuine opal.
