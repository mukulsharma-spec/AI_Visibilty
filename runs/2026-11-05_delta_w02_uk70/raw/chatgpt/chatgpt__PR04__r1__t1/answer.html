A few things I'd look for

14ct or 18ct gold: A lovely choice for a lasting piece.

Natural versus lab-grown emerald: Make sure you know which you're buying. Lab-grown emeralds can offer excellent colour for less.

Treatment disclosure: Many natural emeralds are oiled or fracture-filled. Ask the jeweller about treatments.

Certification: Ask for details of the emerald's origin, weight and any treatments, particularly for a more expensive purchase.
