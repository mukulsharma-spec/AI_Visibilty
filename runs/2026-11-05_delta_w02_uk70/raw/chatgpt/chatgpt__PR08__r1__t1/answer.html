What I'd look for with £2,000

A few things matter particularly with tanzanite:

Colour: Look for a rich, saturated violet-blue rather than pale or washed-out blue.

Clarity: Ideally eye-clean, without obvious inclusions.

Cut: A well-cut oval, cushion or emerald cut can look beautiful.

Gold: I'd aim for 18ct if possible, although 14ct is also a good choice.

Certification: Ask for independent confirmation of natural tanzanite, its carat weight and any treatments. Most tanzanite is heat-treated, which is normal.
