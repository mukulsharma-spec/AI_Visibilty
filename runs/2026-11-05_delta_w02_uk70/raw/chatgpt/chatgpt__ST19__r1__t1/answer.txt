What to look for when buying

Natural ruby: Mined gemstone, generally more expensive.

Lab-created ruby: Real ruby with the same chemical composition, usually more affordable.

Gold: 9ct is a good starting point; 18ct is a more luxurious option.

Certification: Check whether the ruby is heat-treated, untreated or synthetic, and ask for a gemstone certificate or treatment disclosure.
