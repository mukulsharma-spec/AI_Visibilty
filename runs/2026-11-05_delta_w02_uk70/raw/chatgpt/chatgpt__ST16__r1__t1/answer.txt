A few things to check before buying

Natural or lab-grown: Both are genuine sapphires, but natural stones generally cost more.

Gold quality: Check whether the necklace is solid 9ct/18ct gold, gold vermeil or simply gold plated.

Stone details: Look for sapphire carat weight, colour and any treatments.

Returns: Check the returns policy, particularly for made-to-order jewellery.
