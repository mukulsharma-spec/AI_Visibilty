What to check before buying

A lab-grown ruby is a real ruby (corundum), not an imitation. I'd check:

Stone: Confirm it is laboratory-grown ruby, not glass or red spinel.
Metal: Ideally 14ct or 18ct solid gold, or platinum.
Treatment: Ask whether the stone has undergone any colour or clarity treatment.
Documentation: Get the gemstone description in writing.
Returns: Check whether bespoke rings can be returned.
