Something important about tanzanite

Tanzanite is relatively soft (6–7 on the Mohs scale) and can be damaged by knocks. This matters for an engagement ring worn every day. I'd favour:

A protective halo or bezel setting.

A low-profile design rather than a tall, exposed solitaire.

Platinum or 18ct gold.

A gemstone certificate or independent appraisal.

A good returns policy and a written warranty.

I'd also ask for actual photographs or video of the exact stone in daylight, rather than relying on heavily enhanced website photographs.

One thing I'd like to establish before recommending a particular ring

Tanzanite varies enormously in colour and quality. I'd particularly look for a rich, saturated royal blue with violet undertones, rather than a pale purple-blue.
