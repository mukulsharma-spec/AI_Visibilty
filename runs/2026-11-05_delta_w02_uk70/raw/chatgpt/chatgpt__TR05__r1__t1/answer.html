Is it safe to buy a sapphire engagement ring online? Where should I buy one?

Yes! Buying a sapphire engagement ring online can be very safe, and you can often find better value and a much wider choice online than in traditional high-street jewellers.

The important thing is choosing a reputable jeweller that provides proper gemstone information, independent certification where appropriate, and a good returns policy.

I would be quite selective, especially when buying an engagement ring, because sapphires can vary enormously in quality, colour and value.

1. What to look for before buying

Natural or lab-grown sapphire

Check that the listing clearly states which it is. Natural sapphires are mined; lab-grown sapphires have essentially the same chemical composition but are generally much less expensive.

Independent gemstone report

For a valuable natural sapphire, look for a report from a respected gem laboratory, such as GIA or SSEF. It should identify the gemstone and disclose treatments.

Treatment disclosure

Heat treatment is common and accepted in the sapphire trade. However, untreated sapphires can command a premium. Be wary of undisclosed diffusion treatment or fracture filling.

Returns and warranty

Check the return period, resizing policy, independent valuation, insurance documentation and lifetime maintenance arrangements before paying.
