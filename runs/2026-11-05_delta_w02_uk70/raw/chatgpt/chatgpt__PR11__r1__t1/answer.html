How to make sure they're genuinely real

This is the important bit. Before buying, I'd ask the jeweller:

Are the sapphires natural earth-mined corundum, rather than lab-created?

Have they been heat-treated? (Heat-treated natural sapphire is still real sapphire.)

Can you supply an independent gemmological report confirming natural origin?

Does the report identify any diffusion treatment or fracture filling?

The National Association of Jewellers distinguishes natural gemstones from synthetic gemstones, even when they have the same chemical composition.
