What I'd look for specifically
Setting: A full bezel with a smooth, rounded rim, rather than a partial bezel or bezel-prong combination.
Stone: A small-to-medium round or oval sapphire. A flatter cut can help keep the whole ring closer to the finger.
Metal: Solid 14k gold is a good everyday-jewelry choice; platinum is another durable option.
Band: A smooth, sturdy band without pavé stones, sharp edges, or elaborate side detailing.
Fit: Ask for the actual setting height in millimeters, because “low profile” doesn't guarantee that it will fit comfortably under snug medical gloves.
