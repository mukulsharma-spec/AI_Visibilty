Before you buy
Natural vs. lab-grown: Both are real gemstones, but if you want a stone formed in the earth, make sure the listing explicitly says natural.
Treatments: Heated sapphires and oiled emeralds can still be genuine natural gems. Check that treatments are disclosed.
Metal: Prefer solid 14K or 18K gold for a lasting keepsake.
Documentation: For an expensive stone, consider an independent report from a recognized gemological laboratory such as GIA.
Returns: Check the return window and exclusions for customized or engraved pieces before ordering.
