Congratulations! Here’s where I’d look, depending on what you want.

Best for real colored gemstones and birthstones

  • Angara: huge selection of natural and lab-created gemstones, and you can customize settings and metals. Great if you want the baby’s birthstone.
  • Brilliant Earth: strong on sapphires, emeralds, and diamonds, with good certification and return policies.
  • Blue Nile / James Allen: best for diamonds and stackable bands, with detailed grading info online.

Good value, fast shipping

  • Ross-Simons: often has real gemstone pieces at lower prices, and frequent sales.
  • Mejuri: modern, everyday-wear fine jewelry in solid gold, with some gemstone options.
  • Zales / Kay / Jared: easy for mother’s rings (multiple birthstones in one ring) if you want to see things in person.

Custom or one-of-a-kind

  • A local independent jeweler: they can design something around her taste and the baby’s birthstone, and you can see the stones in person.
  • Etsy: lots of handmade options, but stick to sellers with many reviews and clear info about the stone and metal.

Tips

  • Ask whether a stone is natural or lab-created. Both are “real” gemstones, but natural costs much more.
  • For bigger purchases, ask for GIA or AGL certification.
  • Look for 14k or 18k gold and check the return and resizing policy.
  • Popular push present picks are a mother’s ring with the baby’s birthstone, an eternity band, a pendant necklace, or stud earrings.
