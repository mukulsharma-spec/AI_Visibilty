Tips for a nurse’s ring

Choose a plain bezel. Milgrain, engraving, and textured details can trap soap and grime. A smooth, polished bezel is easier to keep clean with frequent hand washing.
Keep the stone’s profile low. A flat or slice-cut sapphire sits lower than a tall domed or faceted one. That means less glove snagging, and gloves tear less often.
Go with a half-round or comfort-fit band. It feels better over long shifts, and it’s easier to wear under gloves.
Choose 14k gold or platinum. 14k is a bit harder than 18k and holds up better to daily wear.
Ask about resizing and return policies. Her hands may swell during long shifts, so it helps to size carefully and to know if you can resize or return it.
