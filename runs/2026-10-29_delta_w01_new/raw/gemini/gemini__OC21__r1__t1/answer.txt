Finding a push present featuring a real gemstone is a wonderful, meaningful way to celebrate your wife and the new baby. Many people opt to use the baby's birthstone (or a combination of your family's birthstones), making the jewelry deeply personal.

Where you should look in the USA depends on your budget, whether you want to shop online or in-person, and if you want something custom-made.

2. High-End Department Stores (In-Person & Online)

If you prefer the peace of mind of walking into a physical store, seeing the gemstones in person, and getting immediate customer service, look at upscale department store fine jewelry departments:

Nordstrom: Many major Nordstrom locations carry fine jewelry lines featuring real precious and semi-precious gemstones (like LAGOS, Bony Levy, or Meira T). They make great, elegant everyday pieces like gemstone stations on gold chains.

Bloomingdale’s / Saks Fifth Avenue: These stores feature robust fine jewelry floors with designers who frequently work with colorful sapphires, emeralds, rubies, and opals.

3. Local Independent Jewelers (The Best Route for Ultimate Customization)

If you have a specific vision—like incorporating the baby’s birthstone alongside yours, or engraving the baby's birthdate and initials on the back of a pendant—your local independent jeweler is often your best bet.

Why go local: They can source specific, high-grade natural gemstones that fit your exact budget, show you stones under a loupe, and custom-design a mounting or engraving that a mass-market retailer can’t match.

Quick Tip on Practicality for New Moms

Keep in mind that new moms spend a lot of time holding, rocking, and dressing a baby. When choosing a real gemstone piece, consider setting style:

Bezel settings (where a metal rim wraps smoothly around the gemstone) or flush/channel settings are fantastic because they won't scratch the baby or catch on blankets and clothes.

Prong settings show off more of the gemstone's sparkle, but higher-set stones might require a bit more mindfulness with a newborn.
