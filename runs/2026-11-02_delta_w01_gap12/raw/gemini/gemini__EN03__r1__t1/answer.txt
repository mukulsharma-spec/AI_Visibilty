Quick Tips for Buying a Sapphire:

Durability: Sapphires rank a 9 on the Mohs hardness scale (diamonds are a 10), making them exceptionally durable and ideal for everyday wear as an engagement ring.

Natural vs. Lab-Grown: Lab-created sapphires are chemically, physically, and optically identical to natural mined sapphires, but typically cost significantly less. If you choose a natural stone, check whether it has been heated (standard practice to enhance color/clarity) or is unheated (rarer and commands a higher premium).
