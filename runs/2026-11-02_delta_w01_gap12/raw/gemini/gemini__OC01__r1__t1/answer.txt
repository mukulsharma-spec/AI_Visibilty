Since January’s birthstone is the garnet (traditionally known for its deep, rich red hues, though it also comes in rare green and other varieties), you have plenty of great options across the US, ranging from major traditional jewelry chains to fine online jewelers and trendy accessory brands.

1. Major Fine Jewelry Retailers (In-Store & Online)

Helzberg Diamonds: Offers a wide selection of garnet and diamond accent rings, necklaces, and earrings. You can browse online or visit one of their nationwide mall locations.   
Helzberg Diamonds

Zales & Jared: Both nationwide mall staples feature dedicated birthstone collections with classic garnet rings, tennis bracelets, pendants, and customizable options.   
Jared

Kohl’s & Macy’s: Excellent mid-tier department store options if you are looking for affordable sterling silver or gold-over-silver garnet statement pieces and gift sets.

2. Fine Online Jewelers (Customizable & High-End)

Angara: Known for extensive gemstone customization. They let you choose your exact garnet quality grade, carat size, and metal type (such as platinum, 14K white, yellow, or rose gold) for rings, earrings, and bracelets.   
Angara

Blue Nile & Brilliant Earth: Perfect if you are looking for high-end fine jewelry. Both offer exquisite fine-crafted garnet and diamond-accented pieces set in precious metals with free shipping and returns.   
Brilliant Earth

3. Contemporary & Designer Brands

Kendra Scott: Known for popular everyday birthstone lines (such as the Cailin and Davie collections) featuring vibrant red glass or genuine garnet styles across necklaces, huggie earrings, and stackable rings.   
Kendra Scott

BaubleBar: A great spot for trendier, playful, and budget-friendly pieces, including customizable birthstone Pisa bracelets, pendant necklaces, and stacking rings featuring garnet.   
BaubleBar

Catbird: Based out of Brooklyn with online shopping nationwide, they offer delicate, heirloom-quality independent designer garnet rings, charms, and stackable pieces.   
Catbird
