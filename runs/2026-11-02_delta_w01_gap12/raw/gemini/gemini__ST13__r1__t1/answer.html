Tips for Buying a Sapphire Ring Online in the USA:

Check the Certification: For high-value natural sapphires, look for independent lab reports from reputable authorities like the GIA (Gemological Institute of America) or AGL to verify authenticity and treatments.
GemsNY

Understand Treatments: Most natural sapphires on the market are gently heated to enhance color (which is standard and permanent). "Unheated" sapphires are rarer and command a higher collector's price.
GemsNY

Review Return Policies: Because color can look slightly different on a screen depending on lighting, ensure the retailer offers a secure shipping window and a reasonable return or exchange policy (most trusted US sites offer 14- to 30-day returns).
