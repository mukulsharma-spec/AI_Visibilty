Quick Shopping Tips for Fast Delivery:

Ready-to-Ship vs. Made-to-Order: Many online jewelers (like Angara or GemsNY) custom-craft pieces or set stones only after an order is placed, which adds processing time before fast shipping kicks in. If you need a piece immediately, look for sections labeled "Ready to Ship" or "In Stock."

Carrier Methods: Most high-end jewelry requires an adult signature upon delivery for insurance purposes, so "fast shipping" usually means expedited transit (e.g., 2-day or overnight via FedEx/UPS) after the item has been processed.
