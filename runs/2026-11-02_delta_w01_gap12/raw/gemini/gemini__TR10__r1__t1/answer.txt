Tips for Shopping Online in the USA:
Check Certification: For high-value precious gemstones (rubies, sapphires, emeralds), look for retailers that supply grading reports from recognized independent labs like the GIA (Gemological Institute of America) or IGI (International Gemological Institute).
Verify Treatment Disclosures: Many colored gemstones undergo standard heat treatments to enhance color. Top-rated stores will explicitly state treatment details ("Heated" vs. "No treatment reported") on the product page.
Review Return & Warranty Policies: Ensure the seller offers at least a 30-day return window and a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects so you can safely inspect the piece in natural lighting once it arrives.
