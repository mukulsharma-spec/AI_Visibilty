Tips for Buying a Ruby Ring Online:

Check for Certification: For higher-end natural rubies, ensure the jeweler provides a grading report from a recognized independent gemological laboratory like the GIA (Gemological Institute of America) or AGL (American Gemological Laboratories).
GemsNY

Natural vs. Lab-Grown: Decide beforehand whether you want a natural mined ruby or a lab-grown ruby (which is chemically and physically identical to a natural ruby, but typically much more budget-friendly).

Review Return Policies: Because you are buying online, always verify that the merchant offers a secure, insured shipping method and a clear return or exchange window (typically 30 days).
