Several reputable online jewelers catering to shoppers in the USA offer a 30-day money-back guarantee with free return shipping on gemstone rings (such as sapphires, rubies, emeralds, and other fine colored gemstones).

Prominent options include:

1. James Allen

The Policy: Offers a 30-day, 100% money-back guarantee on all jewelry, including their extensive collection of natural and lab-created gemstone rings.

Returns: Returns within the USA are fully covered with free return shipping. You just need to contact customer service to obtain a Return Authorization Number (RAN).

[Diamondrensu]

2. Angara

The Policy: Known specifically for their vast array of handcrafted gemstone rings (sapphires, rubies, emeralds, tanzanite, etc.), Angara features a 30-day return and exchange policy.

[Angara]

Returns: They provide free insured return shipping for eligible U.S. orders (typically including up to two free return labels per order).

[Angara]

3. Brilliant Earth

The Policy: Offers an extensive selection of ethically sourced gemstone and colored diamond rings. They provide a 30-day money-back guarantee from the date the item is available for shipment or pickup.

Returns: U.S. returns are free, and they supply a pre-paid, fully insured FedEx shipping label through their online return portal.

4. Blue Nile

The Policy: As one of the largest online fine jewelers, Blue Nile features a robust selection of gemstone cocktail and birthstone rings. They offer a 30-day return policy for a full refund on items in their original, unworn condition.

[Blue Nile]

Returns: Free shipping is provided on U.S. returns.

Important Tips When Shopping Online for Gemstone Rings:

Customization Exclusions: Keep in mind that standard 30-day return policies almost universally apply to "stock" or "running line" pieces. If you custom-design a ring from scratch or have the piece specially engraved, it is usually final sale or subject to a partial restocking/modification fee.

[Brilliant Earth]

Condition Requirements: To ensure a hassle-free refund, the ring must remain unworn, unaltered (un-resized by a third-party jeweler), and returned with all original laboratory certificates and packaging.

[Jagdish Jewellers]
