What to Keep in Mind When Buying Online:

Certifications: For high-value natural yellow sapphires, look for independent lab reports (such as GIA or AGS) to verify authenticity and treatments. Many yellow sapphires are heat-treated to enhance their color, which is standard industry practice, but unheated stones carry higher collectibility and value.

Return & Resize Policies: Ensure the retailer offers a clear return window (typically 30 days) and complimentary ring resizing, especially if you are purchasing an engagement ring.
