1. Fine Jewelry & Luxury Retailers (For Emerald Jewelry)

If you are looking for traditional gemstone rings, necklaces, earrings, or bracelets:

2. Artisan & Handcrafted Marketplaces (For Unique Keepsakes)

3. Department Stores & Home Goods (For Emerald Decor & Tableware)

If you are looking for home decor, glassware, or keepsakes in the theme's signature green hue:

  • Nordstrom & Saks Fifth Avenue: Ideal for luxury home accents like emerald glassware, vases, and crystal decor.
