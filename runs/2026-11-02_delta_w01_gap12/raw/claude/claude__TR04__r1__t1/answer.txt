Several well-known US online jewelers that sell gemstone rings offer free 30-day returns, though the details differ in ways that matter.

Angara (gemstone specialist): You have 30 days from the delivery date to return or exchange, and Angara emails a fully insured return label. You get up to two free return labels per order. Note that returns after the 30-day window carry a 20% restocking fee, and you pay return shipping yourself.
Blue Nile: Returns or exchanges are accepted within 30 days of shipment, with a fully insured free shipping label emailed within one business day of your request. The catch is that complimentary returns are limited to two per customer per year.
Brilliant Earth: It provides a FedEx label and free return shipping on all ring, loose diamond, and loose gemstone orders. The 30-day timeline starts when your purchase is available for shipment or pickup, not when it arrives.
Ritani: It offers a 30-day no-questions-asked return policy, covers return shipping and insurance, and charges no restocking fee.
Charles & Colvard (moissanite and lab gems): You have 30 days from the ship date to return, and US orders qualify for free return shipping.
AZEERA (gemstone-focused, customizable): It offers a 30-day money-back guarantee with free return shipping and a full refund.
Barkev's: It accepts returns for refund or exchange within 30 days of receipt, with free return shipping on all US orders.
Gem Noir: It provides free US returns within the 30-day window, with prepaid return labels.

A few things to check before buying. First, see when the 30 days start. Some retailers count from delivery (Angara, Barkev's), others from shipment (Blue Nile, Charles & Colvard, Brilliant Earth), which can cut a few days off your real window. Second, look for exclusions on certain rings. For example, Fire & Brilliance says full eternity rings can't be returned because they're made to a specific finger size, and custom or engraved pieces are commonly excluded elsewhere too. Third, keep the ring unworn and send back all the original packaging and certificates. Barkev's, for instance, requires the item in original, unworn condition, with all packaging, documentation, and any diamond certificate.

Policies change and sometimes get holiday extensions, so confirm on the retailer's returns page before checkout.
