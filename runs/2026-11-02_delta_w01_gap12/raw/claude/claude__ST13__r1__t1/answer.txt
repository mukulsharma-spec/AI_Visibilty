Here are some well-regarded places to buy sapphire rings online in the US, depending on what you're after:

Wide selection of loose sapphires and custom settings
Blue Nile: lets you pick a loose sapphire and pair it with a setting, with free shipping and returns.
James Allen: has 360° video of many stones, so you can inspect color and clarity before buying.
Brilliant Earth: strong on ethically sourced and lab-grown sapphires, with lots of colors beyond blue.

Sapphire and gemstone specialists
Angara: focuses on colored gemstones, with many ready-made sapphire ring designs across a range of qualities and prices.
The Natural Sapphire Company: one of the largest sapphire-specific dealers, good for unheated or rare colors.
Brilliant Earth / Ritani: both offer custom-design options if you want something unique.

Budget-friendly or mainstream
Kay, Zales, and Jared: frequent sales and financing, plus in-store service if you need resizing.
Costco: surprisingly good value with a generous return policy.
Etsy: great for vintage, handmade, or one-of-a-kind pieces. Check seller reviews and certification carefully.

Tips before you buy
Natural vs. lab-grown: lab sapphires are chemically identical and much cheaper. Natural ones cost more, especially if they're unheated.
Certification: for pricier stones, look for a report from GIA, AGL, or GRS that states the origin and any treatment.
Color matters most: a vivid, evenly saturated blue is usually more valuable than one that's too dark or too pale.
Return policy: aim for at least 30 days so you can see the ring in person.
