Several well-known online jewelers ship gemstone jewelry free within the US, but how fast "free" is varies quite a bit:

Angara (gemstone specialist). Angara offers free express shipping on all orders within the U.S., with no minimum purchase required. Delivery takes 3-4 business days and includes insurance and tracking. Returns are free too, with free express shipping and free return shipping within the US.

Blue Nile. Shipping speed depends on order value: overnight for orders over $500, two-day for orders under $500. There's no minimum purchase and no code required.

James Allen. It has the same structure as Blue Nile. Fast, free, insured shipping applies regardless of order amount, and US orders over $500 ship overnight. Orders under $500 ship two-day.

Brilliant Earth. Free FedEx shipping on every order, plus free return shipping. It doesn't promise overnight delivery the way Blue Nile and James Allen do, and Saturday delivery costs an extra $30.

Gem Jewelers Co. (smaller, Los Angeles-based). This is the most generous on speed: free overnight shipping anywhere in the USA, fully insured. Its focus is natural rubies, sapphires, and emeralds set with diamonds, mostly at higher price points.

One important caveat is that the shipping speed only counts from the day the item leaves the warehouse, not from when you order. Made-to-order or customized pieces can take one to three weeks before they ship. For example, James Allen notes that jewelry and engagement rings can take one to three weeks depending on production schedule. If you need something by a specific date, look for "ready to ship" or "in stock" filters and check the estimated delivery date before you check out.

If you want overnight shipping on a smaller purchase (under $500), Gem Jewelers Co. is the clear standout. For a wide range of colored gemstones at various price points with fast free shipping regardless of order size, Angara is a strong option. Policies can change, especially around the holidays, so it's worth confirming on each site before ordering.
