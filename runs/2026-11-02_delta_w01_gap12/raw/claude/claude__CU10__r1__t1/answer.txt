Here are some good options for designing a gemstone ring online in the US. Many “design your own” tools are built around diamonds, so I’ve sorted these by how well they handle colored stones.

Best for colored gemstones

AZEERA has a dedicated custom gemstone ring design experience, with jewelry experts available for advice. [azeera]
Angara is a well-known US retailer built around colored gemstones (sapphire, emerald, ruby, aquamarine and more) with a customization tool. This one is from my own knowledge rather than my search, so check its current features.
Brilliant Earth is known for ethical sourcing and a large selection of sapphires, and it offers custom design services.

Best for diamonds, with some gemstone options

Blue Nile created the first major online custom ring builder and offers virtual appointments alongside its online tools. [princessly]
James Allen is one of the platforms noted for a streamlined, user-friendly custom design experience. [thecaratcut]

Best for fully bespoke or one-of-a-kind designs

CustomMade is a marketplace where you work with an independent jeweler. It’s recommended for making a ring no one else has. [gemfind]
Valley Rose lets you design a custom ring through a virtual consultation. It offers fairmined gold, and its overnight shipping from the USA is free and insured. [valleyrosestudio]
Etsy has many independent jewelers who take custom commissions. Check reviews carefully.

Tips

Custom work typically takes 3–6 weeks from first consultation to finished ring, and prices often fall between $1,500 and $12,000, mostly driven by the center stone. [shesaidyes]
For colored stones, ask whether the stone comes with a lab report (GIA, AGL, etc.) and whether it’s natural, lab-grown or treated. Heat treatment is common and affects price.
Match the stone to your lifestyle. Sapphire, ruby and diamond suit daily wear, while softer stones like opal, emerald and morganite need more care or a protective setting.
Before ordering, check the return policy for custom pieces, since many are final sale.
