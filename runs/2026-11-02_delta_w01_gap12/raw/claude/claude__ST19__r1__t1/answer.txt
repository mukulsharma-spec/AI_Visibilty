Vintage, antique, and independent makers
1stDibs: antique and estate pieces from vetted dealers.
Etsy: handmade and vintage rings from independent jewelers. Quality varies, so check reviews and certification.

Tips before you buy
Natural vs. lab-grown: Lab-grown rubies are chemically identical to natural ones and cost far less. Make sure the listing says which you're getting.
Treatments: Most natural rubies are heat-treated, which is standard and stable. Be cautious with "glass-filled" or "composite" rubies. They're much cheaper and less durable.
Certification: For a significant purchase, look for a report from a lab like GIA, AGL, or GRS that confirms the stone's origin and treatment.
Color matters most: A vivid, slightly purplish-to-pure red is the most valued. Very dark or pinkish stones usually cost less.
Return policy: Look for at least a 30-day return window, since colors can look different on screen than in person.
