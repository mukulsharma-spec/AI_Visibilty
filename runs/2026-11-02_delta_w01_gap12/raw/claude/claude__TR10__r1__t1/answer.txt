Here's how the main US gemstone-focused retailers compare on independent review platforms. Keep in mind that review scores shift over time and companies that actively invite reviews tend to score higher, so read some recent negative reviews too.

Angara is the most commonly cited gemstone specialist, with a wide range of colored stones in configurable quality grades. On Trustpilot it has a TrustScore of 4.5 out of 5 (4.7 average) from about 3,000 reviews, and it invites all customers to review, positive or negative. On Bizrate it scores 9.4/10 across post-delivery reviews, and the company is headquartered in Los Angeles. One independent guide names Angara the best pick for gemstones based on variety. The main complaint pattern is shipping: one reviewer said their item didn't ship before the expected delivery date and noted other reviews mentioned the same issue.

The Natural Sapphire Company (New York) is a strong choice if you specifically want sapphires, rubies, or emeralds, especially untreated ones. It holds a 5.0 out of 5 rating on WeddingWire from 251 reviews, and has about 3,220 reviews collected on Verified Reviews since 2019. Reviewers frequently praise the one-on-one help from consultants on custom pieces.

Brilliant Earth is better known for diamonds and ethical sourcing but carries gemstone jewelry too. Its Trustpilot TrustScore is 4 out of 5 (4.1 average) from about 1,486 reviews. One review aggregator notes it charges roughly a 15-20% premium over James Allen and Blue Nile for comparable diamonds, and complaints often involve delivery timing.

Ross-Simons is a value-oriented option for affordable gemstone pieces. It has a Trustpilot TrustScore of 4 out of 5 (4.2 average) across about 2,000 reviews, and a 9.1/10 overall satisfaction on Bizrate from over 7,000 reviews. A recurring theme in critical reviews is that stones and chains appear smaller or finer in person than in website photos, so check dimensions carefully.

GemSelect is worth knowing about if you want loose gemstones rather than finished jewelry; a recent Bizrate reviewer said their stones always match the pictures.

For the highest ratings with a large review volume, Angara and The Natural Sapphire Company stand out among gemstone specialists. Whichever you choose, look for independent gem lab certification on higher-value stones, disclosure of any treatments (heating, filling, etc.), and at least a 30-day return policy.
