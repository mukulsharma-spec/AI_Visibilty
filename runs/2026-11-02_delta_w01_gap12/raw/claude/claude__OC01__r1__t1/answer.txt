January’s birthstone is garnet, usually deep red, though it also comes in orange, green, and other colors. Here are good places to look in the US, grouped by what you want.

Mainstream jewelry chains (easy to browse, often have sales around holidays)

Kay, Zales, Jared, and Helzberg Diamonds carry garnet rings, pendants, earrings, and bracelets at moderate prices.
Macy’s, Nordstrom, and Costco offer a mix of fine and fashion garnet pieces.

Online fine jewelry

Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth are better for higher-quality or custom-style pieces.
Ross-Simons and Amazon (look for sellers with good reviews and clear gemstone details) are good for lower-priced options.

Handmade and unique pieces

Etsy has the widest variety, including custom birthstone jewelry. Check seller reviews and ask whether the garnet is natural or lab-created.
Local independent jewelers and craft fairs are also good, especially if you want something custom or can see it in person.

Trendy or personalized birthstone jewelry

Mejuri, Pandora (birthstone charms), and Catbird tend to have delicate, giftable pieces.
Many sites let you engrave or combine several birthstones, which works well for family or mother’s jewelry.

Tips

Garnet is fairly durable (about 6.5 to 7.5 on the Mohs scale), so it suits earrings and pendants best. For daily-wear rings, look for a protective setting.
Check whether the listing says “natural garnet” and what metal it’s set in. Sterling silver, 14k gold, and gold vermeil are common and differ a lot in price and longevity.
If it’s a gift, look at the return policy and ring sizing options.
