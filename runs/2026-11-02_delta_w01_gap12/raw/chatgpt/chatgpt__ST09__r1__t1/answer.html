What to check before buying

For a yellow sapphire ring, pay attention to:

Natural vs. lab-created: These are different products with different price points.

Treatment: Ask whether the stone is heated, unheated or diffusion-treated.

Certification: For a significant purchase, request an independent GIA or AGL report where available.

Metal: 14K or 18K solid gold is a good choice.

Returns: Check the return period and resizing policy.

The Jewelers of America also provides a useful colored gemstone buying guide.
