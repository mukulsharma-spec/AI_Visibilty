A few things to check before ordering
When buying a custom gemstone ring online, check:
Whether the gemstone is natural or lab-created.
Whether it has been heat-treated, irradiated or otherwise treated.
Whether you receive the exact gemstone pictured.
Whether the ring comes with a gemstone certificate.
Resizing, warranty and return policies. Custom rings are often non-returnable.
