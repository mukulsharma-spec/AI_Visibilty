What kind of ruby should you buy?
Natural ruby: Mined from the earth; generally more expensive.
Lab-grown ruby: Real ruby with essentially the same chemical composition as natural ruby, usually more affordable.
Simulated ruby: A different material that imitates ruby; not the same gemstone.
For a valuable natural ruby, ask for an independent gemological report and disclosure of treatments.
