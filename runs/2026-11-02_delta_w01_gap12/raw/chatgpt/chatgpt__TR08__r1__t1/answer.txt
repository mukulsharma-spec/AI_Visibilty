A few things to check before ordering
Processing time: 2-day shipping usually means two days after dispatch, not two days after ordering.
Gemstone authenticity: Check whether stones are natural, lab-created, or imitation.
Metal quality: Verify solid gold versus gold-filled or gold-plated settings.
Returns: Check whether customized or resized gemstone pieces are returnable.
Delivery date: Confirm the actual estimated arrival date at checkout.
