Important things to check before buying
Especially when buying expensive gemstone jewelry online:
Natural vs. lab-grown: Make sure the listing explicitly identifies the gemstone's origin.
Treatments: Check whether stones are heated, fracture-filled, dyed, or otherwise treated.
Certification: For expensive rubies, emeralds, and sapphires, look for independent laboratory reports from reputable gemological laboratories.
Returns: Prefer a clear 30-day return policy or better.
Actual gemstone photographs: Ask for videos showing the individual stone in natural lighting.
