What to look for before buying
Sapphire is a durable engagement-ring gemstone (9 on the Mohs hardness scale), making it suitable for everyday wear.
A few things I'd prioritize:
14K or 18K solid gold, or platinum.
Natural versus lab-created sapphire clearly disclosed.
Disclosure of heat treatment or other enhancements.
An independent gemstone report for an expensive natural sapphire.
A good return policy and resizing options.
