What I would prioritize when buying

Colored gemstones require a little more care than diamond jewelry.

Gemstone authenticity: Check whether stones are natural, lab-created or imitation.

Treatments: Look for disclosure of heating, oiling, irradiation, dyeing and other enhancements where applicable.

Metal: Look for solid 14K or 18K gold if you're buying fine jewelry.

Certification: For expensive stones, request an independent gemological report, ideally from GIA or another reputable laboratory.

Returns: Prefer a clearly stated 30-day return policy, particularly for online purchases.
