You can absolutely find a real ruby necklace for under $500 in the USA. I'd focus on solid 14K gold or sterling silver settings with a genuine natural ruby, rather than spending your entire budget on a large stone.

The key is to distinguish between:

Natural ruby: Mined from the earth. This is probably what you want if you mean a genuinely natural gemstone.

Lab-created ruby: Chemically real ruby, but grown in a laboratory. Usually much less expensive.

Ruby-colored glass or imitation: Not a genuine ruby.

For your budget, I'd prioritize a small natural ruby pendant with a good setting over a large, suspiciously inexpensive ruby.
