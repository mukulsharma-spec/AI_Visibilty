Without knowing your budget, I’d lean toward Angara if the gemstone itself matters to you, and Kay if convenience and price matter more. Angara is online-only and focuses on colored gemstones. You can usually choose the stone quality grade, metal, and size, which makes it good for a more personal piece. Their stones tend to be better quality, but prices run higher, and customized orders can take a week or two to ship. Kay is a mall chain with stores nationwide, so you can see the necklace in person, get help from a salesperson, and handle returns or resizing locally. It also offers financing and frequent sales. Birthstone pieces there are often more standard designs, and lower-priced ones may use lab-created stones, so check the product details. Kay’s “sale” prices are also often marked down from inflated list prices, so compare the final price rather than the discount. If she’d like a mother’s necklace with several children’s birthstones, both carry them, but Angara gives you more control over stone quality and layout. Since it’s late October, you have time to wait for the Black Friday sales, which both retailers usually run, and to order well ahead of Mother’s Day (May 9, 2027) in case a custom piece needs extra time.
