Why this isn’t an exact comparison:

- Stone size: Angara’s example has a larger stone (10x8mm) than Brilliant Earth’s 8x6mm for a similar or lower price.
- Metal: Brilliant Earth’s examples are 18K or platinum, while Angara’s is 14K, which accounts for some of the gap.
- Stone quality: Brilliant Earth describes its center stones as hand-selected and eye clean. Angara’s listings say “lab-grown quality,” and at least one lists heated and irradiated, AAA grade. Compare clarity and treatment details on the specific stones you’re considering.
