4. Smart Shopping Tips to Lower the Cost Further:

Choose Lab-Grown or Simulated Sapphires: Lab-created sapphires are chemically and physically identical to mined natural sapphires, but cost significantly less. If you are looking at white sapphires (often used as a diamond alternative), they are much cheaper than diamonds while retaining great brilliance.

Opt for Sterling Silver or 10k Gold: Blue Nile predominantly sells fine pieces in 14k/18k gold or platinum. Choosing the exact same stone style set in rhodium-plated sterling silver or 10k gold will slash the price immediately while still giving you that continuous line of sparkle.
