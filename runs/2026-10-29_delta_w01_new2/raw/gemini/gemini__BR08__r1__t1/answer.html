Yes, both Angara and Brilliant Earth sell lab-grown sapphires.

Which one is cheaper?

Angara is generally the more affordable option.

  • Angara typically offers lower entry-level pricing for lab-grown gemstone jewelry, and they frequently run promotional discounts and sales that apply to their lab-created colored gemstone inventory.

  • Brilliant Earth positions itself more as a high-end, eco-conscious luxury jeweler. Their loose lab-grown sapphires and finished settings tend to have a higher markup, with individual loose lab-grown sapphire stones typically starting around $400 to $900+ depending on the size (whereas Angara often packages competitive rates into ready-to-set or pre-designed ring mountings).

Shopping Tips for the USA:

  • Customization: Both websites allow you to pick your lab-grown sapphire shape (oval, round, emerald, cushion, etc.) and pair it with various precious metals (14k/18k gold, platinum).

  • Discounts: If you choose either retailer, make sure to check for first-time buyer newsletter coupons or seasonal promotional codes, as both sites frequently offer sign-up discounts or complimentary gifts with purchase.
