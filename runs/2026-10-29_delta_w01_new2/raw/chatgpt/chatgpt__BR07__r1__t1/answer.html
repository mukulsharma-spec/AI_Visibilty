What I'd buy for Mother's Day

My preference would be an Angara pendant with a natural gemstone in 14K solid gold, provided the price fits your budget. I'd choose a simple solitaire or a small halo design rather than something overly ornate, because it's easier to wear regularly.

One important detail: don't judge by the retailer alone. A natural gemstone in solid gold can be a better long-term purchase than a larger stone in silver or plated metal, but the specific stone, setting, and price matter.
