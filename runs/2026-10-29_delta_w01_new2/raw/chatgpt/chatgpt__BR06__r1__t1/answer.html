What I would buy for under $1,000

I'd prioritize these specifications rather than chasing the biggest sapphire:

Metal: 14K solid gold if possible; sterling silver if you want to allocate more of the budget to the gemstone.

Stone: Natural sapphire if origin matters to you, or lab-grown sapphire if you want a larger, vivid stone for less.

Treatment: Heat-treated is a common, accepted choice. Ask before buying if you want an untreated stone.

Setting: A simple solitaire or a modest halo. More elaborate diamond accents can consume your budget.

Returns: Confirm the exact ring is eligible, especially if it is custom-made or resized.

One important point: Kay offers a lifetime gemstone guarantee on eligible sapphire jewelry, including stone replacement under specified conditions, provided you complete documented inspections every six months. That's a meaningful advantage if you plan to wear your ring regularly.
