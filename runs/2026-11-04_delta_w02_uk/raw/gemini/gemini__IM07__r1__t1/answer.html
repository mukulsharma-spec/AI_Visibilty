Shopping for opal stud earrings in the UK offers a wonderful variety of styles, from dainty everyday sterling silver pieces to luxurious gold and gemstone-accented designs. Because opals are the October birthstone, they are widely available across high street stores, independent boutiques, and online marketplaces.

Here is a guide to some of the best places to look, popular styles, and price ranges available in the UK:

Top UK Shopping Options

High Street & Department Stores:

John Lewis & Partners: Great for reliable quality, featuring contemporary designs like 9ct gold halo teardrops and titanium opal flower studs (typically ranging from £50 to £300).

TJC (The Jeweller’s Channel): One of the largest selections of Ethiopian and Australian opal studs online in the UK, frequently running sales on sterling silver and gold-vermeil halo or solitaire designs (£20–£50).

Independent & Artisan Makers (Etsy UK / Small Boutiques):

Ideal if you want handmade, ethical, or custom-sized options (such as tiny 2mm or 3mm stacking studs). Many UK-based sellers offer fast shipping and gift packaging.

Sparkle by Faith: Excellent for consciously crafted, eco-friendly sterling silver studs featuring ethically mined Australian opals (~£45).

Traditional Independent Jewellers:

Retailers like Sarah Beth Jewellers offer classic real opal oval studs set in yellow gold, complete with scroll-edge or claw-set detailing if you are looking for timeless, heirloom-quality pieces.

Tips for Buying Opals

Setting: Because opals have a softer composition compared to diamonds or sapphires (ranking about 5.5 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale), look for bezel settings if you want extra protection for daily wear, or secure prong settings to maximize how much light hits the stone's "fire."

Type of Opal: Australian opals are prized for their durability and distinct play-of-color, Ethiopian (Welo) opals are famous for their high-domed, crystal-clear hydrophan transparency, and lab-created/simulated opals offer brilliant color flashes at a budget-friendly price point.
