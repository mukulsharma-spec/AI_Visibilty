Whether you are looking for classic diamonds, colourful precious stones (like sapphires, rubies, or emeralds), or budget-friendly lab-grown options, the UK has a diverse range of high street jewellers, independent specialists, and online boutiques.

Depending on your budget and preferred shopping style, here are some of the best places in the UK to find a gemstone tennis bracelet:

1. High Street & Department Stores (Reliable & Accessible)

Ernest Jones: A well-known fixture on UK high streets and online, Ernest Jones offers a dedicated selection of tennis bracelets ranging from traditional diamonds to vibrant colored gemstone styles. It’s a great option if you want to view pieces in person and get standard warranties.

T.H. Baker: An established independent British family jeweller with multiple stores and a strong online presence. They carry a variety of fine tennis bracelets featuring precious stones and diamonds.

2. Fine Jewellery Districts (Best for Variety & Value)

Hatton Garden (London): If you are in or near London, visiting Hatton Garden—the UK’s jewellery hub—is hard to beat. Dozens of independent specialists (such as Amada Diamonds or Regal Hatton Garden) hold massive stocks of tennis bracelets. Buying here often gives you room to compare prices, examine stones up close, and choose between natural or lab-grown gemstones.

3. Online Specialists & Value Retailers (Best for Budget & Choice)

TJC (The Jewellery Channel): If you are looking for a colorful gemstone tennis bracelet (such as multi-gemstone styles, Tanzanite, or emeralds) without breaking the bank, TJC has an extensive online collection of gold and silver gemstone tennis bracelets at competitive price points.

Carat London:* Known globally and based in the UK, they specialise in high-end lab-grown stones and alternative fine jewellery. They offer gorgeous, contemporary tennis and slider bracelets set in sterling silver or 18k gold vermeil, making them a fantastic mid-range option.

4. Luxury & Designer Houses (Best for Investment & Splurge)

Tiffany & Co. (Boutiques across the UK): If you are looking for timeless, ultra-luxury craftsmanship and don't mind a higher price tag, Tiffany’s UK locations offer exquisite, heirloom-quality tennis bracelets.

Tips Before You Buy:

Setting Matters: Gemstones other than diamonds (like emeralds or tanzanite) can be softer. Look for secure settings like prong (claw) settings for maximum light play, or bezel settings if you want extra protection for everyday wear.

Clasp Security: A tennis bracelet is flexible and prone to catching. Always check that the clasp has a safety latch or figure-eight safety catch so it doesn’t slip off your wrist unnoticed.
