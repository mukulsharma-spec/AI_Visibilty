With a budget of $500 (roughly £390 to £400 GBP), you have a very healthy budget to find a gorgeous, real, mined ruby necklace set in solid gold or silver. Because rubies are precious gemstones, sticking to smaller stone sizes, 9-carat gold, or sterling silver will keep you comfortably under budget while ensuring authenticity.

Here are the best places to look in the UK:

1. Online UK Fine Jewellers (Best Balance of Price and Quality)

Gemondo: Known for ethically sourced genuine gemstones, Gemondo offers stunning real ruby and diamond cluster pendants set in 9ct yellow or white gold typically ranging from £130 to £250. They frequently feature classic vintage-inspired or floral designs.

Diamonds Factory UK: They feature customisable pendant sections where you can select real rubies paired with small accent diamonds. Many of their simpler 4-prong solitaire or bezel-set ruby pendants in 9ct gold start around £250 to £400.

Ruby & Oscar: A UK-based online jeweller dedicated heavily to ruby pieces. They offer a wide array of solid 9ct gold and sterling silver ruby necklaces ranging from under £200 up to £485 for their "Legacy Simplicity" or "Petite Infatuation" 9ct gold lines.

2. High Street Mainstays

Goldsmiths / Ernest Jones / Beaverbrooks: These are premier high street names across the UK. While many of their larger pieces stretch far past £500, they routinely stock 9ct white or yellow gold ruby and diamond birthstone pendants or delicate halo necklaces right in the £300 to £450 sweet spot. Shopping with them gives you the security of easy in-store returns and robust customer service.

3. Antique & Vintage Dealers (For Unique Character)

If you want something with history, look at UK antique specialists or platforms like Etsy (filtered to UK-based sellers) or regional auction houses.

Victorian and Edwardian jewellery often featured smaller, untreated rubies paired with seed pearls or old-cut diamonds. You can frequently find authentic vintage 9ct or 15ct gold ruby pendants in the £250–£450 range that offer incredible craftsmanship you won't find in modern mass-produced pieces.

Quick Shopping Tips for Rubies:

Check the Metal: Ensure you are buying solid gold (9ct is standard and durable for a budget) or sterling silver, rather than gold-plated or vermeil, so your necklace lasts a lifetime.

Natural vs. Lab-Created: At a £400/$500 budget, you can easily afford natural, earth-mined rubies (though they will likely be smaller carats or commercially heated, which is standard practice). Just double-check product descriptions to ensure they aren't selling synthetic/lab-created glass-filled rubies at inflated natural prices.
