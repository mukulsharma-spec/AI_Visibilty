When shopping for fine jewelry online in the UK, transparency regarding gemstone treatments (such as heat treatment in sapphires/rubies or oiling/resin filling in emeralds) is a key indicator of a reputable jeweller. Legitimate, high-standard online jewellers will explicitly state whether a gemstone is untreated ("no heat") or enhanced, often backing this up with independent grading certificates (like GIA, IGI, or anchor lab reports).

Key Questions to Ask Any UK Online Jeweler Before Buying:

Even if a jeweler has a strong reputation, always protect your investment by asking these direct questions:

Is the gemstone treated in any way? (e.g., Is this ruby composite/glass-filled, or is this sapphire heat-only?) Note: Glass-filled rubies require extreme care and are worth significantly less than heat-treated or unheated stones.

Does the gemstone come with an independent lab certificate? Look for certificates from globally recognized bodies like the GIA (Gemological Institute of America), AGL, or SSEF/Gübelin for high-value stones, as these reports explicitly note thermal treatments, clarity enhancements (oiling/resins), and geographic origin.

What is your return policy if an independent appraisal reveals undisclosed treatments? Reputable sellers will always allow returns if a stone's condition or treatment status was misrepresented.
