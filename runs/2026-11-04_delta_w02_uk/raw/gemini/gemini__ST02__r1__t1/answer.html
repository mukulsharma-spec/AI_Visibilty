You can find a great variety of peridot earrings online in the UK, ranging from affordable sterling silver everyday wear to fine gold and gemstone pieces.

Depending on your budget and style preference, here are some of the best UK online stores to check out:

1. Department Stores & Multi-Brand Retailers

John Lewis & Partners: A reliable option carrying a curated range of peridot earrings. They feature popular contemporary jewelry brands like Monica Vinader (known for birthstone huggies), Missoma, and classic fine gold/silver pieces from makers like E.W Adams.

TJC (The Jewellery Channel): Great if you are looking for extensive variety at competitive price points. They stock hundreds of options including peridot studs, hoops, and drop earrings set in sterling silver, yellow gold vermeil, and platinum-plated settings.

2. Dedicated Gemstone & Fine Jewellery Specialists

Gemondo: A UK-based gemstone specialist offering a vibrant collection of peridot earrings set in genuine 9ct gold and sterling silver. They specialize in ethically sourced gemstones and feature classic studs, intricate drops, and vintage-inspired designs with free UK delivery and returns.

FineDiam: Handcrafted fine jewellery based out of London's Hatton Garden. They offer natural peridot stud, halo, and drop earrings set in solid gold and platinum, perfect if you are looking for a high-end heirloom piece.

3. Independent & Marketplace Options

Etsy UK: Excellent if you want something unique, handmade, or vintage. You can find independent British silversmiths and designers crafting custom peridot studs, raw stone earrings, and bohemian drop styles.

Not On The High Street (NOTHS): Features peridot birthstone earrings designed by small, independent UK creative businesses and boutique brands.

Tip: Because peridot is the birthstone for August, you'll often find special collections or promotional pushes around late summer, but most of these retailers stock peridot pieces year-round. What style of earrings are you leaning toward (e.g., subtle studs, huggie hoops, or statement drops)?
