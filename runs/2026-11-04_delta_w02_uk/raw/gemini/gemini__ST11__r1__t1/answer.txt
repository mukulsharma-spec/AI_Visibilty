Whether you are looking for an everyday silver piece, a classic gold and diamond halo design, or a vintage-inspired heirloom, there are plenty of great options to buy a ruby pendant necklace in the UK.

The best places to shop depend on your budget and preference for high street stores, specialist online jewellers, or independent boutiques:

1. UK High Street Jewellers

If you want to view pieces in person or prefer the security of a well-known national retailer with physical stores:

H.Samuel & Ernest Jones: Both are nationwide high-street staples offering a variety of ruby and diamond pendant necklaces, typically set in yellow gold, white gold, or sterling silver. They frequently run online and in-store sales.

T.H. Baker: An established family jeweller with brick-and-mortar stores across parts of the UK as well as a strong online shop, featuring a dedicated selection of ruby rings, earrings, and necklaces.

2. Specialist Online Fine Jewellers

For a wider curated selection of gemstone-specific fine jewellery with free UK delivery:

Gemondo: A trusted UK online destination specialising in authentic gemstone and birthstone jewellery. They offer lovely vintage- and art deco-inspired ruby necklaces set in ethical gold and silver.

TJC (The Jewellery Channel): Great if you are shopping on a budget. They offer a vast selection of ruby pendants, chains, and chokers ranging from affordable silver pieces to luxury gold-set genuine rubies.

Lloyds Family Jewellery & FineDiam: Excellent online UK jewellers providing a variety of natural and lab-grown ruby pendants, often paired with diamond halos in 9k or 18k gold.

3. Independent Boutiques & Designer Jewellers

If you are looking for something unique, high-end, or artisan-crafted:

New Cavendish Jewellers (London): Located on Marylebone High Street, they offer a stunning collection of natural ruby pendants if you prefer a bespoke or high-end luxury shopping experience in person.

Hatton Garden (London) or local quarters: If you live near or can travel to London, Hatton Garden features dozens of independent Hatton Garden jewellers where you can hand-select loose rubies and have a custom pendant necklace made to your exact specifications.

Quick Tips for Buying a Ruby:

Check the Metal: Decide whether you prefer yellow gold (which warms up the rich red tone of the ruby), white gold or platinum (which offers a striking modern contrast), or sterling silver for a more budget-friendly option.

Look for Hallmarking: Reputable UK jewellers will always sell pieces that carry an official UK hallmark stamped by an assay office (London, Birmingham, Sheffield, or Edinburgh), guaranteeing the purity of the precious metal.
