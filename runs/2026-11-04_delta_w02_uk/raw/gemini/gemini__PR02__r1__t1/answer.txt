Shopping for real gemstone earrings in the UK with a budget under $300 (roughly £230–£240 GBP based on current exchange rates) gives you access to a fantastic selection of independent British jewellery brands and online boutiques.

Depending on whether you want genuine precious/semi-precious stones set in sterling silver, gold vermeil, or even solid 9ct gold, several great UK destinations fit your budget:

1. Gemondo

Best for: Authentic, hallmarked real gemstones set in solid 9ct gold or sterling silver.

Why shop here: Gemondo specializes explicitly in genuine natural gemstones (everything from classic sapphires, rubies, and emeralds to tanzanite, morganite, and opal). A massive portion of their extensive earring collection sits comfortably under the £200–£250 mark. You can easily find solid 9ct gold styles (like opal, garnet, or peridot studs and drop earrings) well within your $300 limit.

Price Range: Mostly £50 to £280.

2. Carrie Elizabeth Jewellery

Best for: Trendy, celestial, and vintage-inspired demi-fine gemstone pieces.

Why shop here: Based in London, Carrie Elizabeth offers gorgeous rings, necklaces, and earrings featuring real semi-precious stones like green amethyst, aquamarine, smoky quartz, and opals. Most of their pieces are crafted from sterling silver or 14k gold vermeil. Their statement drop hoops and gemstone studs are usually priced between £50 and £160, leaving you plenty of room under your budget.

Price Range: £50 to £200.

3. Astrid & Miyu

Best for: Contemporary, stackable, and modern everyday earrings.

Why shop here: While widely known for sleek hoops and ear cuffs, they frequently feature collections incorporating birthstones and real semi-precious stones (such as turquoise, opal, and quartz) set in recycled sterling silver and 14k gold plating. They lean heavily into modern, minimalist designs.

Price Range: £40 to £150.

4. Missoma

Best for: Architectural, gold-plated demi-fine everyday wear.

Why shop here: A staple of modern British jewellery, Missoma is famous for its semi-precious stone drops, huggies, and charm earrings (featuring stones like malachite, lapis lazuli, rhodochrosite, and pearl). Their pieces are high quality, highly stylish, and sit nicely within a sub-$300 budget.

Price Range: £60 to £180.

5. Department Stores (Liberty London, Selfridges, or John Lewis)

Best for: Curated multi-brand browsing.

Why shop here: If you prefer to browse a physical store or want a mix of designer diffusion brands, major UK department stores carry contemporary jewellery lines featuring real gemstones (such as Scream Pretty or Astrid & Miyu concessions) that hover right around the £100–£200 mark.

💡 Quick Shopping Tips for the UK Market:

Check the Metal: At this price point, items under £100 will typically be Sterling Silver or Gold Vermeil (thick gold plating over sterling silver). If you specifically want solid gold (like 9ct yellow or white gold) paired with real gemstones, look closely at brands like Gemondo, which frequently offer solid 9ct gold options under £250.

Shipping & Duties: Since you are shopping in the UK, ordering from UK-based sites means you won't have to worry about international customs fees or unexpected import VAT.
