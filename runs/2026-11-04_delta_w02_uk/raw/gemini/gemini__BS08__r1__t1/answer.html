Shopping for aquamarine halo stud earrings in the UK gives you a great mix of options, ranging from online fine jewellers with customisable metals to high street staples and independent boutiques.

Depending on your budget and whether you want real diamonds surrounding the aquamarine or a more budget-friendly halo (like moissanite or white sapphire), here are the best places to look:

1. Dedicated Fine Jewellers & Online Specialists

Angara (UK): Excellent for gemstone jewellery, offering dedicated round, oval, or cushion-cut aquamarine halo studs where you can choose your metal type (white gold, yellow gold, platinum) and gemstone quality grade. They frequently run online discounts and ship with a certificate of authenticity.

The Diamond Store: A trusted UK-based online fine jeweller that features lovely precious gemstone and diamond pieces. They offer UK-hallmarked aquamarine earrings, often paired with diamond halos set in white or yellow gold.

Diamonds Factory UK: Known for bespoke craftsmanship, they carry elegant aquamarine and diamond halo arrangements with options to select your exact gold karat weight and stone specifications.

2. High Street & Independent British Jewellers

H.Samuel / Ernest Jones: Great traditional high street options if you prefer browsing in-store or want reliable customer service and easy returns. They routinely stock birthstone collections featuring aquamarine (March's birthstone).

Independent Hatton Garden & Regional Jewellers: If you are near London, Hatton Garden features independent shops like Finediam that specialize in custom gemstone and diamond halo clusters. Across the UK, high-end regional independents (such as Fogal & Barnes or local jewellers in the Burlington Arcade) often carry or can custom-craft oval or round aquamarine halo studs in 9ct or 18ct gold.

3. Budget-Friendly & Artisan Choices

TJC (The Trading Jewelers): If you are looking for something more budget-conscious (such as aquamarine paired with sparkling white topazes or cubic zirconia halos in sterling silver or yellow gold vermeil), TJC has a massive, rotating collection of affordable gemstone jewellery.

Etsy (UK Makers): If you like vintage, art deco, or uniquely handcrafted styles, searching for UK-based independent sellers on Etsy will yield stunning natural aquamarine halo studs (often paired with moissanite or conflict-free accents) usually set in recycled sterling silver or gold.

Tips Before You Buy:

Check the Metal: If you plan to wear them every day, look for 14ct/18ct gold or platinum to avoid skin sensitivities and ensure longevity. Sterling silver is great for a lower budget, but requires more frequent cleaning to prevent tarnishing.

Halos Matter: Ensure you check what stones make up the "halo"—some use genuine diamonds, while others use more affordable alternatives like moissanite or white sapphires.
