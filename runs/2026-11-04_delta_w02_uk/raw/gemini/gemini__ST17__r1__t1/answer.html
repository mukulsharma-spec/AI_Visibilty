There are several reputable online options to buy emerald earrings in the UK, ranging from high-street names to fine jewellery specialists and bespoke creators:

1. High Street & Fine Jewellery Retailers

Ernest Jones: A well-known UK high-street jeweller offering a polished online collection of emerald earrings, typically featuring classic emerald-and-diamond clusters, yellow or white gold studs, and drop styles.

W Hamond: A long-standing British gemstone specialist with online shopping (and physical stores in Yorkshire). They feature exquisite, high-end fine jewellery, including 9ct and 18ct gold emerald and diamond cluster and stud earrings.

2. Specialist & Luxury Independent Jewelers

The Emerald Shop: Located in London’s famous Hatton Garden, they specialize in luxury emerald jewellery and offer online browsing alongside virtual consultations if you want something handcrafted or custom-designed.

The Fine Jewellery Company: A UK-based online fine jewellery retailer that features an array of pre-owned, vintage, and contemporary emerald earrings, often beautifully presented with free fully insured UK delivery.

3. Customizable & Mid-to-High Range Options

Angara UK: Great if you want to customize your jewellery. Their online store lets you pick the stone quality grade, carat size, and metal type (such as platinum, white gold, or yellow gold) for emerald studs, drops, and hoops.

TJC (The Jewellery Channel): A budget-friendly option if you are looking for silver or lower-carat gold emerald earrings, birthstone pieces, or more accessible everyday styles with frequent online discounts.

Tips for buying online in the UK:

Check for hallmarking (UK law requires precious metal items above a certain weight to be hallmarked by a UK assay office).

Look closely at the return policy, especially for high-value gemstone purchases, to ensure you can exchange or return them if the shade of green doesn't suit you in person.

Are you looking for a specific style (like subtle studs, dramatic drops, or hoops) or a particular metal type (like yellow gold or white gold)?
