Tips for Buying a Tanzanite Engagement Ring Online:

Check the Setting: Because tanzanite can scratch or chip more easily than a diamond, look for settings that protect the girdle (edges) of the stone—such as halo settings (surrounded by smaller diamonds) or bezel settings, rather than high, exposed prongs, especially if it's meant for daily wear as an engagement ring.

Look for Grading: Reputable sellers will tell you the color grade, clarity, and carat weight. Deep, vivid blues mixed with rich violet undertones command higher value than pale, washed-out stones.

UK Hallmarking: Ensure that any gold or platinum ring you purchase online complies with UK hallmarking laws legally required by the Birmingham, London, or Edinburgh Assay Offices.
