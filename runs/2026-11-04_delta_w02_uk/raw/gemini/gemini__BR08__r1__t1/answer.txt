Yes, both Angara and Brilliant Earth sell lab-grown sapphires. Both brands cater to customers in the UK, offering localized websites with prices typically displayed in British Pounds (GBP).

Which one is cheaper?

Generally speaking, Angara tends to be the more budget-friendly option, especially when looking at finished pieces of jewellery (like rings, earrings, and pendants).

A breakdown of how their pricing structures compare highlights these differences:

Angara: They specialize heavily in coloured gemstones and manufacture their jewellery to order. They frequently run deep discount sales and promotional codes (often 10% to 15% off), and their entry-level lab-grown sapphire jewellery starts at a lower price point because they offer smaller stone carat weights paired with varying qualities of accent lab-diamonds.

Brilliant Earth: Known more for their ethical diamond sourcing, Brilliant Earth operates on a slightly different model where you often choose a loose gemstone (specifying exact dimensions and carat) and pair it with a setting. Their lab-created coloured gemstone collection tends to carry a higher designer markup, making their overall configuration generally more expensive than pre-set or customizable options at Angara.

Summary Tips for Shopping in the UK:

Check for duties and taxes: Both brands ship to the UK, but make sure to check whether UK VAT and import duties are already included in the listed online price or if they will be added at checkout.

Customization: If you want a specific loose lab-grown sapphire to put into a bespoke setting, Brilliant Earth has an extensive loose-gemstone search tool. If you want a finished ring style (like a halo or three-stone sapphire ring) with frequent promotional discounts, Angara usually delivers better value for money.
