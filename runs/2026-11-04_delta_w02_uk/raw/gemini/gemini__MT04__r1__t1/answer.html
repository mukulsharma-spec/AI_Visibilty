For a May birthday, your mom’s traditional birthstone is the emerald—a gorgeous, vibrant green gemstone that symbolises love, wisdom, and vitality.

Shopping for emerald jewelry in the UK offers everything from high-street household names to independent artisan pieces. Depending on your budget and her style, here is a guide to the best places and types of jewelry to look for:

1. High-Street & Mid-Range Jewellers (Great for Quality & Hallmarking)

If you want something classic, reliable, and hallmarked to UK standards with good customer service, these national jewellers have dedicated May birthstone and emerald collections:

Beaverbrooks: A staple on UK high streets. They carry lovely pieces ranging from 9ct yellow and white gold emerald-and-diamond pendant necklaces to stunning hoop or halo stud earrings (often paired with diamonds).

The Diamond Store: Excellent online option if you are looking for variety. They offer both natural emeralds and more budget-friendly lab-grown emerald options set in silver, 9K gold, or 18K gold vermeil (with pieces like rings and necklaces often starting around £150–£300).

T.H. Baker / Ernest Jones: Both traditional UK chains feature beautiful selections of emerald cluster rings, teardrop pendants, and tennis bracelets that make standout milestone gifts.

2. Contemporary & Designer UK Brands

If your mom prefers something a bit more modern, unique, or daintily styled:

Kit Heath: Known for contemporary British silver design. They feature specific birthstone lines (like their Céleste birthstone collections) which include delicate emerald-style crystal or gemstone ball chains and pendants.

Vianne Jewellery / Astley Clarke: Great for modern demi-fine jewelry if she loves layered necklaces, green chalcedony, or vibrant emerald-accented stackable rings.

3. Handcrafted & Personalised (Etsy UK)

If you want something personal, artisan-made, or more budget-conscious:

Searching Etsy UK for "May birthstone jewelry for mom" yields thousands of independent UK-based makers. You can find:

Dainty birthstone drop necklaces featuring a single pear-cut or round emerald.

Personalized initial-and-birthstone charm necklaces.

Handmade sterling silver or 14K gold-filled minimalist bracelets featuring raw or faceted Zambian emeralds.

Ideas by Jewelry Type

Necklaces & Pendants: Safe if you aren't sure of her ring size. A simple pear-cut emerald pendant surrounded by a small diamond halo, or an infinity-symbol gold necklace, looks timeless on moms.

Earrings: Classic emerald stud earrings or small gold huggie hoops with emerald drops add a subtle, everyday pop of green colour.

Rings: If buying a ring, note that emeralds are a slightly softer stone than diamonds (ranking 7.5–8 on the Mohs scale), meaning bezel settings or halo settings (where the stone is protected by surrounding metal or small diamonds) are best for daily wear.
