If you are shopping in the UK for a genuine Akoya pearl strand necklace online, you have several reputable options ranging from dedicated pearl specialists to luxury fine jewellers.

1. Dedicated Pearl Specialists (Great for Variety and Quality Assurance)

Pearls.co.uk: A long-established UK-based specialist offering hand-selected single and multi-strand Akoya pearl necklaces. They provide detailed specifications on pearl size, overtone, and clasp material (often with options like sterling silver or gold) alongside UK shipping.

PearlsOnly UK: A major online pearl retailer with a dedicated UK storefront. They carry a wide array of Japanese Akoya strand necklaces ranging from budget-friendlier grades to high-luster AAA/AAAA options. Purchases typically include a certificate of authenticity and appraisal.

The Pearl Source UK: An internationally recognized pearl company with a localized UK site. They feature extensive selections of single, double, and triple-strand Akoya pearl necklaces direct from pearl farms, offering customizable lengths and clasp metals.

2. High-End & Luxury UK Jewellers (Investment Pieces)

Yoko London: A globally renowned luxury pearl brand based in the UK. They specialize in exquisite, high-end Japanese Akoya pearl jewelry, set in 18k gold. If you are looking for a top-tier heirloom piece (AAA grades paired with exceptional design), their online boutique offers complimentary UK delivery and luxury packaging.

Mikimoto (UK Online Store): As the creators of cultured pearls, Mikimoto is the ultimate name in Akoya pearls. Their UK online boutique and flagship services offer pristine, perfectly matched Japanese Akoya pearl strands, though they sit at the absolute luxury price point.

3. Fine Jewelry Retailers

Angara UK: An online fine jeweler with a dedicated UK site that allows you to build and customize your own jewelry. They feature a robust selection of Japanese Akoya pearl strand necklaces and matinee lengths where you can often select your preferred pearl quality grade, length (e.g., 16-inch to 22-inch), and gold type.

Tips for Buying Akoya Strands Online in the UK:

Check the Return Policy: Fine pearls are a significant investment. Ensure the retailer offers a clear, hassle-free 14-to-30-day return window in case the length or pearl overtone doesn't match your expectations in person.

Look for Certification: Reputable sellers will state the exact millimeter size (e.g., 6-6.5mm or 7.5-8mm), clasp metal (14k/18k gold or sterling silver), and often supply a certificate of authenticity.

Import Duties & VAT: If ordering from international sites that ship to the UK, double-check whether UK VAT and customs duties are included in the listed online price to avoid unexpected courier fees upon delivery.
