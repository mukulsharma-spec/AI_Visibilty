Key Buying Tips for the UK Market:

UK Hallmarking: Ensure any piece you purchase carries a legal UK hallmark verifying the gold purity (e.g., 375 for 9ct, 750 for 18ct). Reputable jewellers like those in Hatton Garden or established online stores will always provide this.

Sapphire Treatment: Natural blue sapphires are routinely heat-treated to enhance their colour and clarity (which is standard practice in the industry). Always check the product specifications or ask the jeweller if the stone is heat-treated or unheated.

Certifications: For larger center stones, check if a gemmological certificate (such as GIA or IGI) is included to verify the sapphire's origin and quality.
