Congratulations on your upcoming 45th wedding anniversary! A sapphire is a magnificent and meaningful choice to mark such a major milestone.

Depending on whether you want to buy a ready-made piece, go the bespoke route, or browse historic houses, the UK has fantastic options for shopping.

1. Hatton Garden (London) – For Choice and Independent Experts

If you can make it to London, Hatton Garden is the historic jewellery quarter and home to dozens of independent gem dealers and bespoke jewellers.

Flawless Fine Jewellery or Apsara (specialists in fine, unheated coloured gemstones) are great places to explore.

Why shop here: You can look at loose stones under a loupe, compare different shades of blue (or even pink/yellow sapphires), and have a custom setting made specifically for her.

2. High-End Independent Family Jewellers

If you prefer a more personal, high-street boutique experience outside of London, look for accredited independent family-run jewellers:

C W Sellors: A brilliant UK-wide independent luxury jeweller with decades of heritage, known for fine precious gemstone collections.

Titcombe Bespoke Jewellery: Based in the South West and the Cotswolds (Bristol and Cirencester), they are well-loved for exceptional craftsmanship and carrying a gorgeous selection of loose natural sapphires that they can set into custom pieces.

3. Luxury Heritage Houses (London & Major Cities)

If you want to pull out all the stops and buy from a storied British institution with royal associations:

Garrard: The longest-serving Crown Jeweller in the world, famous for extraordinary sapphire and diamond heritage pieces (reminiscent of famous royal sapphire rings).

Why shop here: Ultimate luxury, historic craftsmanship, and an unforgettable retail experience.

4. High Street & Department Stores

If you are looking for something accessible, straightforward, and ready to wear immediately:

Ernest Jones: A reliable nationwide high-street jeweller with a dedicated collection of sapphire and diamond rings, necklaces, and earrings.

Independent local jewellers on your local high street often source high-quality pre-owned vintage sapphire pieces, which can offer incredible character and value for a 45th anniversary.

Tips for Buying a Sapphire:

Color & Quality: Sapphires don't just come in deep blue—they also come in teal, pink, yellow, and peach. Consider her personal style.

Treatments: Ask if the stone is natural and whether it has been heat-treated (which is standard and stable) or is unheated (rarer and more collectible).

Durability: Sapphires rate a 9 on the Mohs scale of hardness (second only to diamonds), meaning they are durable enough for daily wear if you are buying a ring.
