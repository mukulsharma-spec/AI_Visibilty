Tips for Buying an Emerald-Cut Sapphire Online:

Pay attention to clarity: Unlike round-cut stones which hide inclusions well, the open, step-cut facets of an emerald-cut sapphire make any internal flaws (inclusions) much more visible. Look for "Eye-Clean" stones.

Check the Side Diamond Specs: When buying a ring with side diamonds, check the product details to ensure the colour and clarity of the side diamonds (ideally G-H color, VS-SI clarity) complement the deep blue of the sapphire.

UK Hallmarking: Ensure the online store explicitly states that the piece will feature a official UK hallmark from an Assay Office (London, Birmingham, or Edinburgh), guaranteeing the purity of the gold or platinum.
