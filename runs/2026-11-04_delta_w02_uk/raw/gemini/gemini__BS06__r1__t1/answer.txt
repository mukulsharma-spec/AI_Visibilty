Shopping for a three-stone (or trilogy) sapphire and diamond ring in the UK gives you a great mix of options, ranging from modern build-your-own online jewellers to high-end Hatton Garden specialists and antique/vintage dealers.

The best places to look depend on your preferred shopping style:

1. Dedicated Online & High Street Jewellers (Customisable & Made-to-Order)

These retailers allow you to pick your metal (platinum, white/yellow/rose gold), choose the shape of the sapphire (oval, round, emerald cut, etc.), and select your preferred side diamonds.

Austen & Blake: Known for wide customization options. They have showrooms in various UK cities (like London, Manchester, Birmingham, Belfast) and a comprehensive online platform where you can build your own trilogy ring.

Diamonds Factory: Offers a large collection of pre-set and customisable sapphire trilogy and three-stone rings with physical showrooms in places like London, Birmingham, and Manchester.

Steven Stone: A prominent UK jeweler offering bespoke options and pre-made sapphire and diamond trilogy rings in various gold and platinum settings.

2. Fine & High-End Jewellers (Luxurious & Certified Stones)

If you are looking for investment-grade stones, GIA-certified sapphires, or high-end craftsmanship:

Hancocks Jewellers: Excellent for high-end, vintage-inspired, and opulent fine jewellery pieces, featuring stunning statement sapphire and diamond 3-stone rings.

Hatton Garden (London): If you are able to visit London, Hatton Garden is the UK’s jewellery hub with dozens of independent family-run shops and bespoke makers where you can view and compare loose sapphires and side diamonds side-by-side.

3. Antique & Pre-Owned Specialists (Unique & Vintage Character)

Three-stone sapphire rings (often reminiscent of Victorian, Edwardian, or Art Deco eras) look incredible with older-cut diamonds and deep, royal-blue natural sapphires.

Powells Jewellery: Specialises in fine pre-owned and antique pieces (including classic platinum sapphire and diamond dress/engagement trilogy rings).

Local Antique Dealers & Auction Houses: Keep an eye out for regional antique jewelry dealers or regional auction houses (like Fellows in Birmingham), which frequently list certified vintage trilogy rings at great value.

Tips Before Buying:

Check the Certification: Sapphires are often heat-treated to enhance color (which is standard practice), but you should always check whether the stone is natural or lab-created, and whether it comes with a gemological certificate (e.g., GIA or similar).

Durability: Sapphires rate a 9 on the Mohs scale of hardness (second only to diamonds), making a sapphire and diamond trilogy ring an exceptional, durable choice for everyday wear like an engagement ring.
