When shopping for ruby and diamond halo stud earrings in the UK, the "best" place depends on whether you prefer customisable online luxury, a high-street heritage jeweller, or an independent boutique experience.

Top options across the UK include the following trusted retailers:

1. Customisable & Made-to-Order Online Specialists

Austen & Blake: Known for bespoke and made-to-order fine jewellery. They allow you to customize your ruby and diamond halo (or cluster) earrings by selecting the metal type (white, yellow, rose gold, or platinum) and stone dimensions. They have showrooms in places like London, Nottingham, and Southampton if you want to view pieces in person.

FANCI Fine Jewellery: A top-rated independent fine jeweller (based in Southampton with an online store) that frequently carries exquisite ready-to-ship and customisable ruby and diamond halo earrings crafted in various gold purities and platinum.

2. High Street & Traditional Heritage Jewellers

T.H. Baker: A long-standing British family jeweller with a massive online presence and stores across the UK. They offer an extensive range of ruby and diamond cluster/halo stud earrings ranging from affordable 9ct gold pieces to high-end 18ct white gold and certified stones.

Beaverbrooks / Ernest Jones: As staple high street luxury jewellers across the UK, they regularly stock classic precious gemstone halo earrings. They are a great choice if you prefer to try earrings on in-store and want robust aftercare guarantees.

3. Independent Luxury Boutiques (London & Regional)

New Cavendish Jewellers (Marylebone, London): If you are in or near London, independent jewellers on historic streets like Marylebone High Street offer exceptional, high-end customer service. They carry stunning 18ct white and yellow gold ruby and diamond cluster/halo earrings with certified quality.

Hatton Garden (London): If you want to compare multiple independent diamond and gemstone specialists side-by-side, taking a trip to London's famous Hatton Garden allows you to commission or buy bespoke ruby halo studs directly from craftsmen and wholesalers.

Key Tips Before Buying:

Check the Metal: Rubies look striking against yellow gold (which warms up the red hue) or white gold/platinum (which makes the diamond halo pop and gives a modern look). Ensure the piece carries a clear UK hallmark.

Treatment & Origin: Natural rubies are frequently heat-treated to enhance their color and clarity, which is standard industry practice. Always check product descriptions to see if the rubies are natural or lab-grown, as lab-grown options will significantly lower the price point while offering identical physical properties.

Setting Type: For halo studs, look for claw/prong settings or micro-pavé to ensure maximum light enters both the central ruby and the surrounding diamonds.
