With a budget under $600 (roughly £450–£480), you have plenty of great options for real emeralds set in solid 9ct or 14ct gold, as well as sterling silver or gold vermeil pieces paired with diamonds.

The following UK-accessible brands and collections fit comfortably within this price range:

1. Gemondo UK

What you get: Exceptional variety of genuine emerald and diamond cluster styles set in 9ct yellow or white gold.

Price range: Most of their gold emerald studs, art-nouveau half-hoops, and delicate drops sit between £150 and £300 ($200–$400 USD).

Examples: Classic oval emerald & diamond cluster stud earrings or 9ct gold huggie hoops.

2. F. Hinds & Ernest Jones (High Street Favorites)

What you get: High-street names that offer secure shopping, easy returns, and physical UK brick-and-mortar stores if you want to inspect the stones in person.

Price range: F. Hinds has 9ct gold emerald and diamond swirl studs or double-cluster drop earrings running around £200 to £560 ($260–$730 USD, with many well under $600). Ernest Jones also features 9ct gold gemstone flower studs starting around £425 (~$550 USD).

3. Angara UK (Customizable Online)

What you get: While many of their large luxury pieces exceed $600, Angara allows you to scale down gemstone quality grades or choose smaller carat weights to hit a lower budget.

Price range: You can find entry-level solitaire emerald studs or smaller accent styles starting right around £300–£450 (~$390–$580 USD).

4. Independent British Designers & Etsy UK (Handmade / Solid Gold)

What you get: If you prefer unique, artisan-made pieces or 14ct solid gold (which is purer than 9ct), independent makers on platforms like Etsy UK offer stunning options.

Price range: £100 to £350 ($130–$450 USD) can easily buy you a dainty pair of 14ct solid yellow gold lab-created or natural emerald studs/drops.
