Several prominent online UK jewelers cater to customers looking for both natural (mined) and lab-grown stones (including diamonds and colored gemstones).

Top options operating online in the UK that explicitly feature both choices include:

1. The Diamond Store

Overview: A major online UK fine jeweler that gives shoppers clear filters to toggle between mined/natural and lab-grown options for diamonds and colored gemstones (sapphires, emeralds, rubies, aquamarine, tanzanite, etc.).

What they offer: Rings, earrings, and necklaces. They explicitly categorize their inventory by stone nature (Mined vs. Lab-Grown), letting you customize or choose settings built in London with UK hallmarking.

2. Lily Arkwright

Overview: Based in Manchester, Lily Arkwright is well-known for ethical fine jewellery and engagement rings.

What they offer: They specialize heavily in lab-grown diamonds, moissanite, and premium lab-grown colored gemstones (partnering with brands like Chatham for lab emeralds, sapphires, and rubies), but they also offer natural diamond and natural gemstone collections. Their website allows you to choose your preferred stone origin.

3. Austen & Blake

Overview: A prominent custom jeweler with a strong online presence in the UK, allowing you to design your own rings and jewelry pieces from scratch.

What they offer: They feature extensive searchable databases for both natural diamonds/gemstones and lab-grown diamonds, allowing you to pick your exact stone specifications (cut, clarity, carat, color) and match it with your preferred precious metal setting.

4. Queensmith / Vashi-style Bespoke Online Retailers

Many high-end contemporary UK direct-to-consumer jewelers (such as Queensmith or similar Hatton Garden-origin online brands) stock both mined and lab-grown alternatives side-by-side. They typically let you choose the exact origin of your diamond or colored center stone during the bespoke design process.

Tips for Shopping Online in the UK:

Check the Hallmarking: Reputable UK online jewelers will feature a British Hallmark stamp on precious metals (gold, platinum, silver), ensuring the metal quality is independently verified.

Certifications: For both lab-grown and natural stones above a certain size, look for grading certificates from recognized authorities like the IGI (International Gemological Institute) or GIA (Gemological Institute of America) to verify quality.
