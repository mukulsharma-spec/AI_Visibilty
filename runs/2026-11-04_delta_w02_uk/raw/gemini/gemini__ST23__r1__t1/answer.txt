Shopping for an aquamarine engagement ring in the UK gives you some fantastic options, ranging from large online fine jewelers with custom design features to traditional high street institutions and independent Hatton Garden specialists.

Because aquamarines are softer than diamonds (ranking around 7.5 to 8 on the Mohs scale), you'll want to buy from a reputable jeweler that uses secure settings (like bezels or protective prongs) and offers solid warranties.

The best places to buy an aquamarine engagement ring in the UK are categorized below by what they do best:

1. Dedicated UK Online Jewelers (Great for Customization & Value)

The Diamond Store: One of the UK's leading online fine jewelers, they have a massive collection of natural and lab-grown aquamarine rings. They frequently run sales, offer free UK delivery, and include a 5-year warranty. Their styles range from simple solitaires to vintage-inspired halo and three-stone designs paired with diamonds.

Austen & Blake: Known for bespoke and custom-made jewelry. If you have a very specific vision for your aquamarine ring (e.g., a particular shade of ice blue, a specific cut like emerald or pear, and a unique band design), they let you build your ring from scratch online or via their UK showrooms.

GLAMIRA UK: Highly customizable. Their platform lets you pick an aquamarine center stone and completely customize the metal (9ct, 14ct, 18ct gold, or platinum), stone quality, and band width, giving you immense control over the final look and price tag.

2. Vintage & Antique Specialists (Great for Unique, Heirloom Style)

London Victorian Ring Co. (Hatton Garden & Online): If you love vintage, Edwardian, or Art Deco aesthetics, this is a premier spot. Aquamarine was immensely popular in antique jewelry, and they specialize in crafting exquisite, authentic-looking vintage reproduction rings featuring deep or ice-blue aquamarines surrounded by intricate filigree or diamond halos.

Berganza / Vintage Jewellers: If you want an actual antique ring (Victorian, Edwardian, or Art Deco) rather than a reproduction, browsing high-end vintage dealers in London’s Hatton Garden or the Burlington Arcade often yields breathtaking, one-of-a-kind natural aquamarine pieces.

3. High-End Independent & Designer Brands (Great for Artistic Craftsmanship)

Emma Chapman Jewels: If you want something artistic, bespoke, and award-winning, independent UK designers like Emma Chapman offer stunning, statement-making gemstone rings that step away from cookie-cutter corporate designs.

Garrard: If budget is no object and you want royal-tier luxury, Garrard is a historic London House (and former Royal Warrant holder) famous for magnificent colored gemstone jewelry. (Fun fact: Prince Harry famously gave Meghan Markle an aquamarine ring that once belonged to Princess Diana).

Quick Tips for Buying an Aquamarine in the UK:

Check the Metal: Aquamarine looks striking in white metals (platinum or white gold) which emphasize its icy, crystal-clear blue. However, setting it in yellow or rose gold gives it a gorgeous, warm, vintage contrast.

Protection is Key: Because it can scratch more easily than a diamond, look for settings that protect the corners or edges of the stone (such as a bezel setting or V-prongs if it's a pear/marquise shape).

Ask About Treatment: Most aquamarines on the market are gently heat-treated to enhance their blue color and remove green undertones—this is standard industry practice, but it's always good to confirm authenticity and certification.
