When you want to customize a pendant online—specifically choosing both your preferred precious metal and the exact gemstone size or carat weight—traditional "off-the-shelf" retail sites won't give you that level of control. Instead, you'll want to look at online jewelers that feature interactive product builders or made-to-order bespoke services operating in the UK.

The best options available to UK shoppers include:

1. Dedicated Online Custom Builders & Fine Jewelers

HiSpek Diamonds

What you can pick: Precious metal (9K or 18K yellow/white gold, platinum) and gemstone size/carat weight (ranging from subtle 0.15ct solitaires up to larger statement stones).

How it works: Their online collection features direct dropdown product builders where you select your metal purity/color and choose exact diamond or gemstone dimensions/carats before the piece is custom-crafted.

100kGems

What you can pick: Base metal (e.g., 925 Sterling Silver, 10K/higher gold karats) and overall pendant scale/size (ranging from compact 1.5-inch builds up to 4-inch statement sizes), alongside stone types (moissanite, lab diamonds, or custom gemstone requests).

How it works: They feature an interactive online custom pendant builder where you configure your sizing and materials to see live price adjustments before submitting the order for UK/international fulfillment. (All qualifying precious metal items are independently hallmarked in Hatton Garden, London).

2. British Bespoke & Made-to-Order Workshops (Interactive Customisation)

If you are looking for artisan British jewelry houses where you can specify exact stone dimensions and metal types, you can use their made-to-order services:

Pruden & Smith (Based in Ditchling, East Sussex)

What you can pick: Precious metals and specific natural gemstones (sapphires, emeralds, opals, etc.).

How it works: They specialize in handmade British fine jewelry. Many of their collection items (such as their cluster and birthstone pendants) let you select the specific metal finish and stones directly on the product page, or you can contact their workshop team to scale a gemstone to exact millimeter requirements.

Charms of Light / Independent UK Bespoke Artisans

If you want a specific semi-precious stone cut to a certain wire-wrap or bezel metal setting (sterling silver, gold, copper), independent bespoke creators allow complete custom specification over email/consultation, shipping easily across the UK.

3. Charm-Based & Modular Builders (For Multi-Stone/Charm Pendants)

If your version of a pendant is a chain stacked with customizable individual charms or birthstones:

Astrid & Miyu (The Story Chain® Builder)

What you can pick: Metal base (9k solid yellow or white gold) and individual letter, symbol, or gemstone/birthstone charms of varying shapes.

How it works: Their online visualizer lets you build a custom necklace from scratch, choosing how many stones or charms you want and what metal color they are cast in.

Tip for your search: If you have a very specific millimeter size for a gemstone in mind that isn't appearing on a standard dropdown menu, look for sites offering a CAD (Computer-Aided Design) bespoke service (like HiSpek or 100kGems). They will let you send dimensions or reference photos so a goldsmith can custom-set a stone of your exact dimensions into your preferred metal.
