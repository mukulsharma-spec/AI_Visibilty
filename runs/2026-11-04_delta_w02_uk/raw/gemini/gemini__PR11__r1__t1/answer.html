What to Look Out for When Shopping:

"Real" vs. "Created": Make sure the product description specifies Natural Sapphire if you want mined earth stones, or Lab-Created / Lab-Grown Sapphire if you prefer man-made stones. Lab-created sapphires are chemically real sapphires (the exact same crystal structure and physical properties), but they cost a fraction of mined stones. Most items under £500 in standard retail stores will either be smaller natural sapphires or lab-created sapphires set in solid 9ct gold.

The Metal Hallmark: If you are buying gold or silver from a UK retailer, look for the official UK Assay Office hallmark (stamped inside or on the post/back) to guarantee the purity of the metal (e.g., 375 for 9ct gold, 925 for sterling silver).
