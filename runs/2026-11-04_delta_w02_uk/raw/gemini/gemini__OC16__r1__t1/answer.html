Shopping for October birthstone jewelry in the UK gives you a wonderful selection of options, as October actually has two official birthstones: Opal (known for its iridescent play-of-color) and Tourmaline (frequently found in stunning shades of pink, green, and multi-color).

Where you should look depends on your budget and whether you want to shop on the high street or online:

1. High Street Jewellers (Great for Quality Fine Jewelry & Service)

Goldsmiths: Offers a dedicated October birthstone collection featuring high-end gold and silver pieces, primarily showcasing Pink Tourmaline and Opal rings, earrings, and pendants.

H. Samuel: A familiar high-street name carrying a range of opal and gemstone birthstone jewelry, often with accessible price points and seasonal sales.

John Lewis & Partners: Their online and select department stores feature a stylish selection of contemporary opal women's jewelry from various fashion and fine jewelry brands.

2. Specialist Online Fine Jewellers

The Diamond Store: A UK-based online jeweler with an extensive collection dedicated to October birthstones, offering a wide range of natural opals and pink tourmalines set in 9K/18K gold and silver, backed by UK hallmarking.

Monica Vinader: Known for modern, demi-fine everyday luxury (sustainable 18ct gold vermeil and sterling silver), they carry popular October birthstone chain necklaces featuring pink tourmaline.

3. Indie Brands & Casual Styles

Boho Betty UK: Excellent if you are looking for more casual, affordable, or boho-style gemstone jewelry. They have an entire collection dedicated to October birthstone tourmaline bracelets, charm bracelets, and beaded necklaces.

4. Handmade & Personalised Options

Etsy UK: If you are looking for something unique, custom-made, or budget-friendly, Etsy has thousands of independent UK makers crafting everything from dainty sterling silver pink tourmaline rings to minimalist opal layering necklaces.
