Finding a gemstone ring with free engraving in the UK requires a bit of looking, as gemstone rings can be trickier to engrave than plain metal bands (due to stone settings taking up inner band space or delicate shanks). However, several major UK jewellers, contemporary brands, and bespoke workshops offer complimentary engraving services online or in-store.

Here are some of the best places to look:

1. Contemporary & Designer Brands (Online & Flagship Stores)

Monica Vinader: Well-known for semi-precious gemstone jewellery, birthstone pieces, and stacking rings. They offer complimentary engraving on a wide selection of their engravable rings and gemstone-accented lines (like their Siren or Riva collections). You can choose text, dates, or even small doodles/motifs.

Astrid & Miyu: Popular for contemporary stacked rings. They feature an engravable collection where you can get pieces personalised. They offer engraving services either online or via select UK concept stores (such as London and Edinburgh locations).

Missoma: Famous for semi-precious stone rings and demi-fine jewellery. They periodically or permanently offer complimentary engraving on select engravable rings and customisable pieces.

2. High Street & Independent Jewellers (Wedding/Fine Jewellery)

Orla James: If you are looking for a gemstone-set wedding band or a custom fine ring, they specialise in engraved rings and offer free engraving on all online products.

Friendly Diamonds UK / Lab-Diamond Specialists: If you are shopping for a gemstone or lab-grown diamond ring, online fine jewellers like Friendly Diamonds provide free engraving services for custom-made engagement and statement rings.

Hatton Garden Independent Workshops (London): If you prefer shopping in person, many independent bespoke workshops in London's Hatton Garden (such as A Star Diamonds or similar family-run shops) will often include complimentary laser engraving as a courtesy if you purchase a fine gemstone ring directly through them. It never hurts to ask the jeweller on the spot if they can throw it in for free!

Important Tips Before You Buy:

Check the Band Width/Space: Gemstone rings—especially those with multi-stones or intricate settings—might have limited smooth metal space on the inside band (the shank). Check with the jeweller to ensure there is enough room for your desired text.

Return Policies: Be aware that engraved items are usually non-refundable unless they arrive faulty, as they are considered custom-made.
