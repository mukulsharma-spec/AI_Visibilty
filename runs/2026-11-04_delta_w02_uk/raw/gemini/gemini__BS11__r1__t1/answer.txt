If you are shopping online in the UK for an opal pendant necklace, there are several reputable retailers catering to different styles, budgets, and metal preferences (from sterling silver to fine gold and diamonds).

Great places to look include:

1. Fine Jewelry & Custom Specialists

Angara UK: A great destination if you want customisation. They offer a vast collection of opal solitaire, halo, and vintage-style pendants where you can often choose your preferred metal type (white, yellow, rose gold, or platinum) and opal quality grade. They also include free insured shipping and easy returns.

C W Sellors: A well-known UK-based luxury jeweller featuring an extensive online collection of British-crafted and designer opal necklaces, often incorporating exquisite Derbyshire Blue John or fine Australian and Ethiopian opals. They offer free delivery and 0% finance options.

2. Affordable & Broad Selection Stores

TJC (The Jewelry Channel): If you are looking for budget-friendly options, TJC offers a wide variety of opal pendants set in silver and gold-plated settings. They feature everything from simple Ethiopian opal solitaires to complex multi-gemstone and drop designs, often with frequent discounts.

The Opal (theopal.co.uk): A specialist online retailer focused heavily on opal jewelry. They stock a wide array of sterling silver and gold opal pendant designs (including blue and white opals), often bundling matching earring sets.

3. Independent & Designer Boutiques

Independent UK Jewellers (e.g., T. Paterson Jewellers, Sheila Fleet): Many independent regional UK jewellers have robust online stores featuring high-end 9ct and 18ct gold opal and diamond cluster pendants if you are looking for something unique or artisan-made.

Etsy UK: Excellent if you want hand-crafted, vintage, or bespoke opal pieces directly from independent makers across the UK.

Tip before buying: Opals are a softer gemstone (scoring about 5.5 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale), so if you plan on wearing your pendant every day, check whether it features a robust setting (like a bezel or secure prongs) to protect the stone.
