You can find a wide range of sapphire necklaces and pendants online in the UK, catering to all styles, budgets, and occasions.

The best places to shop online depend on what you are looking for:

1. High Street & Department Stores (Mid-Range to Fine Jewellery)

Ernest Jones: A reliable high street jeweller offering a great selection of diamond and sapphire cluster pendants, halo necklaces, and gold chains.

Goldsmiths: Known for fine jewellery, they offer stunning pieces ranging from accessible 9ct white gold sapphire halos to luxury designer brands (like Roberto Coin and Fope).

John Lewis & Partners: Ideal if you are looking for classic, gift-friendly sapphire pendants set in silver or gold with dependable UK mainland delivery.

2. Specialist & Luxury Jewellers

Austen & Blake: Great if you want customization. They allow you to choose your preferred metal type (white gold, yellow gold, rose gold, platinum) and select between natural or lab-grown sapphires and diamonds for a bespoke pendant.

Berry’s Jewellers: A luxury jeweller with a rich heritage offering high-end sapphire and diamond cluster necklaces.

Garrard: If you are looking for ultimate British luxury and royal-tier sapphire craftsmanship (they hold a Royal Warrant), their high-end fine jewellery collections are exceptional.

3. Budget-Friendly & TV/Online Retailers

TJC (The Jewellery Channel): Excellent if you are shopping on a budget or looking for sterling silver pieces, multi-sapphire bead necklaces, or heavily discounted gemstone designs.

Quick Tips for Buying Online in the UK:

Check the Hallmarking: Ensure any precious metal (gold, silver, platinum) is legally hallmarked in accordance with the UK Assay Office.

Lab-Grown vs. Natural: Decide beforehand if you prefer earth-mined or lab-grown sapphires, as this significantly impacts the price tag while offering identical physical properties.

Returns & Delivery: Check if the retailer offers free secure insured delivery (standard for higher-end jewellery) and a clear return window in case it doesn't look quite right in person.
