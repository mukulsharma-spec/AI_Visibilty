Several reputable online jewellery retailers operating in the UK offer gemstone collections alongside free delivery options. Depending on how fast you need your items and your order value, here are the top choices to consider:

1. The Diamond Store

The Offering: One of the UK's largest online jewellers, featuring an extensive selection of precious and semi-precious gemstone rings, earrings, and necklaces (such as sapphires, rubies, emeralds, and birthstones).

Shipping Policy: Offers free worldwide/UK delivery on orders, often with rapid dispatch windows for standard stock items.

2. Astley Clarke

The Offering: A well-known British fashion and fine jewellery brand heavily recognized for its contemporary semi-precious and precious gemstone designs (birthstone styles, stacking rings, and vibrant colour gemstone lockets).

Shipping Policy: Provides Free Global/UK Delivery on all orders, alongside free UK returns, making it very seamless if you need something delivered quickly without added shipping overhead.

3. TJC (The Jewellery Channel)

The Offering: A massive online destination for loose gemstones and a vast array of affordable gemstone jewellery set in gold, silver, and platinum.

Shipping Policy: Offers free UK delivery once you meet a relatively modest minimum spend threshold (typically on orders over £65), with prompt standard and expedited shipping routes.

4. C W Sellors Jewellers

The Offering: A long-standing British luxury jeweller specialising in fine British-crafted gemstone jewellery, featuring regional stones like Whitby Jet alongside traditional precious gemstones.

Shipping Policy: They routinely offer free standard delivery across their online gemstone collections, with secure tracked options.

Quick Shopping Tips for Fast UK Delivery:

Check Cut-off Times: If you need next-day delivery, look out for express courier options at checkout and note the retailer's daily order cut-off time (often mid-afternoon, around 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM).

Bespoke vs. Ready-to-Ship: Ensure the gemstone piece you are eyeing is marked as "in stock" rather than "made to order" or "custom sized," as rings requiring custom resizing will add a few days to the turnaround time.
