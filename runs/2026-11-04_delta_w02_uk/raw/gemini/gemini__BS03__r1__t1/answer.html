An oval blue sapphire pendant with a diamond halo is a timeless, classic piece (reminiscent of Princess Diana and Kate Middleton’s famous ring style). Several reputable fine jewellery retailers and online makers in the UK offer this specific design, catering to different budgets and preferences for natural vs. lab-grown stones:

1. Dedicated UK Online Fine Jewellers

The Diamond Store: A major UK-based online jeweller that features a dedicated collection of sapphire and diamond halo pendants (including oval options). They offer choices between natural mined sapphires and lab-grown stones, paired with various gold carats or platinum. Pieces are designed and hallmarked in London, and they often include warranties and finance options.

Jay Nick: Offers a customizable Oval Blue Sapphire Halo Pendant handcrafted and hallmarked at the London Assay Office. Their platform lets you select the exact gemstone grade (from AA to AAA quality richness), gemstone size, and your choice of 9ct/18ct white, yellow, rose gold, or platinum.

Austen & Blake: Known for customizable fine jewellery with showrooms across the UK. They allow you to build or tailor sapphire and diamond halo pendants to your preferred specifications, choosing between natural or lab-grown diamonds and your preferred precious metal.

Shining Diamonds: Based online with physical showroom appointments available (such as in Birmingham and Brighton), they carry an Oval Shape Blue Sapphire Single Halo Pendant crafted to order with secure UK delivery and a 30-day return policy.

2. International Brands with UK Platforms

Angara (UK): A major online gemstone specialist with a dedicated UK storefront. They feature a wide variety of handcrafted oval blue sapphire halo pendants where you can customize the sapphire quality grade, carat weight, and metal type (such as 14ct or 18ct white gold, yellow gold, or platinum).

3. High Street & Independent Jewellers

High Street Names: Traditional UK high street jewellers like Goldsmiths, Ernest Jones, or Mappin & Webb frequently carry precious gemstone cluster and halo pendants. Stock can vary by location, but they are great options if you prefer to see the deep blue hue of the sapphire in person before buying.

Local Independent Jewellers: Visiting your local jewellery quarter (such as Hatton Garden in London, the Jewellery Quarter in Birmingham, or the historic lanes in other major cities) is an excellent alternative. An independent bench jeweller can custom-make an oval sapphire and diamond halo pendant tailored precisely to your budget and choice of chain length.

A quick shopping tip: When buying, check whether the side diamonds and central sapphire are natural or lab-grown, as lab-grown alternatives can significantly lower the overall price while offering the exact same physical and optical brilliance. Also, ensure the piece carries a proper UK Hallmark from an official Assay Office (like London, Birmingham, or Edinburgh) to guarantee the purity of the gold or platinum.
