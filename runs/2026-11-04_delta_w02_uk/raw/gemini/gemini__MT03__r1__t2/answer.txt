Yes, TJC (The Jewellery Channel) is a legitimate, legal, and long-standing company operating in the UK (with physical operations and a broadcasting TV channel). They are not an outright scam, and thousands of customers regularly buy from them.

However, looking at customer feedback across platforms like Trustpilot and consumer review sites, opinions are a bit mixed, and there are a few important things to keep in mind:

The Pros (Why people trust them)

Established & Regulated: They have been operating in the UK for roughly two decades, are fully registered, and handle a massive volume of transactions daily.

Price & Value: They are popular because they offer heavily discounted, budget-friendly jewelry, often running auction formats or steep clearance sales.

Charitable Model: Every purchase triggers a donation to feed a child in need through their "One for One" program, which many buyers appreciate.

The Cons (What to watch out for)

Customer Service & Returns: The most common complaints center around customer service delays, slow shipping, or friction when trying to process returns and refunds.

Quality Match to Expectations: Because they sell low-cost jewelry (such as lower-carat gold or silver plated with gemstones that may be heavily treated), some buyers feel the items look smaller or less vibrant in person than they did on the television screen or high-definition website.

Verdict

If you are buying a low-cost fashion piece or a casual gift, TJC is generally safe to use as long as you read the product descriptions carefully (checking stone sizes and metal types).

However, if you are shopping for a high-value investment piece (like a fine ruby engagement ring), you are usually much better off buying from a dedicated high street jeweller or a reputable specialist where you can inspect the stone in person and receive independent gemological certification.
