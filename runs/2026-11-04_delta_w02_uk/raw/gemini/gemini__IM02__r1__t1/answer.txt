Shopping for an emerald-cut ring in the UK gives you access to some incredible traditional jewelers, modern lab-grown specialists, and bespoke artisans.

Depending on whether you mean an emerald-cut emerald center stone with diamond side stones, or an emerald-cut diamond center stone with emerald side stones, several standout UK options and styles are available to explore:

Popular Design Styles to Look For

The Three-Stone (Trilogy) Setting: Features a striking emerald-cut center stone flanked by smaller diamond side stones (such as trillion, baguette, or smaller emerald cuts). Baguette side stones are particularly popular with emerald-cut centers because their linear step-cuts complement each other seamlessly for an Art Deco look.

Side-Stone / Shoulder-Set Styles: The center emerald is accented by rows of pavé or channel-set round diamonds along the band, adding extra brilliance.

Toi et Moi or Mixed Gemstones: Some modern designs flip the script, pairing an emerald-cut diamond with an emerald side stone, or vice versa.

Tips for Buying Emeralds in the UK

Check for Certification: If you are investing in a high-value emerald or diamond side-stone piece, ensure the diamonds and the central gemstone come with recognized certificates (like GIA, IGI, or anchor lab reports).

Understand Inclusions: Natural emeralds naturally feature tiny internal inclusions (often called jardin or garden). These are proof of authenticity, but always check that the stone doesn't have fractures threatening its structural integrity, especially for an emerald cut, which has open steps.

Metal Choice: Emeralds pop intensely against yellow gold (which warms up the green hue traditionally) or look sleek and icy when paired with platinum or white gold alongside white diamonds.
