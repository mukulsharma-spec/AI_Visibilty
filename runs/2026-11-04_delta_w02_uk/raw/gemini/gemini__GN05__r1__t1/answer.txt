When shopping for colored gemstone jewelry online in the UK, the "best" place depends on your budget, whether you are looking for fine precious stones (sapphires, rubies, emeralds) or semi-precious everyday pieces, and whether you want high street convenience or independent craftsmanship.

The top options are broken down below by category to help you choose:

1. High Street & Established UK Retailers (Best for Reliability & Variety)

If you want the security of buying from well-known UK brands with physical storefronts, easy returns, and customer service guarantees, check out these online catalogs:

Goldsmiths: One of the UK's leading luxury jewellers. They offer a gorgeous online selection of fine coloured gemstone rings, necklaces, and earrings (particularly sapphires, rubies, and tanzanite) set in precious metals.

Berry’s Jewellers: A long-standing luxury UK jeweller with an extensive online collection of high-end gemstone pieces, blending classic British craftsmanship with vibrant, premium stones.

TJC (The Jeweller's Channel): If you are looking for more affordable or budget-friendly fashion and semi-precious gemstone jewelry, TJC has a massive online inventory ranging from multi-gemstone bracelets to everyday silver pieces, often with great value deals.

2. Independent British Boutiques & Bespoke Makers (Best for Unique Quality & Ethical Sourcing)

If you want something artisanal, unique, or custom-made with traceable stones:

Titcombe Bespoke Jewellery: Based in the Cotswolds and Bristol, they specialize in exceptional British craftsmanship. Their online collection showcases stunning natural sapphire, emerald, and ruby pieces, and they excel at bespoke online consultations if you want to design a custom piece.

London Gemstones: A great online option focusing specifically on natural gemstones and pearl jewelry that are handmade by trusted artisans, cutting out middlemen to offer authentic stones.

3. Major International Direct-to-Consumer Specialists (Best for Customization & Value)

If you want to pick your exact gemstone type, grade, and metal setting (e.g., choosing a specific aquamarine or morganite cut and placing it in a custom ring mounting):

Blue Nile & Angara: While operating globally, both ship seamlessly to the UK with duties handled. Angara in particular is renowned for its massive online selection of colored gemstone jewelry, allowing you to customize the stone quality grade, carat size, and metal type for rings, pendants, and earrings.

Tips for Buying Gemstones Online in the UK:

Check for Certification: For high-value precious stones (like rubies, sapphires, and emeralds), ensure the retailer provides a certificate of authenticity or gemological lab report (such as GIA or IGI).

Understand Treatments: Many colored gemstones (like rubies and sapphires) are routinely heat-treated to enhance color. Reputable UK sellers will disclose whether a stone is treated or "no heat" (which commands a higher price).

Review Return Policies: Because color can look slightly different on a computer screen compared to real life, always buy from a retailer with a clear, hassle-free 14-to-30-day return policy.
