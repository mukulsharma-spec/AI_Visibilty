An budget of under £800 gives you plenty of great options for a tanzanite ring in the UK. Because tanzanite is a striking blue-violet gemstone that is often paired with diamonds and set in white gold or yellow gold, you can find lovely cluster, halo, or trilogy styles within this price bracket.

Several reputable UK online jewelry stores and high street brands offer options well within your budget:

1. The Diamond Store

Why look here: They feature a dedicated collection of natural tanzanite rings paired with diamonds, often set in 9K or 18K white/yellow gold.

What you’ll get for under £800: They frequently run online promotions. You can easily find 9K white gold tanzanite and diamond halo or multi-stone rings ranging from £250 to £750, with some select 18K gold pieces dipping just under the £800 mark.

2. TJC (TV Jewellery / The Jewellery Channel)

Why look here: TJC is well-known for offering budget-friendly gemstone jewelry sourced directly, featuring various grades of tanzanite (from A to AAA/AAAA).

What you’ll get for under £800: If you want a larger tanzanite stone without breaking the bank, TJC is a great place to look. You can find sterling silver or solid gold tanzanite rings for anywhere from £100 to £500, allowing you to get a much larger carat weight for a fraction of the cost.

3. F. Hinds

Why look here: A long-standing, trusted British high street and online jeweller with a solid selection of tanzanite and diamond pieces (often under their "Kilimanjaro" tanzanite collection).

What you’ll get for under £800: They feature 9K white and yellow gold tanzanite cluster and oval-cut rings typically priced between £350 and £700.

4. T.H. Baker

Why look here: Another historic UK family jeweller offering both new pieces and pre-owned selections.

What you’ll get for under £800: They carry brand-new 9K gold tanzanite-and-diamond cluster rings starting around £450 to £675. Shopping their pre-owned section can also yield higher-carat vintage tanzanite rings for under £800 that would normally retail much higher.

Tips for Buying Tanzanite in the UK:

Check the Metal: At £800 and under, most new rings will be set in 9K gold (white or yellow) or sterling silver. If you prefer 18K gold or platinum, look toward pre-owned/vintage collections (such as Browns Family Jewellers or T.H. Baker's estate pieces), where your money will stretch further.

Understand Durability: Tanzanite ranks around 6 to 7 on the Mohs hardness scale (softer than a diamond). If you plan to wear the ring every day (like an engagement ring), look for a protective setting like a bezel or a low-profile halo, and avoid heavy knocks.
