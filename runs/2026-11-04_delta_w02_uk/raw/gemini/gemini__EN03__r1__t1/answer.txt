Shopping for a sapphire engagement ring in the UK gives you a wonderful range of choices, from contemporary custom online jewellers to historic showroom districts. Because sapphires rank 9 on the Mohs scale of hardness (just under diamonds), they are exceptionally durable for daily wear.

Depending on your preference for shopping online, visiting a showroom, or going completely bespoke, here are the best places to look in the UK:

1. Specialist & Custom Online/Showroom Jewellers (Best for Customisation & Value)

Queensmith (Hatton Garden, London): Highly regarded for bespoke and custom-made engagement rings. They source exceptional ethical sapphires (in classic deep blue, but also teal, pink, and yellow) and handcraft everything in their central London workshop.

Austen & Blake (Multiple UK Locations - e.g., London, Manchester, Birmingham, Glasgow): They specialise in made-to-order rings. You can choose your sapphire shape (oval, cushion, round, emerald cut) and pair it with a platinum or gold band, either online or by booking an in-store appointment.

Purely Diamonds (London showroom / Online): A great option if you want high-street style savings. They manufacture their gemstone and diamond rings directly in the UK and feature an extensive collection of sapphire halo and solitaire designs.

2. Independent Regional Specialists (Best for Antique & Heritage Styles)

Hancocks Jewellers (Manchester): If you are in the North, Hancocks is famous for magnificent fine jewellery, offering stunning platinum and 18ct gold sapphire and diamond cluster or three-stone rings.

Local Antique & Vintage Jewellers (e.g., London's Hatton Garden, Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter, or The Argyll Arcade in Glasgow): If you love the vintage, Victorian, or Edwardian look (such as cluster settings reminiscent of Princess Diana/Kate Middleton), visiting historical jewellery quarters will give you access to incredible estate and vintage-inspired sapphire pieces.

3. High Street Names (Best for Convenient Browsing)

Ernest Jones & H.Samuel: Both high-street mainstays carry ready-to-wear sapphire and diamond cluster or trilogy engagement rings online and across numerous physical shopping centres throughout the UK.
