Superior durability and clarity: Natural emeralds are notoriously heavily included (full of internal fractures called jardin), which makes them quite brittle and prone to breaking under impact. Lab-created emeralds are grown in controlled environments, meaning they have far fewer structural flaws. While they share the same Mohs hardness of 7.5 to 8, their cleaner internal structure usually makes them less fragile than a natural emerald of equivalent size.

Price: They cost a fraction of the price of mined emeralds—typically 80% to 90% cheaper—allowing you to get a much larger, clearer, and more vibrant stone for your budget.

Where to buy lab-created emeralds in the UK

Whether you are looking for loose stones or finished fine jewelry (like engagement rings, necklaces, or earrings), several reputable options operate in the UK market:

Dedicated Fine Jewelers (Online & Showrooms):

The Diamond Store: A well-known UK-based online fine jeweler that offers an extensive collection of lab-grown emerald rings, earrings, and pendants set in silver, gold vermeil, and solid gold.

Novita Diamonds UK: They specialize in lab-grown gemstones (including a large inventory of lab-created emeralds mimicking famous origins like Colombian and Zambian) and allow you to custom-design your own rings online or via their UK showrooms (London/Manchester).

GLAMIRA UK: Great if you want heavy customization. They let you pick a jewelry design and swap out the center stone for a lab-created emerald, choosing your preferred metal type and purity.

High Street & Department Stores:

Traditional UK high street jewelers are gradually expanding their lab-grown selections. Keep an eye on contemporary collections at stores like Signet-owned brands (H.Samuel / Ernest Jones) or independent bespoke jewellers who can source loose lab-grown hydrothermal emeralds for custom mountings.

Bespoke Independent Jewellers:

If you want a truly unique piece, find a local independent jeweller (many have workshops in areas like London’s Hatton Garden or Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter). They can easily source premium loose lab-created emeralds and handcraft a bespoke setting for you.

Pro-Tip when shopping: Make sure the retailer explicitly calls the stone "lab-created" or "lab-grown/synthetic" rather than an "emerald simulant" (like cubic zirconia, glass, or composite stones), which look green but share none of the actual properties of a real emerald.
