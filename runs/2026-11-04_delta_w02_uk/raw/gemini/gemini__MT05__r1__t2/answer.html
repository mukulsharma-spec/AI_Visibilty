When it comes to speed, independent workshops with on-site goldsmiths (particularly in jewellery hubs like London's Hatton Garden) are significantly faster than high street chain stores.

1. The Speed Comparison

Independent Workshops / Hatton Garden Specialists (Fastest):

Turnaround: Same-day express (often ready in a few hours if dropped off by late morning) or standard 3 to 5 working days.

Why: They have master goldsmiths working directly on-site with their own laser welders and benches, meaning your ring doesn't get mailed away to a central facility.

High Street Jewellers (Slower):

Turnaround: 1 to 3 weeks.

Why: Most high street storefronts do not handle repairs in-store. They have to log the item, securely ship it to an off-site regional workshop, have it processed, and ship it back.

2. A Crucial Caveat for Gemstone Rings & Speed

While you can get a "same-day express" resize at places like Hatton Garden, the presence of gemstones can complicate a rush job:

If it's a hard stone (Diamond, Ruby, Sapphire) and the jeweller uses a Laser Welder: They can often safely resize it on the same day because they don't need to remove the stone.

If it's a heat-sensitive or soft stone (Emerald, Opal, Tanzanite): Even the fastest workshop will usually need extra time. Safely un-setting a delicate gemstone, resizing the band, and re-setting the stone tightly requires meticulous bench work that cannot be rushed without risking damage to the stone.

Summary Recommendation

If you need it done as fast as possible, look for an independent manufacturing jeweller with an on-site workshop. Call ahead, explain that it is a gemstone ring, and ask if they offer an on-site express/same-day service using a laser welder.
