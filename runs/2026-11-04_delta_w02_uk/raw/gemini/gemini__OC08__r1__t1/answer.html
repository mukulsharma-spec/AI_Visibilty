Finding a ruby gift for a 40th wedding anniversary in the UK is relatively easy, as the milestone is traditionally associated with fine jewellery, rich red tones, and personalized keepsakes. Depending on whether you are looking for fine jewelry, unique artisan goods, or meaningful symbolic items, several great options are available across the UK:

1. Fine Jewellery & Traditional Ruby Pieces

If you are looking for authentic ruby rings, necklaces, earrings, or bracelets:

High Street & Independent Jewellers:

C W Sellors (and their sister boutiques across the UK) features dedicated collections explicitly curated for ruby wedding anniversaries, ranging from classic white gold ruby clusters to contemporary pieces.

PureJewels (with a flagship in London and online) offers specialized gold and ruby anniversary jewelry pieces.

High street names like Ernest Jones or Goldsmiths carry a wide selection of ruby gemstone rings and pendants year-round.

Designer & Luxury Boutiques:

Dinny Hall offers gorgeous contemporary fine and semi-fine jewelry featuring pink precious gemstones and rubies (such as their Shuga collection).

For high-end luxury, London-based high jewelry houses like David Morris or designer brands like Tiffany & Co. feature exceptional luxury ruby pieces.

2. Personalised & Artisan Gifts (Online Marketplaces)

If you prefer something unique, customized, or non-jewelry-related:

Not On The High Street: This is one of the best UK platforms for 40th anniversary gifts. You can find everything from personalized ruby-themed family tree prints, custom-engraved glass vases, and ruby-red keepsake blankets to handmade glass friendship balls and milestone celebration candles.

Etsy UK: Perfect for supporting small British creators. You can source customized 1986 vintage memory posters, engraved ruby-accented champagne flutes, and bespoke memory books.

3. Keepsakes, Crystal, & Homeware

Department Stores (John Lewis & Selfridges): Excellent for luxury homeware gifts with a deep red or ruby theme, fine crystal glassware, or high-end kitchenware.

The Chinashop: Offers specialized crystal and porcelain milestone gifts if you want to lean into traditional anniversary elements alongside the ruby theme.
