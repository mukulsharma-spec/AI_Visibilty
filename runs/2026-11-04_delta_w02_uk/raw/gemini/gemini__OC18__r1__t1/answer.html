💡 Tips for Ordering for Valentine's Day:

Check the Delivery Cut-off: Because courier delays can happen around mid-February, look for retailers explicitly stating "Next Day Delivery" or "Express UK Shipping" and place your order a few days in advance to account for processing time.

Opt for Gift Wrapping: Many UK online jewellers offer complimentary luxury gift boxes and gift bags at checkout—saving you a scrambling trip to the shops later!
