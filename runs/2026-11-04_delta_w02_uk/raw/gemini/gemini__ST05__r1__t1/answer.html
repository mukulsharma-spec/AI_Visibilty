Citrine’s warm, golden-yellow tones make it a stunning and versatile gemstone. Whether you are looking for delicate everyday birthstone pieces, contemporary designer wear, or fine vintage gold, the UK has plenty of great online stores to choose from.

The best places to shop for citrine jewelry online in the UK span several distinct styles:

1. Department Stores & Curated Multi-Brand Retailers

John Lewis & Partners: An excellent starting point because they aggregate a massive variety of independent and mid-to-high-tier jewelry brands. Through their site, you can find citrine pieces from design-led labels like Alex Monroe (famous for their nature-inspired "Buttercup" citrine pieces), Rachel Jackson London (great for art-deco style sun amulets and birthstone hearts), Missoma (chic gold vermeil hoops), and Monica Vinader.

T.H. Baker: A long-standing British family jeweller offering a dedicated range of fine citrine rings, drop earrings, and diamond-accented pendants set in 9ct and 18ct yellow gold. They also feature a unique collection of second-hand and antique citrine statement rings if you love vintage character.

2. Contemporary Designer & Fashion-Forward Brands

ChloBo: If you love stacking bracelets, chokers, and modern silver or gold-plated boho jewelry, ChloBo features lovely pieces (like their "Soulful Rays" or "Sun Catcher" lines) incorporating citrine.

Daisy London: Known for elegant, highly wearable everyday jewelry, they feature distinct birthstone rings and pendants that highlight citrine's bright energy.

3. Crystal & Semi-Precious Artisan Brands

Djuna & Co.: If you prefer raw, bohemian, or healing-crystal-style jewelry, they specialize in handmade natural gemstone pieces, offering everything from dainty faceted citrine bead bracelets to raw crystal point necklaces set in sterling silver.

4. Antique & Pre-Loved Fine Jewelry

If you want something truly unique with a bit of history, looking at online pre-owned specialists like T.H. Baker’s Vintage collection or dedicated vintage dealers hosted on platforms like John Lewis often yields stunning Victorian or retro 9ct gold cushion-cut citrine cocktail rings that you simply won't find on the high street.
