The traditional January birthstone is garnet, known for its deep, rich red tones (though garnets can actually be found in several other colors).

Depending on your budget and style preference, you can find a wide range of January birthstone jewelry across the UK through high street stores, department stores, independent jewelry designers, and online platforms.

1. High Street Department Stores & Multi-Brand Retailers

John Lewis & Partners: A fantastic option if you want to browse multiple contemporary brands in one place. They carry gorgeous garnet and birthstone pieces from popular labels like Daisy London, Rachel Jackson London, Astley Clarke, and Ottoman Hands.

H.Samuel & T.H. Baker: Well-known British high street jewellers that stock dedicated selections of traditional garnet rings, pendant necklaces, and earrings, often set in 9ct yellow gold or sterling silver.

2. Contemporary British Jewellery Brands

If you are looking for trendy, everyday birthstone pieces, check out these designer brands online or in their standalone boutiques:

Rachel Jackson London: Famous for their "Electric Love" art-deco-inspired heart necklaces, rings, and bracelets featuring genuine garnet.

Daisy London: Offers delicate, stackable birthstone rings and talisman necklaces.

Astley Clarke: Great for higher-end contemporary pieces, including luxury garnet tennis bracelets and lockets.

3. Online Marketplaces & Independent Makers

Etsy UK: Perfect if you are looking for something unique, handmade, or personalized. You can find everything from dainty minimalist sterling silver garnet rings to vintage-style gold vermeil signet rings from UK-based independent artisans.

Angara UK: A great online specialist if you want to customize your jewelry (choosing the gold quality, gemstone grade, and carat weight for garnet rings, tennis bracelets, or halo pendants).

Shopping Tip: Because garnet is a relatively durable and traditional gemstone, many traditional jewellers also carry antique and vintage garnet pieces (such as Victorian cluster rings) if you are looking for something with a bit of history.
