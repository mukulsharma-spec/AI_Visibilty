When looking at jewellers like Queensmith, Austen & Blake, and Novita Diamonds, Queensmith stands out with the strongest return policy.

A breakdown of how their policies compare highlights the differences:

1. Queensmith (The Winner)

Return Window: 40 days (compared to the industry standard 30 days).

Why it's better: You get an extra 10 days to make up your mind, which is particularly helpful if you're planning a surprise proposal and need a little extra breathing room. They also offer a generous 60-day window for complimentary resizing if the fit isn't quite right.

2. Austen & Blake

Return Window: 30 days.

The Catch: Like most online custom jewellers, standard ready-to-customize rings can be returned within 30 days, but bespoke/custom designs or rings that have been engraved or altered are strictly non-refundable.

3. Novita Diamonds

Return Window: 30 days.

The Catch: While they offer a 30-day return policy for standard items, their policy explicitly states that fancy colored diamonds, gemstone rings (including sapphires), and custom/Toi Et Moi designs often fall outside their standard refund/exchange policy, meaning custom sapphire configurations might be final sale. (Though loose center stones can sometimes be returned, the custom-built setting may not be).

Important Golden Rules for Engagement Ring Returns:

No matter which jeweller you choose in the UK, keep these caveats in mind to avoid voiding a return policy:

The Security Tag: Most online jewellers attach a barcode security tag to the ring. Do not cut or remove this tag until you are 100% sure you are keeping the ring. Cutting the tag usually voids the return policy instantly.

Custom vs. Standard: If you design a completely unique, one-off sapphire ring from scratch using a bespoke CAD service, most high street and online jewellers will not accept returns on it because it cannot easily be resold. If you want a return safety net, look for pre-set or semi-customisable standard settings rather than full bespoke.
