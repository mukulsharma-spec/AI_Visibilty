Important Shopping Tips for the UK Market:

Bespoke vs. Ready-to-Ship: Under UK consumer regulations, custom-made, bespoke, or heavily engraved rings are generally exempt from standard change-of-mind return windows. If you are designing a ring from scratch using a "ring builder" tool, double-check whether they classify it as non-returnable.

Resizing: Many online jewellers offer a complimentary ring resizing service within the first 30 days, but it's always wise to verify if a resized ring voids or alters your ability to return it later for a full refund.

Condition: Ensure the ring remains unworn, undamaged, and accompanied by all original certificates (such as GIA, IGI, or gemstone reports) and packaging to ensure a seamless refund.
