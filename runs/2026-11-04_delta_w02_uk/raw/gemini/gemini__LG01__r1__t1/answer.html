Tips for Buying Lab-Grown Sapphires Online in the UK:

Check the Hallmark: Ensure any precious metal (gold or platinum) you buy online carries a legitimate UK hallmark from an Assay Office to guarantee metal purity.

Clarify "Created" vs. "Simulated": Ensure the listing explicitly says lab-grown or lab-created sapphire (which shares the exact physical, optical, and chemical properties of mined sapphires) rather than sapphire simulant (like cubic zirconia or glass, which just mimics the look).

Return Policies: Because you are buying jewelry online, double-check that the retailer offers a secure, tracked UK return or exchange policy—especially important if you are purchasing an engagement ring.
