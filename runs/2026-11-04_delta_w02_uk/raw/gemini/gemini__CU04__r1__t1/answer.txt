Tips for Ordering a Ring to Arrive in 2 Days:

Check the Finger Size: Ready-to-ship rings cannot usually be custom-resized on short notice without delaying shipment. Ensure the stock size matches your partner's ring size.

Watch the Cut-Off Time: Most UK jewelers offering next-day or 48-hour delivery have a strict daily order cut-off (often early-to-mid afternoon, around 1:00 PM or 2:00 PM). Make sure to order early in the day.

Confirm Courier Delivery Days: Bear in mind that guaranteed next-day services often operate on working days (Tuesday–Saturday). If you order on a Friday afternoon or over the weekend, dispatch may default to Monday for a Tuesday arrival.
