Shopping for fine or demi-fine jewelry featuring real gemstones under £200 in the UK is entirely doable. Many wonderful British brands and independent designer-makers offer pieces crafted with sterling silver, gold vermeil, or even solid gold paired with genuine natural or ethically-sourced gemstones.

The best UK brands, collections, and specific piece ideas within your budget include:

1. Astley Clarke

The Vibe: Contemporary, elegant, and deeply popular in the UK demi-fine market.

Gemstone Options: They feature stunning pieces using lapis lazuli, malachite, black onyx, green agate, moonstone, turquoise, and white sapphire.

Under £200 Ideas:

Pendant Necklaces: Gold Luna or Deco pendants featuring real green agate, lapis, or black onyx typically range from £118 to £178.

Huggie Earrings & Rings: Gemstone huggies and half-eternity stacking rings with white sapphires or semi-precious stones hover around £98 to £145.

2. Gemondo

The Vibe: A UK-based specialist in authentic, ethically sourced coloured gemstones and certified metals (often using sterling silver and solid gold options).

Gemstone Options: Everything from classic birthstones like amethyst, garnet, peridot, and citrine to rubies, sapphires, and emeralds.

Under £200 Ideas: Because of their focus on genuine stones, they have extensive lines of vintage-style art deco rings, stacking bands, and delicate gemstone cluster earrings well below the £200 mark.

3. H.AZEEM London

The Vibe: Luxury-inspired, colorful statement and everyday jewelry often inspired by history, art, and nature.

Gemstone Options: Blue topaz, peridot, garnet, smoky quartz, turquoise, and amethyst set in 925 sterling silver with 18k gold or rose gold plating.

Under £200 Ideas:

Necklaces & Earrings: Items like their Luscious Blue Topaz or Peridot drop earrings and pendant necklaces sit comfortably between £150 and £175.

4. Maya Magal London

The Vibe: Handcrafted, sustainable modern jewelry designed in London using recycled metals.

Gemstone Options: Real turquoise, moonstone, lab-options, and birthstones.

Under £200 Ideas:

Bracelets & Rings: Their delicate beaded gemstone bracelets (like turquoise or pearl mixes) and stacking organic light rings feature real stones and range from £95 to £180.

5. Spells of Love

The Vibe: Ethically made, delicate demi-fine jewelry crafted in 18k gold vermeil and sterling silver.

Gemstone Options: Real aquamarine, opal, garnet, pearls, and sapphires.

Under £200 Ideas:

Charm Hoops & Necklaces: Their bestselling gemstone droplet hoops (featuring genuine sapphires, aquamarine, or garnets) sit around £120, making them brilliant, high-quality gifts that look far more expensive than they are.

Tips for Shopping Real Gemstones Under £200:

Check the Metal Base: At this price point, look for 925 Sterling Silver or 18k Gold Vermeil (thick gold plating over sterling silver) rather than standard "gold-plated brass." Vermeil ensures the piece lasts much longer and is considered proper demi-fine jewelry.

Natural vs. Lab-Grown: Brands like Loveness Lee offer stunning lab-grown rubies and sapphires under £200, which are chemically identical to mined stones, highly sustainable, and budget-friendly.

Birthstone Personalization: If you are buying a gift for a milestone or birthday, filtering by the recipient's birthstone (e.g., Garnet for January, Amethyst for February, Peridot for August) adds a deeply thoughtful touch.
