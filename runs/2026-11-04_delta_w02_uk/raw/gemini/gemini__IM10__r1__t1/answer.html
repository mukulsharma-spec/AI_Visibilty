Three-stone ruby and diamond rings (often called trilogy rings) carry a gorgeous symbolic meaning, representing the past, present, and future of a relationship.

A variety of styles, price points, and designs are available across popular UK jewellery retailers to suit what you might be looking for:

1. Classic & Timeless Styles

The Look: Typically features a vibrant central oval or round ruby flanked by two brilliant-cut white diamonds, mounted on yellow gold, white gold, or platinum.

Where to find it: The Diamond Store (offers options like an 18K Yellow/White Gold Oval Ruby and Diamond Trilogy Ring starting around £1,164 to £1,615 depending on natural vs. lab-grown stone options).

Alternative: Angara UK features a popular Classic Oval Ruby and Round Diamond Three-Stone Ring starting around £819 upwards depending on the metal quality and gemstone grade you customise.

2. Modern & Contemporary Designs

The Look: Features alternative stone cuts—such as emerald-cut rubies paired with trapezoid or side diamonds—or unique settings like a bypass shank or east-west orientation.

Where to find it: Diamonds Factory UK (has showrooms in major cities like London, Birmingham, and Manchester). They offer Oval Trilogy and Emerald Trilogy ruby rings starting from roughly £829 to £1,400+, with heavy customisation available for band metal and claw settings.

Alternative: Angara UK also lists modern statement pieces like the East-West Oval Ruby and Pear Diamond Three-Stone Ring if you prefer a horizontal setting.

3. Fine Heritage & Luxury Pieces

The Look: Premium, investment-grade GIA-certified rubies matched with high-clarity (VS1/VVS) brilliant-cut or pear-cut diamond side stones in heavy platinum or 18k settings.

Where to find it: Hancocks Jewellers offers exceptional high-end pieces, such as an Oval Ruby and Brilliant Cut Diamond 3-Stone Ring or a heavier Oval Ruby and Pear Cut Diamond Trilogy, generally ranging from £4,000 to £8,500+.

Tips for Buying in the UK:

Hallmarking: Ensure any ring you buy is legally hallmarked by a UK Assay Office (such as London, Birmingham, or Edinburgh) to guarantee the purity of the precious metal (gold or platinum).

Certification: If you are spending a larger amount on a natural ruby, check whether the retailer provides certification (like GIA or equivalent) verifying the gemstone's origin and treatment status.

Lab-Grown Options: If you love the look of a large, flawless ruby and diamond combination but want to keep the budget lower, retailers like The Diamond Store offer gorgeous lab-grown ruby alternatives that significantly cut down the price tag.
