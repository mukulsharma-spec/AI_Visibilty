1. High Street & Fine Jewellers (Online Stores)

Beaverbrooks: Visit beaverbrooks.co.uk and search for "Emerald Jewellery" or "Birthstone Pendant". They offer home delivery or click-and-collect at your nearest high street store.

The Diamond Store: Go to thediamondstore.co.uk. They have a massive dedicated section for May Birthstone/Emerald jewellery (spanning lab-grown and natural emeralds) with filters for rings, necklaces, and earrings.

Austen & Blake: Visit austenblake.co.uk if you want to customise the metal type (e.g., white gold, yellow gold) on an emerald ring or necklace. They also have physical showrooms in a few UK cities if you prefer to look in person.

2. Contemporary & Designer Brands

Monica Vinader: (A favourite for modern British demi-fine jewelry). Go to monicavinader.com and search "Emerald Birthstone" to find their popular Vermeil stacking rings, friendship bracelets, and pendant necklaces.

Kit Heath: Go to kitheath.com to browse their British-designed sterling silver birthstone collections.

3. Artisan & Handcrafted Options

Etsy UK: Go to etsy.co.uk and type "May birthstone necklace mom UK" or "Zambian emerald sterling silver ring UK" into the search bar. This will filter results specifically for independent UK makers who can often ship custom or engraved pieces within a few days.
