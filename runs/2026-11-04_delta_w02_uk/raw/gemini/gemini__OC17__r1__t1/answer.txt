Yes, absolutely. Many high-street, independent, and luxury online jewellers in the UK routinely offer gift-wrapping services and gift receipts for items like gemstone necklaces.

1. Gift Wrapping

Standard Presentation: Almost all reputable UK jewellers will automatically supply a branded presentation box for a necklace free of charge.

Luxury Gift Wrapping: Many retailers (such as Goldsmiths, Beaverbrooks, Ernest Jones, Berry's Jewellers, and Astley Clarke) offer dedicated gift-wrapping services. This usually includes signature wrapping paper, ribbons, and a customized gift message card. Some offer it as a complimentary service, while others charge a small fee (typically between £3 and £6).

2. Gift Receipts & Invoices

Price Omission: If you specify during checkout (online) or at the counter (in-store) that the item is a gift, UK jewellers will provide a gift receipt or a non-priced dispatch note.

Returns & Exchanges: This special receipt ensures the recipient can exchange the gemstone necklace if it's the wrong length or style, but without showing them how much you paid.

Tips for Shopping in the UK:

Online Checkouts: Look out for a checkbox at checkout that says "Is this a order a gift?" or "Add gift options." This is where you can usually type a custom message and opt-in for a price-free gift receipt.

Independent Boutiques: If you are buying from independent gemstone or artisan jewellers (e.g., via Etsy UK or boutique online shops), they are often very accommodating and will happily wrap the item and omit pricing if you leave a note at checkout.
