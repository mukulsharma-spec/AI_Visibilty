When shopping for a ruby ring in the UK, neither Kay Jewelers nor Blue Nile is typically your best option.

Kay Jewelers is an American high-street giant and does not practically or easily serve the UK market (they are heavily US-centric, and ordering cross-border incurs massive international shipping, duties, and complicated returns).

Blue Nile does ship to the UK, but their inventory is heavily focused on diamonds and lab-grown stones. Their selection of colored gemstones like rubies is often quite limited, and buying a colored gemstone completely sight-unseen online can be risky since color, hue, and saturation vary so drastically from stone to stone.

If you are looking for a ruby ring in the UK, you have much better alternatives depending on whether you want high street convenience, custom online design, or antique/vintage character.

Alternative Places to Shop in the UK
1. For Vintage & Antique Character (The Best Choice for Rubies)

Rubies have a long history in British jewelry, and older cut rubies (like old mine cuts or cushion cuts) often have a deep, velvety "pigeon's blood" red that looks incredible in antique settings.

The Vintage Jeweller / Local Antique Shops (e.g., Grays Antique Centre in London): Buying pre-owned or antique (Victorian, Edwardian, or Art Deco) often gives you a much better quality-to-price ratio, and the stones are completely natural.

Browns Family Jewellers or Berganza: Excellent UK-based specialists for pre-owned and antique gemstone rings.

2. For High Street Trust & Service

If you want to walk into a store, see the ring in person, and have an easy time with resizing and warranties:

UK High Street Jewellers (Ernest Jones, H.Samuel, or Beaverbrooks): They regularly carry ruby and diamond cluster rings (the classic style featuring a central ruby surrounded by diamonds) or modern gold ruby bands.

Independent Local Jewellers: Visiting the jewellery quarter in your nearest major city (like Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter, Hatton Garden in London, or the Argyll Arcade in Glasgow) will give you access to hundreds of independent jewellers who can show you loose rubies and custom-build a ring for you.

3. For Modern Custom Online Shopping (Better than Blue Nile for Colored Stones)

If you prefer shopping online but want specialized craftsmanship:

Gemporia or Gemondo: Well-known UK-based online jewellers that specialize heavily in ethically sourced precious and semi-precious colored gemstones, offering a massive variety of ruby rings set in gold and silver at transparent price points.

Brilliant Earth or Taylor & Hart: If you want a bespoke engagement-style ring built around a certified ruby, Taylor & Hart is a UK-based custom jeweller that offers excellent sourcing for ethical rubies with 3D renderings and personal design consultations.

What to Look For When Buying a Ruby

If you decide to venture outside of Kay and Blue Nile, keep these quick tips in mind for rubies:

Color is King: The most valuable rubies have a vibrant, pure red to slightly purplish-red color. Avoid stones that look overly dark/blackish or too pale (pinkish).

Treatments: Almost all natural rubies are heat-treated to improve color and clarity—this is standard industry practice and generally accepted. However, always check if the ruby has been "glass-filled" or "composite" treated, which drastically lowers its value and durability.

Setting: Rubies are a 9 on the Mohs hardness scale (very durable), making them great for daily wear, but a secure setting like a bezel or strong double-claws will keep your stone safe.
