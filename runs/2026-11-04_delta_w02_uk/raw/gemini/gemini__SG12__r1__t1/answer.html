Shopping for a men’s ring featuring a colored gemstone in the UK gives you some fantastic options, ranging from traditional high street jewellers to independent artisans and online specialists.

The best places to look depend on your style and budget:

1. High Street & Online Fine Jewellers (Mid-to-High Range)

Angara UK: Excellent online selection specifically catering to men. They offer customizable men’s rings (such as signet styles or ribbed bands) where you can select stones like Blue Sapphire, Emerald, London Blue Topaz, or Black Onyx paired with yellow gold, white gold, platinum, or silver.

Diamond Heaven: Based in Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter (with showrooms in London, Manchester, etc.), they feature dedicated men's gemstone ring collections featuring robust bezel-set sapphires, emeralds, and rubies.

Ruby & Oscar: Known for accessible fine and contemporary jewellery, they carry men's statement rings featuring stones like London Blue Topaz, Sapphire, and Emerald set in solid silver or gold.

2. Vintage, Antique & Unique Statement Rings (Character & Heritage)

Hatton Garden (London) / The Jewellery Quarter (Birmingham): If you can visit in person, these historic districts are packed with independent jewellers who specialize in heavy men's signet rings featuring deep red Garnets, Rubies, Blue Sapphires, or Lapis Lazuli.

Etsy UK & Independent Makers: If you want something artisan-crafted, searching platforms like Etsy yields a vast array of UK-based independent silversmiths making men's heavy sterling silver signet or band rings with stones like Moss Agate, Labradorite, Amethyst, Tiger’s Eye, or Turquoise.

Popular Stone Choices for Men's Rings

Dark & Moody: Black Onyx, Hematite, or Black Obsidian give a sleek, masculine, understated look—frequently set into flat-topped signet rings.

Rich Blues & Greens: Deep blue sapphires, London blue topaz, or rich green emeralds offer a pop of color that pairs brilliantly with yellow gold or polished sterling silver.

Earth Tones: Tiger’s eye, malachite, or moss agate provide unique, organic patterns that lean heavily into modern men's streetwear and vintage aesthetics.
