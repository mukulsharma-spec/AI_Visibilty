Goldsmiths: If you are looking for luxury high-street pieces, Goldsmiths carries upscale options like 9ct/18ct Yellow Gold Ruby & Diamond Halo Studs (such as emerald-cut or oval variations starting around £750 upwards).

2. Independent & Specialist Boutiques

Imrays: Known for offering fine British-made pieces, such as 18ct Yellow Gold Natural Ruby Stud Earrings featuring natural round-cut rubies in classic six-claw settings (around £110–£870 depending on the carat weight and gold purity).

Berry’s Jewellers: A prominent luxury independent jeweller across the UK offering exquisite 18ct yellow gold ruby earrings, ideal if you are looking for premium investment pieces.

3. Online Marketplaces & Vintage Finds

eBay UK: If you are looking for either brand new British-hallmarked pieces or unique vintage/second-hand yellow gold and ruby cluster studs, eBay UK features dozens of verified independent UK sellers with 9ct and 14ct gold options starting from under £40 up to several hundred pounds.

What to Look For When Buying in the UK:

Hallmarking: Ensure any gold jewelry you buy carries a UK Hallmark (stamped by an official UK Assay Office like London, Birmingham, or Sheffield) to guarantee the purity of the gold (e.g., 375 for 9ct, 750 for 18ct).

Stone Type: Decide whether you want natural mined rubies or lab-created/synthetic rubies, as this heavily impacts the price tag while lab-created stones still offer real corundum chemical properties with flawless clarity.
