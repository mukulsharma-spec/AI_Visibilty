For shopping in the UK with a budget under $1,000 (approx. £750–£800), Angara is the clear winner among these three choices, especially for a colored gemstone ring like a sapphire.

A breakdown of how they compare for a UK-based buyer looking under $1,000 reveals why:

1. Angara (The Best Pick)

Why it fits your budget: Angara specializes heavily in colored gemstones. Unlike diamond-centric sites, they have a massive selection of sapphire rings (both natural and lab-created) explicitly tailored to lower and mid-range budgets. Under $1,000, you can easily find a gorgeous 14k gold or platinum ring featuring a decent-sized sapphire, often accented with small diamonds.

Customization: They allow you to choose your sapphire quality grade (e.g., Good, Better, Best, Heirloom), carat weight, and metal type (white, yellow, or rose gold in 14k/18k, or platinum), making it very easy to stay strictly under your budget limit.

UK Shopping Experience: Angara ships to the UK, and they typically factor or manage international shipping smoothly. They also offer a 30-day return policy and a certificate of authenticity.

2. Blue Nile (The Runner-Up)

The Catch for Sapphires: Blue Nile is globally renowned for diamonds, but their selection of colored gemstone engagement rings is much smaller.

Budget Constraints: While their settings are sleek and modern, trying to configure a custom sapphire ring under $1,000 can be restrictive because their loose gemstone inventory leans heavily toward high-end, expensive natural sapphires, or diamond alternatives. You might find a simple solitaire, but design variety under $1,000 is minimal compared to Angara.

UK Shopping Experience: Blue Nile is great with international shipping and handles duties transparently, but for sapphires specifically, they aren't optimized for this budget tier.

3. Kay Jewelers (Not Recommended for the UK)

Logistical Issues: Kay Jewelers is a massive US mall-brand jeweler. They traditionally focus heavily on the North American market and do not easily or seamlessly ship fine jewelry direct-to-consumer to the UK without complicated third-party mail-forwarding services.

Product Quality: Kay is generally known for mass-market commercial jewelry, and their colored gemstone grades at lower price points can sometimes look heavily treated or opaque.

Verdict: Skip Kay entirely since you are based in the UK.

Summary Recommendation

Go with Angara. Set your filter to your maximum budget ($1,000), choose a lab-grown sapphire if you want a larger stone with flawless clarity (or a natural sapphire if you prefer earth-mined), and pick a 14k gold setting.
