Shopping for a pear-shaped emerald engagement ring in the UK gives you a great mix of high-street options, online custom fine jewelers, and historic jewelry districts.

The best places to look depend on whether you want a bespoke design, an online storefront with wide customization, or an in-person shopping experience:

1. Dedicated Fine Jewelers & Online Specialists (Customizable)

Angara UK: A massive online selection dedicated entirely to fine gemstones. They feature a dedicated pear-emerald section where you can choose the exact stone quality grade, carat size, and metal type (platinum, yellow gold, white gold, etc.) with various halo, side-stone, or solitaire settings.

Diamond Heaven: With showrooms in major UK cities (including London, Birmingham, Manchester, and Glasgow), they specialize in diamond and gemstone rings and offer customizable platinum and gold pear-cut emerald rings.

GLAMIRA UK: Known for high customizability, allowing you to tweak every detail of a pear-shaped emerald ring—from the band width to stone accents—with free UK delivery.

2. London Shopping Hubs (In-Person Experience)

Hatton Garden (London): If you want to browse multiple jewellers in one afternoon, London’s historic jewelry quarter is packed with independent experts and bespoke makers (such as Simon Lewis Jewellers and many others) who can source loose pear-shaped emeralds and custom-build your ring from scratch.

Jay Nick (London): Known for specialized fine jewelry and multi-stone settings, offering various natural and lab-grown options with robust UK-based customer care.

3. Alternative & Independent Makers

Etsy UK: If you are looking for something unique, antique-inspired, art-deco, or botanical/leaf-motif (which pairs wonderfully with pear-shaped green stones), Etsy features hundreds of independent UK and international independent designers crafting solid gold and platinum pear emerald rings.

Tips for Buying an Emerald Ring:

Setting Protection: Pear shapes have a pointed tip that can be vulnerable to catching. Ensure the jeweler uses a V-prong or a secure bezel setting at the tip to protect the emerald.

Natural vs. Lab-Grown: Decide early on which route you prefer. Natural emeralds often feature natural inclusions (known as jardin) which give them unique character, whereas lab-grown emeralds offer a cleaner look at a lower price point.
