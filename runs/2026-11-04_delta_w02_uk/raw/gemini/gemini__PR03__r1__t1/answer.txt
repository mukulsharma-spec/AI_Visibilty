What to Look For When Buying Sapphire Studs in the UK:

Metal Choice: White gold is the most popular pairing for blue sapphires because it enhances the deep, rich blue of the stone. Yellow gold gives a more vintage, regal look (reminiscent of traditional royal styles).

Setting Type:

Prong/Claw settings let the maximum amount of light hit the sapphire, making it sparkle more.

Bezel settings encircle the stone in metal, offering a sleek, modern look and protecting the edges of the gemstone (ideal for daily wear).

Treatment & Origin: Most commercial sapphires are heat-treated to enhance their natural color and clarity—this is standard practice. If you prefer lab-grown sapphires (which are chemically identical to mined stones but more budget-friendly or larger for the price), brands like TJC also offer great selections.
