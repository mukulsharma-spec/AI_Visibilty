Here are some UK options, grouped roughly by budget and style.

Mainstream and mid-range gold or silver
- Goldsmiths has a citrine collection including gold citrine and diamond bracelets, and you can try pieces on in their UK stores.
- Ramsdens offers gold and silver citrine rings, earrings and pendants, with free standard UK delivery and a 14-day returns policy.
- The Fine Jewellery Company has a ladies’ citrine range with free, fully insured UK delivery.
- Whamond sells hand-crafted gold citrine jewellery and interest-free finance options.

Budget-friendly silver
- Silver Scene is an independent UK jeweller since 1999 with free UK delivery on orders over £30 and a free gift box.
- Michael Jones Jeweller has citrine pieces in 9ct gold, with free UK delivery over £50 and click and collect.

Something distinctive
- Sheila Fleet makes designer pieces such as a honeycomb silver ring with a 6mm citrine. They’re made to order in Orkney, taking about 2-3 weeks, with free 30-day returns.
- Jewellery Discovery specialises in modern, vintage and antique citrine pieces. Its prices run high, with the examples shown at several thousand pounds.

Tips
- Much commercial citrine is heat-treated amethyst, which is normal and fine. Ask if it matters to you, and check the listing says “natural citrine” rather than something vague like “golden quartz”.
- Look for a UK hallmark on gold and on silver items over 7.78g.
- Citrine is a durable quartz, so it suits everyday rings.
