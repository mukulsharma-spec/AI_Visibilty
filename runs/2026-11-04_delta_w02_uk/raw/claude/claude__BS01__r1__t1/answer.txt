When comparing, check these:

Treatment: Most sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal. Angara’s listings state it, for example “Heated” on the pear-shaped pair. Good retailers disclose it, so ask if a listing doesn’t. 
angara

Gold purity: 14ct is a bit harder-wearing and cheaper than 18ct. 18ct has a richer colour.
Centre stone size: Many of these have small stones, around 4mm. Check the carat weight and millimetre size so the earrings are what you expect.
Returns: Compare policies, since 60 days is more generous than many smaller jewellers.
