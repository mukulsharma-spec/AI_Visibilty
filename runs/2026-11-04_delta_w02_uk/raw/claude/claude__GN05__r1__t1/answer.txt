There isn’t one best place. It depends on whether you want fashion-style pieces, fine jewellery with natural stones, or specific high-quality stones.

Everyday and demi-fine coloured stone jewellery

- Astley Clarke and Monica Vinader are well-known UK-founded brands. A press release from Gemondo lists both among the top UK birthstone necklace brands. That release comes from a competitor, though, so take the ranking lightly. [cbinsights]
- Gemondo is a UK online jeweller specialising in genuine coloured gemstones. It sells pieces in 9ct gold and sterling silver, shipped from the UK. I only found its own marketing, so check independent reviews before buying. [cbinsights]

Fine jewellery with natural stones (emerald, ruby, sapphire)

- C W Sellors (Derbyshire) says it makes its natural and coloured gemstone jewellery in its own Peak District workshops. It also has regular sales. [cwsellors]
- Goldsmiths is a large UK retailer with a gemstone range online and in stores. It’s a safe choice if you like the option of seeing pieces in person.

Loose stones to set into custom jewellery

- Crystal Myths is a UK gemstone dealer serving both private buyers and trade clients, and says it can set its stones into custom-made jewellery. [crystalmyths]
- Topgem was recommended on a gem-collector forum as a UK dealer specialising in unheated sapphires and rubies. That’s an older forum post, so check it’s still trading and well reviewed. [purseblog]
- For tools and loose gems on a budget, an Evening Standard roundup lists Cooksongold and G.F. Williams & Co (a Hatton Garden dealer). [inkl]

What to check before buying

- Natural, lab-created or treated? Heat treatment is common and acceptable for most sapphires and rubies, but it should be disclosed. Unheated stones cost much more.
- Certification for expensive stones. For high-value pieces, look for a report from a lab such as the IGI or Gem-A. [sonnysjewellers]
- Hallmarking and returns. Gold and silver pieces above a certain weight should be hallmarked in the UK, and you should have a clear returns policy.
