Things to check before buying

  • Durability: Tanzanite is relatively soft, around 6.5 on the Mohs scale, so it scratches and chips more easily than sapphire or diamond. Halo, bezel or low-profile settings protect it better than a high, exposed solitaire. It’s best suited to someone who isn’t hard on their hands, or you can treat it as a ring to take off for the gym and gardening.
  • Colour: Look for a rich violet-blue with good saturation. Paler or greyish stones are cheaper but less striking.
  • Certification: For anything expensive, ask for an independent report (GIA is the best known). Most tanzanite is heat-treated, which is normal, but the seller should say so.
  • Returns and resizing: Check both policies, since the colour can look different in person than on screen.
