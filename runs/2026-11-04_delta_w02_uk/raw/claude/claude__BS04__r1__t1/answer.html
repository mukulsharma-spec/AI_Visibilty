Vintage or estate

  • 1stDibs has many pear emerald rings, including a 2.72 carat pear emerald platinum ring at about £3,590. Many sellers are overseas, so check import duties and ring sizing before buying. 1stdibs

Things to check

  • Pear points are fragile. Choose a setting with a V-prong or bezel protecting the tip, since emeralds chip more easily than diamonds.
  • Treatment and origin. Most emeralds are oiled. Ask whether the stone is natural or lab-grown, what treatment it has had, and where it comes from. For natural stones above roughly £2,000, ask for a certificate from GIA, SSEF or Gübelin.
  • Hallmarking. UK gold and platinum should carry an assay office hallmark.
  • Return policy. Check this before buying online, especially for bespoke or made-to-order pieces.
