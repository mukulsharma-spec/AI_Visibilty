Angara (UK site) has the widest selection for this exact style. It lists emerald-cut sapphire rings with side diamonds in several designs, and offers both natural and lab-grown stones. One option is a double claw-set emerald-cut sapphire in a diamond halo. A three-stone version with an emerald-cut blue sapphire between two round diamonds is priced around £2,829. There’s also a certified high-end option: a GIA-certified emerald-cut sapphire ring with diamonds at about £18,584 after a 10% discount. The “10% off” banners appear to be a standing offer, so don’t treat them as a special deal.

Rêve Diamonds has a design with an emerald-cut blue sapphire with side baguettes, listed from £4,000. It’s worth a look if you prefer baguette side stones and a UK-based jeweller.

Hancocks (London) is a long-established jeweller. It has a platinum three-stone ring with a rectangular blue sapphire and two emerald-cut diamonds. It’s a good choice if you want to see the ring in person or want help with something bespoke.

Finediam is a smaller UK seller. It lists an emerald-cut sapphire ring with baguette diamond side stones, available in 9K or 18K gold or platinum, with free resizing and 60-day returns. It’s a lower-budget option, but I’d check its reviews independently.

Whichever you pick:
- Treatment disclosure. Most sapphires are heat-treated. That’s normal, but it should be stated clearly, and it affects price.
- Certification. For a significant stone, ask for a report from GIA or a similar lab.
- Hallmarking. Gold and platinum sold in the UK should carry an Assay Office hallmark.
- Returns and resizing. Check the policy on both before buying.
- Photos. Ask for real photos or video of the exact stone, as sapphire colour varies a lot between listings.
