Hancocks Jewellers is a higher-end option, with prices from about £5,700 up to £20,000+. They deliver next day for orders Monday to Thursday processed before 3pm, but they don’t post on Fridays or Saturdays. That means a Friday order wouldn’t ship until Monday. Some of their rings are marked “available to take home today”, so if you’re near their London store you could buy in person.

Tips for a 2-day deadline:

Order early in the day and on a weekday. Cutoffs run from midday to 3pm, and weekend orders often don’t ship until Monday.
Check that your ring size is in stock before you commit. Resizing adds time, and a lot of the “in stock” labels apply only to certain sizes.
If you’re worried about timing, click and collect or buying in store removes the courier risk.
Make sure you can return it. Beaverbrooks and Johnsons both advertise return policies, but check the terms for engagement rings.
