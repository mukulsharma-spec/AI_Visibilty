Things to check before you buy

Treatment: Almost all affordable rubies are heat-treated, which is normal and accepted. Glass-filled (lead-glass filled) rubies are much less valuable and need more careful handling. A cheap eBay listing, for example, lists a ruby pendant at £62.99 with the treatment given as heating and glass filling. Ask the seller for the treatment type, or avoid anything that doesn’t state it. 
ebay

Natural or lab-grown: Lab-grown rubies are chemically real, but they’re a different product with a much lower value. Some brands, like Juvetti, whose Ori pendant is £462 and uses lab-grown stones, are upfront about this. That can be a good choice if you care about colour and quality rather than rarity, but make sure the listing says which you’re getting. 
juvetti

Metal: At this budget, look for hallmarked 9ct gold or sterling silver. Hallmarking is a UK legal requirement for gold, so it’s a useful check.
Returns: Buy from a seller with a clear returns policy, since colour is hard to judge from photos.
