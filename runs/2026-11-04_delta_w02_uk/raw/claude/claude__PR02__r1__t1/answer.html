Tips for staying under budget

Sapphire, ruby and emerald studs with small stones in 9ct gold or silver usually fit. Garnet, amethyst, topaz, peridot and citrine give you bigger, more colourful stones for less.
Look for a hallmark on the metal, and check whether the stone is natural, lab-created or treated. Reputable sellers will say which.
Include delivery and VAT in your comparison. UK retailers’ prices already include VAT, whereas US sites may add import charges.
