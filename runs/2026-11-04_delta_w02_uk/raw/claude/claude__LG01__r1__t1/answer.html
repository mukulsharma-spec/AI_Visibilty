Tips for buying:

Ask whether the stone is lab-grown (synthetic) sapphire and what metal it’s set in. Lab-grown sapphire has the same physical, optical and chemical properties as natural sapphire, so it’s a real sapphire, just made in a lab.
Ask for a certificate or grading report if you’re buying a larger loose stone. This matters most for engagement rings.
Check the returns policy. UK online purchases generally give you a 14-day cancellation window, but jewellers vary on resizing and custom orders.
For solid gold, look for a hallmark.
