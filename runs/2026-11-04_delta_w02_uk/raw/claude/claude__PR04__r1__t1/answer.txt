Created (lab-grown) emeralds

Very.co.uk stocks Created Brilliance, and its 9ct yellow gold created emerald solitaire is £700 and the trilogy is £800. A created emerald has the same chemistry as a mined one, with cleaner colour and clarity, and it costs much less. It’s a good option if you want a bigger, brighter stone.

Vintage

Dealers like From Edinburgh With Love sell hallmarked vintage pieces. One listing is an Art Deco-inspired 18ct gold ring with a 0.38ct emerald and six diamonds, free to resize. I couldn’t see its price, so check it against your budget. Vintage often gives you more gold and character for the money. 
fromedinburghwithlove


Tips for staying under budget

Choose 9ct gold over 18ct. It’s cheaper and tougher, which helps because emeralds are fairly brittle.
Go for a smaller stone, around 0.25 to 0.5ct. Price climbs quickly with size and quality.
Ask whether the emerald is natural or created, and whether it’s been treated. Most natural emeralds are oiled, which is normal, but the seller should say so.
Check for a UK hallmark, and look at the returns policy and resizing options before you buy.
