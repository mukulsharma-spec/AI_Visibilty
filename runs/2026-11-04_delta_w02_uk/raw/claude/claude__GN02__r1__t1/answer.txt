Here are some well-regarded UK options, grouped by what you might be after.

High street and established retailers


Goldsmiths, Mappin & Webb, Beaverbrooks, Ernest Jones: wide ranges, certified diamonds, and frequent sales.

Boodles, Garrard, Annoushka, Pragnell: British luxury houses, with higher prices and strong craftsmanship.



Modern and design-led fine jewellery


Monica Vinader, Astrid & Miyu, Missoma: more accessible, with solid gold and gold vermeil lines. Check which is which before buying.

Mejuri: ships to the UK, with solid gold everyday pieces.



Diamonds and engagement rings


Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, Cred Jewellery: good for choosing a certified stone and a custom setting. Cred focuses on ethical sourcing.

Hatton Garden (London) jewellers such as Vashi and Steven Stone: often better value than the high street, and many sell online.



Antique and vintage


The Antique Jewellery Company, Lang Antiques, 1stDibs: unique pieces and often better value for the quality.



Tips for buying online in the UK


Check for a hallmark. Gold over 1g, platinum over 0.5g and silver over 7.78g must be hallmarked by a UK Assay Office, so ask for hallmark photos if they’re not shown.

Look for independent certification (GIA, IGI or HRD) on any significant diamond.

Know your returns rights. Most online purchases have a 14-day cancellation window, but bespoke or made-to-order items are usually excluded.

Check the reviews on Trustpilot and Google, and confirm insured, tracked delivery.
