There are several good UK options at different price points.

Alison Moore Designs (her own site and Not On The High Street) is a good fit if you want to build a stack. Her “create your own” set mixes cabochon gemstone rings, comes with two free stacking bands, and has free UK postage over £75. Single rings come in 3mm, 4mm, 6mm and 8mm stone sizes. On Not On The High Street, a single 6mm sterling silver gemstone stacking ring is £43.

Gemondo is another option, and its stackable collection includes 9ct gold and sterling silver pieces, with a sale on selected gemstone jewellery.

Jewlr (UK site) suits you if you want personalised or birthstone rings. It has 167 stackable ring styles, including engravable birthstone bands. Prices run higher than the options above, with many styles from around £110 to £250. 
jewlr


For higher-end or in-person shopping, independent jewellers and the main high-street chains also stock stacking bands, and you can try sizes on in store.

Before you buy, check whether the metal is solid gold, sterling silver or gold plated, since that affects both price and how well the ring wears. Also confirm whether stones are natural or lab-grown, and your exact ring size, because stacked rings can fit differently from single ones.
