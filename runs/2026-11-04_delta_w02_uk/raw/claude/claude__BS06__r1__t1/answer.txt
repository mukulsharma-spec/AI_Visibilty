Here are the main places to look in the UK, depending on budget and style.

High-street and large chains (easy to try on, with good returns and aftercare)
- Beaverbrooks, Ernest Jones, Goldsmiths, Fraser Hart and H. Samuel all stock sapphire and diamond trilogy rings, from entry-level to mid-range.
- Warren James is often cheaper for simpler designs.

Online specialists (usually better value and more choice of stones)
- Angelic Diamonds and Taylor & Hart are UK-based and offer customisable rings.
- Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth ship to the UK and let you pick the stones and setting. Check for import duties and VAT before you order.

Hatton Garden, London
- Lots of independent jewellers in one area, with room to negotiate. You can often commission a custom three-stone ring here.

Luxury
- Boodles, Mappin & Webb and Garrard sell high-end sapphire and diamond trilogies.

Vintage and antique
- Antique dealers, auction houses and Etsy or eBay sellers are good for Art Deco and Edwardian three-stone rings, which are often better value for the quality.

Things worth checking before you buy
- Sapphire treatment: Most sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal. Unheated stones cost much more and should come with a lab report.
- Certification: For the diamonds, ask for a GIA, IGI or similar report, especially on larger stones.
- Hallmarking: Gold and platinum should carry a UK hallmark.
- Returns: Online purchases generally give you a 14-day cancellation right, but custom-made rings may be excluded.
- Insurance: Consider insuring the ring from day one.
