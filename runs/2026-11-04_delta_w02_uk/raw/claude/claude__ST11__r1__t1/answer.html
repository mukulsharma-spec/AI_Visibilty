There are plenty of good options in the UK, depending on budget and style.

High street and mainstream

  • Goldsmiths, Ernest Jones, H. Samuel are widely available in-store and online, with a range of ruby pendants at mid-range prices.
  • Beaverbrooks is a well-regarded UK chain with a good selection of gemstone pendants and solid customer service.
  • Pandora and Swarovski are worth a look for lower budgets, but they often use simulated or lab-created stones rather than natural rubies.
  • John Lewis and Argos have affordable options and easy returns.

Online specialists

  • Angara has lots of ruby pendants in different settings and lets you customise metal and stone quality.
  • Blue Nile UK and Brilliant Earth are good for higher quality stones with certification.
  • Etsy UK has independent makers and vintage-style pieces, but check seller reviews carefully.

Luxury and independent

  • Hatton Garden in London has independent jewellers and dealers who can make or customise a piece for you.
  • Mappin & Webb, Garrard, and Boodles are for high-end pieces.
  • Antique and vintage dealers, or auction houses such as Bonhams and Sotheby’s, are good for something distinctive.

Things to check before buying

  • Natural, lab-created, or treated: Most natural rubies are heat-treated, which is normal. Some cheaper ones are glass-filled, which is less durable and worth less. Reputable sellers will disclose this.
  • Metal hallmark: Gold and silver sold in the UK should carry a UK hallmark.
  • Certification: For anything expensive, ask for a gemological report from a lab such as GIA, IGI, or SSEF.
  • Return policy: UK online purchases generally have a 14-day cancellation right, but check the seller’s terms.
