There are plenty of UK options, depending on whether you want real ruby jewellery or something ruby-themed.

For jewellery with genuine rubies

Honey Willow is a UK handmade jewellery brand with ruby-set pieces. A gladiolus flower necklace in recycled 9kt gold set with a ruby is £582. A sterling silver double-ring ruby necklace is £175.

Goldsmiths and The Diamond Store are established jewellers. Rock My Wedding’s recent roundup of ruby anniversary gifts features pieces from The Diamond Store, including ruby pendants.
C W Sellors is a specialist jeweller in Derbyshire. They offer 40th anniversary jewellery set with natural gemstones and diamonds, and you can phone or WhatsApp their team to ask about options. 
cwsellors


For more affordable or personalised gifts

Not On The High Street has 133 products in its 40th anniversary ruby range, from personalised posters (around £17) to jewellery. Pretty Serendipity’s sterling silver necklace with four rings, one for each decade is £75.

Heads and Tails Jewellery makes necklaces, cufflinks and keyrings from genuine coins issued in 1985, which fits a couple who married in 1985 or 1986.

For non-jewellery ideas
Roundups from Yours and For Better For Worse suggest personalised champagne, a ruby rose that lasts, art prints, perfume, and experience days (Virgin Experience Days). A watch, whisky or cufflinks would suit him.
