October has two birthstones, opal and pink tourmaline, so you can shop for either. Here are UK options by budget.

Fine jewellery (gold and diamond)

Allum & Sidaway has an October birthstones collection. It includes pieces like an 18ct yellow gold opal and diamond necklace and an 18ct rose gold pink tourmaline and diamond stud earrings, with some items on sale. 
allumandsidaway

Banks Lyon stocks opal and tourmaline jewellery in store and online, with 0% finance on selected items from £500. 
bankslyon

Kiki McDonough (kiki.co.uk) makes a birthstone necklace collection. The October design features a bright pink tourmaline on a delicate gold chain. 
luxurylondon

Chupi (uk.chupi.com) was featured in Luxury London’s October roundup for its opal pieces, such as an opal and diamond ring.

Mid-range and personalised

Alison Moore offers free UK postage on orders over £50. Her range includes pink tourmaline and opal birthstone rings and pendants, with some pendants from about £63 to £105. 
alisonmoore

Jewlr (jewlr.com/uk) does custom October birthstone pieces. It offers opal and pink tourmaline rings, necklaces and bracelets, including engravable designs. Prices run higher, with many rings in the £130 to £500 range. 
jewlr

Budget-friendly and gifts

Niche Jewellery has a large October collection, including Nomination charms and Kit Heath necklaces. Free UK delivery applies on orders over £49, and next-day delivery is available. Many of its designs are only inspired by the stones, using crystals or enamel instead. Rose-pink crystal or zirconia is a popular choice if you’re on a budget. 
nichejewellery

Check whether a piece uses genuine opal or tourmaline, or a lookalike stone. Product descriptions usually say, and it makes a big difference to the price. Prices and sale offers also change, so confirm them on each site.
