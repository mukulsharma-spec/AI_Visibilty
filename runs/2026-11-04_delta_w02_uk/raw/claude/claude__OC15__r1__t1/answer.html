Tips

Personalised and made-to-order pieces are the risky ones, because making time comes before shipping. Order those by early December at the latest, and now if you can.
Pick a retailer that offers a guaranteed next-day or express service at checkout. Some also have extended Christmas returns, which helps if the stone or length isn’t right.
If you want something with no making delay, a non-personalised, in-stock necklace is the safest bet.
