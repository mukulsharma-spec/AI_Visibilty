Tips

Check the fine print. Look at the time limit, how many sizes are covered, and whether the free resize is one-off. Some rings are hard to resize, especially those with stones set all the way round the band, or in titanium, tungsten or steel. A plain or part-set band in gold or platinum is the safest choice. 
angelicdiamonds

Pick a starting size. The average UK woman’s ring size is between L and N, with N the most commonly cited. Sources differ a little, though: Sonny’s says L is the most common. If you can’t narrow it down, it’s usually safer to go slightly bigger, since resizing down is easier and cheaper than resizing up. 
bridebook

revediamonds

Look for clues. If she wears rings already, you can borrow one when she’s not around and have a jeweller measure it. A friend or family member might also know her size.
