Based on what I found, Taylor & Hart has the longest window, with a few caveats:

Taylor & Hart: 180-day returns is advertised across the site. Their promo pages list this as one of the Ruby Club perks (alongside a one-year resize period and a lifetime warranty), so check on the checkout page whether it applies automatically or needs the Ruby Club.

Lily Arkwright: 50-day returns, with free insured shipping and returns and a free one-year resize. They also sell lab-grown sapphires, which tend to be cheaper.

Avita Jewellery: 60 days from delivery for standard purchases, but bespoke, engraved and resized rings aren’t eligible.

The Diamond Store: free 30-day returns for refunds and 60-day exchanges.

Tiffany: 30 days from purchase for refund or exchange, provided the ring is in saleable condition with all documentation.

Berganza (antique rings): 14 days for unseen purchases, the shortest of the group.

Across nearly all of them, bespoke, engraved or resized rings usually can’t be returned. Don’t resize or engrave until you’re sure. It’s also worth confirming the policy on the specific ring’s page before you buy, since terms can change and some apply only to standard stock.
