Things to know about quality grades

The A to AAAA scale is a retailer’s own system, not an industry standard. “AAA” at one shop can differ from “AAA” at another, so compare actual colour, clarity and cut details rather than the letters alone.
For higher-value stones, ask for an independent lab report, such as Gem-A, SSEF, Gübelin or GIA. Also ask whether the stone is natural or lab-grown, and whether it’s treated (heated, for example, which is common for sapphires and rubies).
For a larger purchase, consider visiting a Hatton Garden jeweller in London. You can see stones in person, and many will source a specific stone and set it for you.
