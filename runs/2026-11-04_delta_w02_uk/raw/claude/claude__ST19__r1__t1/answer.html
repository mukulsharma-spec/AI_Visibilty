Here are some good places to look in the UK, grouped by what you might want.

High-street jewellers with online shops

  • Goldsmiths has a dedicated ruby ring range online, plus UK showrooms if you’d like to see one in person.
  • Ramsdens sells ruby jewellery online with Click & Collect and a 14-day returns period.

Online specialists

  • Shining Diamonds has ruby and diamond rings, including finance options on pricier pieces.
  • The Diamond Store has ruby rings from about £219 for dainty styles to a few thousand pounds for eternity rings.
  • Juvetti is a London brand that sells lab-grown ruby rings, from around £250.
  • Hatton Garden jewellers, such as The Diamond Ring Company, offer bespoke and try-before-you-buy options.

Antique, vintage and second-hand

  • 1stDibs lists vintage and antique ruby rings from UK dealers, including ones in Mayfair, Birmingham and Newcastle.
  • eBay UK has a huge range, from a few pounds to several thousand. Prices at the low end usually mean lab-created or glass-filled stones, so check the listing carefully.

Tips for buying a ruby online

  • Treatment and origin: most rubies are heat-treated, which is normal. Untreated stones cost much more, so the seller should say which you’re getting.
  • Natural or lab-grown: lab-grown rubies are chemically identical and far cheaper. Make sure the listing states which it is.
  • Certification: for anything over a few hundred pounds, ask for a lab report from a body such as GIA or SSEF.
  • Hallmark: UK gold and platinum jewellery should carry a hallmark. Check the metal and size, too.
  • Returns: look for at least a 14-day return window.
