Two UK jewellers offer free next-day delivery on gemstone pieces. Others have free delivery but not fast delivery.

Free next-day delivery

Berry’s Jewellers: Free UK next-day delivery applies to orders placed before 3pm, Monday to Thursday, and there’s a 14-day full refund policy on online purchases. One product page gives a slightly different cut-off, saying orders placed before 3pm Monday to Friday arrive before 4pm the following day. Check the delivery details at checkout. They stock a wide range of sapphire, ruby, emerald, tanzanite and tourmaline pieces, mostly at the higher end (rings and pendants roughly £1,400 to £3,400 and up).

CW Sellors: Their gemstone collection page mentions free next day delivery on all orders. A promotional listing adds a condition: orders placed before 4pm qualify when you spend over £75. Confirm the current terms before buying.

Free delivery, but not fast

John Lewis: Standard delivery is free on orders over £50 and takes 2 to 5 days. Next or named day delivery costs £7.95. It stocks brands such as Monica Vinader.

Ramsdens: They offer free UK delivery and 14-day returns as standard, but the page doesn't state a delivery speed. They sell new and pre-owned stone jewellery and lab-created gems, which suits a smaller budget.
