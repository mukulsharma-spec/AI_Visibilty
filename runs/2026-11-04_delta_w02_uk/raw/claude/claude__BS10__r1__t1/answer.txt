Your best bet depends on the stone and budget. 14ct is less common in the UK than 9ct or 18ct, so fewer high street jewellers stock it, but these retailers do.

For coloured gemstones (sapphire, emerald, ruby)

Angara UK has the widest choice. Its 14ct pendants come in several stone quality grades, from good to heirloom, with a free 14ct gold chain. Prices are quoted including VAT. A 5mm round sapphire solitaire in 14ct yellow gold is one example. It also offers lab-grown rubies and other stones. Stones are often heat-treated, which is normal for sapphire, and the product page lists this. Prices climb quickly with quality grade, so compare the “better” and “best” options.

Before you buy

Check the piece carries a UK hallmark (585 for 14ct), which is legally required for gold over a certain weight.
Ask whether the stone is natural or lab-grown, and whether it’s treated. This changes price far more than the metal does.
Check the returns window. TJC lists 30-day returns, and Astley Clarke offers the same.
