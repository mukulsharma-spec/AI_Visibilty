Your best bet depends on the stone and budget. 14ct is less common in the UK than 9ct or 18ct, so fewer high street jewellers stock it, but these retailers do.

For coloured gemstones (sapphire, emerald, ruby)

  • Angara UK has the widest choice. Its 14ct pendants come in several stone quality grades, from good to heirloom, with a free 14ct gold chain. Prices are quoted including VAT. A 5mm round sapphire solitaire in 14ct yellow gold is one example. It also offers lab-grown rubies and other stones. Stones are often heat-treated, which is normal for sapphire, and the product page lists this. Prices climb quickly with quality grade, so compare the “better” and “best” options. 24/7 Customer Support +2
  • Taylor & Hart is a UK jeweller with a Covent Garden shop. Its Amore necklaces have bezel-set heart-shaped sapphires in 14ct yellow gold, from £1,020. It costs more but suits you if you’d like to see the piece in person. taylorandhart

For a dainty everyday pendant, mostly lab-grown diamond

  • Astley Clarke sells a solid 14ct gold solitaire with a 4mm lab-grown diamond on a 16" chain from £795, with a two-year warranty and free UK delivery over £75. astleyclarke
  • Astrid & Miyu has a 14k solid gold lab-grown diamond solitaire pendant on a 38cm chain with a 5cm extension. It also has a King’s Cross shop and free UK delivery. astridandmiyu

For a lower budget

  • TJC sells 14k pieces at much lower prices, such as a 14k yellow gold diamond solitaire pendant with a 4mm stone for £299.99. The stones are lower grade (I1 clarity in that example), so it’s good value but not top quality. tjc

Before you buy

  • Check the piece carries a UK hallmark (585 for 14ct), which is legally required for gold over a certain weight.
  • Ask whether the stone is natural or lab-grown, and whether it’s treated. This changes price far more than the metal does.
  • Check the returns window. TJC lists 30-day returns, and Astley Clarke offers the same. tjc
