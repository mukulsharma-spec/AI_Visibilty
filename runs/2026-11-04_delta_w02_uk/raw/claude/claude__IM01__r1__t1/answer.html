Here are some oval blue sapphire halo rings in white gold from UK retailers, ranging from 9ct to 18ct.

Prices vary a lot with centre stone size and gold purity, from a few thousand pounds for smaller 9ct or 18ct rings to over £10,000 for large, high-grade stones. Prices and stock can change, so check each retailer’s page.

  • Gold purity: 18ct white gold is the better choice for an everyday ring. It is richer in precious metal and more hypoallergenic. 9ct is cheaper and harder-wearing but less white.
  • Sapphire quality: Colour matters most. A rich, medium-deep blue is generally preferred over very pale or very dark stones. Ask whether the sapphire is natural, heat-treated (very common) or lab-grown, and whether it has a certificate such as GIA for larger stones.
  • Halo style: A single halo looks classic, while a double halo or floral halo looks bolder. Shoulder diamonds add sparkle but also cost.
