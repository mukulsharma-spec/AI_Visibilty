Are they worth it?

Yes, if you’re buying for looks and durability rather than as an investment. A lab-grown sapphire is real corundum with the same chemistry as a mined one. Lab-grown sapphires are rated 9 on the Mohs scale, so they’re tough enough for an everyday ring, and the naked eye generally can’t tell lab-grown from mined. You also typically get a bigger, cleaner stone for the money, and without the supply-chain concerns of mining.

The main downside is resale value. Lab-grown stones don’t hold value the way natural ones can, and you shouldn’t expect to get much back if you sell. Buy it to wear and enjoy.

What to check before buying
Colour and clarity: Look for a deep, even blue rather than very pale or very dark. Check reviews or ask for close-up photos or video.
Certification: For bigger stones, ask whether there’s a grading report, and make sure the listing clearly says “lab-grown” or “created”.
Metal: Solid 9ct, 14k or 18ct gold is better than plated metal for something you’ll wear daily.
Returns: Prefer sellers with at least a 30-day return window so you can judge the stone in person.
