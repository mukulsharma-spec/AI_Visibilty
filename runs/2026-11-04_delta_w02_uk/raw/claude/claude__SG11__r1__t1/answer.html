Choosing between them

Antique rings often have small emeralds (0.5ct or under is common), and real Deco geometry like step-cut shoulders and baguette diamonds is hard to replicate exactly. New builds let you choose a bigger or better-coloured stone.
Emeralds are commonly oiled or filled, which is normal. Ask whether the stone has been treated and what the origin is. A reputable dealer will tell you.
Check the hallmarks (platinum, 18ct) and ask about the ring’s condition and any repairs. Antique shanks can be thin.
Resizing is usually easy, but ask first, since some Deco designs don’t resize well.
