There are several good routes in the UK, depending on your budget and how much you want to customise.

High street and large retailers

Goldsmiths has a selection of sapphire rings for sale both online and in stores across the UK. It’s the easiest place to try rings on and get resizing or repairs done later. [goldsmiths]

Hancocks (hancocksjewellers.co.uk) has a dedicated sapphire engagement ring section.

Austen Blake lets you find and design your sapphire engagement ring online or book an appointment at one of their UK stores. They say UK delivery is free, with 2-3 weeks for standard orders and 4 weeks for customised ones. [austenblake]

Specialists in coloured stones

C W Sellors describes itself as one of the UK’s leading diamond and precious gemstone specialists, with a large sapphire range. [cwsellors]

Holts Gems (London) offers sapphire engagement rings and a bespoke route if you want something custom.

Jewellery Discovery lists a mix of sapphire and diamond rings, with prices from about £5,950 for a 2.14ct total cluster ring in 18ct white gold up to far more for rare pieces. [jewellerydiscovery]

Vintage and antique

Mozeris (Mayfair and Essex) specialises in antique sapphire rings. They offer viewing by appointment in London and Essex plus insured delivery across the UK. For antiques, ask about condition, previous repairs and insurance. [mozerisfineantiques]

Bespoke

Hatton Garden in London has many workshops that will make a ring around a sapphire you choose. This is often the best way to get the exact stone and setting you want.

Budget

One UK specialist says many clients set a budget from around £1,000 upwards for a quality sapphire diamond ring, with rarer stones costing more. For comparison, the average UK engagement ring spend is reported at around £3,928. [mozerisfineantiques] [revediamonds]

What to check before buying

Heat treatment: Most sapphires are heated, which is normal and accepted. Unheated stones cost significantly more, so make sure the listing says which you’re getting.

Origin and certification: For stones of any size, ask for a report from a lab such as GIA, SSEF or Gübelin. Origin (Ceylon, Burma, Kashmir) affects price a lot.

Colour over size: A vivid, evenly coloured stone matters more than carat weight. Very dark or washed-out stones look less attractive.

Durability: Sapphire is very hard (9 on the Mohs scale), so it suits everyday wear.

Lab-grown: These are much cheaper if you’re open to them. Make sure the listing says whether the stone is natural or lab-grown.
