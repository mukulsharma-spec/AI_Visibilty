The “best of” lists I found for UK engagement ring shops are mostly written by retailers or affiliate sites, so I wouldn’t treat any of them as neutral rankings. The names below come up repeatedly, grouped by what they’re good for. The main source I read is from late 2023, so check current reviews and policies before buying.

Online-first, wide choice and good prices

Blue Nile UK has a big catalogue of diamonds and settings, and shows prices with VAT included at checkout. It’s a good place to benchmark prices even if you buy elsewhere.

Rêve Diamonds (Hatton Garden) and The Diamond Ring Company (also Hatton Garden) sell online and let you view in person by appointment. Both stock GIA or IGI certified stones.

Bespoke or made-to-order

Taylor & Hart (London) is known for bespoke work and strong customer service, including video consultations.

Samara James (Surrey) has good online tools, like videos and 3D views of rings, plus a showroom.

Blair and Sheridan (Glasgow), Mitchel & Co. (Birmingham) and Brooklinde (Cardiff) are independents. Brooklinde is in-person only.

Lab-grown or alternative stones

Lily Arkwright focuses on lab-grown diamonds, moissanite and coloured gemstones, and is primarily online.

Before you buy

Certification: for a diamond over about 0.30ct, ask for a GIA or IGI report. It lets you compare stones across retailers properly.

Returns: UK online purchases generally get a 14-day cancellation right, but made-to-order and bespoke rings are usually excluded. Check this first if the ring is a surprise and you may need to resize or swap it.

Sizing: ask about free resizing, and whether it’s included after purchase.

Insurance: get it sorted for the ring from day one. Many home policies need it listed separately.

Hatton Garden: even if you buy online, a short visit to compare stones in person can be worthwhile.
