For a bespoke ring, you could also visit a Hatton Garden jeweller in London or the Jewellery Quarter in Birmingham. They can make a band in your size, metal and sapphire colour.

A few things to consider when choosing:

Setting: Sapphire is very hard (9 on the Mohs scale), but a flush or channel setting protects it best for everyday wear.
Metal: Platinum, white gold and palladium suit blue sapphire well. Yellow gold gives a warmer, more traditional look.
Hallmarking: In the UK, gold, silver and platinum items over a minimum weight must be hallmarked. Check this for any imported or second-hand piece.
Colour: Stones vary from pale to deep blue, so look at photos in daylight or ask for extra images.
