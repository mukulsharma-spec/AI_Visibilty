Second-hand and one-off pieces

eBay UK has everything from cheap silver pieces to solid 9ct gold. Check seller reviews and ask whether the opal is natural or lab-created, since some listings, especially the very cheap ones, are faux opal.

Things to check when buying

Opal type: natural, Ethiopian (Welo), boulder and lab-created opals look and cost quite differently. Reputable sellers should state which one it is.
Metal: sterling silver is cheapest, gold-plated silver sits in the middle, and solid 9ct or 18ct gold costs the most. Plating can wear off over time.
Returns: opals vary a lot in colour play, so a free returns policy is worth having.
Care: opals are soft and sensitive to knocks and harsh chemicals, so a pendant suits them better than a ring.
