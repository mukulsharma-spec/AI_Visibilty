Angara is the better pick for you, mainly because you’re in the UK.

Angara has a dedicated UK site with prices in pounds including VAT, so there are no import surprises. It lists free express shipping and free return shipping within the UK. Its UK pages disagree on the return window, though: one says 60 days from delivery and another says 30 days, so check the page for your exact ring before buying. Every piece is custom-made after your order is placed, so allow production time. The catch is price. A fairly modest oval sapphire ring with diamond accents was listed at £1,439, well over a $1,000 budget (roughly £750-800, depending on the exchange rate). To stay under it, you’d likely need a smaller stone, 9ct gold, or a simpler setting without diamond accents. [24/7 Customer Support +4]

Blue Nile can ship to the UK, with free shipping and VAT included in the displayed price according to a UK buying guide. Returns are free within 30 days in principle, but one source says international customers cover return shipping and any customs duties. It’s also shipped from the US, so customs can go wrong. One recent UK reviewer described a ring held up in customs and sent back to the US, which added weeks. Its sapphire range is also smaller than Angara’s, since it focuses mostly on diamonds. [Blue Nile UK Guide +2]

Kay is the weakest fit. It’s a US chain, and I couldn’t find confirmation that it ships to the UK. You’d likely need a freight forwarder, which adds cost, duties, and complications with returns and warranty. I wouldn’t bother.

My advice:

- Start with Angara’s UK site and filter to your budget. Look at 9ct gold and smaller (4-5mm) sapphires, and check whether the stone grade shown (e.g. “AAA”) fits what you want.
- Compare against Blue Nile only if it has a specific ring you love. Get the duty and VAT position and return costs in writing first.
- Whichever you pick, confirm the total price including VAT and delivery before checkout, and check the sapphire’s treatment. Heat treatment is standard and fine, but it should be disclosed.

Do you want a particular style (solitaire, halo, three-stone) or a ring size in mind? That would help narrow down what’s realistic under your budget.
