The best option depends on what “custom” means to you. Many of the “best of” pages in my search were written by the brands themselves, so I’d treat them as marketing and check independent reviews (Trustpilot, Google) before ordering.

Personalised pieces (names, initials, engraving, birthstones)


Jewlr has a UK site where you pick the metal, gemstones and engraving. You can choose genuine or simulated gemstones, a choice of metals and custom engravings. It’s a good middle ground between price and flexibility. 

Not On The High Street is a marketplace of small UK makers with plenty of personalised jewellery, which makes it good for gifts. Quality varies by seller, so check each shop’s reviews.

Merci Maman and Posh Totty Designs are well-known UK brands for engraved and personalised pieces.



One-off designs from independent makers


Etsy UK is often recommended for flexibility. One roundup says custom pieces usually take 1–3 weeks to make. Choose sellers with lots of reviews and clear photos of past custom work. 



Fully bespoke (engagement rings, fine jewellery, heirloom redesigns)


London’s Hatton Garden jewellers are the main hub for this. Many offer online consultations, so you can work with designers remotely. Taylor & Hart is a well-known name for bespoke engagement rings. 

Expect a slower process. One guide estimates 2 to 6 weeks for premium studios, depending on complexity and gemstone sourcing. Ask for a CAD or 3D preview before they start making. 



Tips


Ask what’s included in the price, such as hallmarking, certification for diamonds, and resizing.

Check the returns policy. Made-to-order items are often non-refundable, even though UK consumer law lets you cancel most online orders within 14 days.

Confirm that metals are properly hallmarked. In the UK, gold and silver items above a certain weight must be hallmarked by law.
