There are a few routes, depending on whether you want genuinely antique pieces or new rings made in a vintage style.

Genuine antique and vintage rings

Berganza (Hatton Garden, London) says it specialises in ancient, antique and vintage engagement rings, and it has a selection of vintage-style rings under £5,000.

Gatsby Jewellery has a vintage gemstone range with sapphires, emeralds, rubies and more, including pieces from the Georgian, Victorian, Edwardian and Art Deco periods.

Parkin & Gerrish is a UK-curated seller of 500+ one-of-a-kind engagement rings and fine estate jewellery, including sapphire, emerald and ruby pieces.

New rings in a vintage style

High-street options: H. Samuel and Ernest Jones both have vintage-style ranges, including sapphire and ruby designs. Ernest Jones notes that Kate Middleton’s sapphire ring boosted interest in vintage-style sapphire and other coloured-gemstone halo designs. These tend to be easier on budget, with less choice in unusual stones.

Tips

With genuine antiques, ask for a gemmological report. Ernest Jones points out that this gives at least some peace of mind. 
ernestjones

Ask whether the stone is natural or lab-created, and whether it has been treated (heat treatment is common in sapphires and rubies).
Check that sellers offer a returns policy, resizing and insurance valuations. Antique rings can be harder to resize depending on the setting.
Hatton Garden is worth visiting in person if you’re near London. Several of the shops above are there, and you can compare stones side by side.
