Some things to check when comparing, since prices alone don’t tell you much:

Natural or lab-grown: retailers usually state this, so make sure it’s natural if that matters to you. Some listings, like the Ernest Jones one, don’t make it obvious.
Stone size: compare the millimetre size, not just the carat weight. Total weight for a pair can mislead.
Colour: sapphire studs with a deep, saturated blue are worth more than pale or very dark ones, so look at customer photos if you can.
Setting: claw settings let more light in. Rub-over (bezel) settings protect the stone better for everyday wear.
