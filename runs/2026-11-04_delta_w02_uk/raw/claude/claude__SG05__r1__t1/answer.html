Tips for buying

Look for the hallmark. In the UK, solid gold items over a small weight threshold should carry a hallmark, so a listing that says “solid 9ct/14ct/18ct” and mentions hallmarking is a good sign. If it just says “gold”, it may be plated or vermeil. As a rule of thumb, if a brand doesn’t explicitly say “solid 14k” or “solid 18k”, assume it isn’t. 
fashionabc

9ct is the most affordable and hardwearing for daily wear. 14ct and 18ct have richer colour but cost more and are slightly softer. For tiny gemstones that you’ll wear every day, 9ct or 14ct is a sensible choice.
Check the gemstone setting and type. Bezel or claw settings hold up better than glued ones. Also check whether stones are natural or lab-grown, since that changes the price a lot.
Prices and ranges change often. Confirm current stock, returns policy and delivery on each site before buying.
