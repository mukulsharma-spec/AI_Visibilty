Opal is soft, so choose carefully. It sits at about 5.5–6.5 on the Mohs scale, compared with 10 for diamond, and it can chip or scratch with daily wear. Rings with a bezel or halo setting protect the stone better than a high, exposed claw setting. Ask any seller about the opal’s origin (Australian, Ethiopian and so on), whether it’s natural or lab-created, and if it’s a solid opal or a doublet or triplet, which is layered and less durable. Also ask about their aftercare and resizing policies.
