A few things to check when comparing:

Emerald quality: Look for a stated grade (Angara uses AAA/AAAA) and, ideally, a lab certificate. Emeralds nearly always have visible inclusions, so judge colour and clarity by eye with photos or video.
Treatment: Most emeralds are oil-treated. Ask about this, and ask about the care that goes with it, since emeralds are more fragile than diamonds and are best protected in a lower-profile setting.
Side stone shape: Baguettes or tapered baguettes give a clean, art deco look with emerald cuts. Rounds, trillions or half-moons are softer.
Returns: UK distance-selling rules help with online purchases, but custom or made-to-order rings are often excluded, so check the retailer’s policy first.
