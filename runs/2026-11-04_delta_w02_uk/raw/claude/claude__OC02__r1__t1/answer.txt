Amethyst is a lovely choice, and there are plenty of places to buy it in the UK, depending on your budget and whether you want to shop in person.

High-street jewellers (easy to browse, with shops to visit)

Goldsmiths has a range of amethyst and diamond necklaces, rings and earrings, which you can buy online or try in one of their UK stores. 
goldsmiths

Beaverbrooks stocks amethyst rings, necklaces, earrings and pendants, with next-day delivery across the UK on orders over £75. 
beaverbrooks

H. Samuel has a wide selection in sterling silver and 9ct gold, including a sterling silver diamond and amethyst February birthstone earring design. 
hsamuel


Independent and online jewellers (often better value)

Silverscene is an independent UK jeweller since 1999, with free UK delivery on orders over £30 and a free gift box on any order. It’s good for sterling silver teardrop earrings and rings. 
silverscene

The Fine Jewellery Company offers sterling silver and 9 or 18 carat gold in white, rose and yellow, with free UK delivery. 
thefinejewellerycompany

C.W. Sellors sells handmade amethyst rings, earrings, necklaces, charms and bracelets. 
cwsellors

TJC is worth a look for affordable rings and matching ring-and-necklace gift sets.

Personalised options

Jewlr lets you customise birthstone pieces, such as initial tag necklaces and engravable heart necklaces. Prices start around £120 and go up from there.

Vintage and antique

Jewellery Discovery sells Victorian and vintage amethyst pieces, with prices from about £850 into the thousands. It suits someone who likes something one of a kind. 
jewellerydiscovery


Choosing a piece

Style: studs and drop earrings are the easiest to get right. Pendants are also safe, and a ring is riskier unless you know her size.
Metal: match her usual jewellery. Sterling silver is the budget-friendly option, while 9ct gold lasts longer for everyday wear.
Budget: a nice sterling silver piece can cost under £50, while 9ct gold with diamond accents typically runs a few hundred pounds.

Prices and stock change often, so check each site for current offers. If you tell me her style (delicate, bold, vintage) and your budget, I can narrow it down.
