What to check before buying

  • Treatment: Most sapphires are heated, which is normal and accepted. Diffused or “glass-filled” stones are not, and they’re worth far less. Ask whether the stone is natural, heated or unheated, and whether it comes with a lab report (GRS, SSEF, Gübelin or similar) for anything pricey.
  • Natural or lab-grown diamonds: Check which the halo uses, because it affects price and resale value.
  • Hallmarking: Gold in the UK should carry a hallmark, so make sure the pendant and chain are both marked.
  • Chain: Some listings include one and some don’t, so confirm before comparing prices.
  • Returns: Jewellers with 14 to 60 day return windows let you check the colour in person. Blue sapphires vary a lot in tone, so photos can mislead.
