New and pre-owned

Tips for buying

Check that gold pieces are UK hallmarked. It’s a legal requirement above a minimum weight, and it confirms the metal purity.
Ask whether the sapphire is natural or lab-created, and whether it’s been heat-treated. Heat treatment is very common and fine, but it affects value. A certificate for the stone is worth having on pricier pieces.
Look at the returns policy. Most of these retailers offer around 14 days, but check before buying, especially for pre-owned or finance purchases.
For a ballpark, a small natural sapphire pendant in 9ct gold usually starts at a few hundred pounds. Larger stones, 18ct gold and diamond accents push the price up quickly.
