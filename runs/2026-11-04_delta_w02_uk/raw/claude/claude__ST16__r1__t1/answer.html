There are plenty of UK retailers with online sapphire necklace ranges. Roughly by price and style:

High-end and luxury

Pragnell sells British-made fine jewellery, with sapphire and diamond pieces that often run into the thousands.
Mappin & Webb has a dedicated sapphire necklace range. Its necklaces and pendants are mostly priced between £1,001 and £5,000, from brands including Fope, Mappin & Webb and Suzanne Kalan, with click & collect available. mappinandwebb
Berry’s Jewellers has its own collections plus brands like Fope. Some pieces have monthly payment options. berrysjewellers

Mid-range

Goldsmiths has a range of sapphire necklaces and pendants, from single pendants to more intricate designs. goldsmiths
Michael Jones Jeweller has sapphire and diamond necklaces in 18ct gold from roughly £1,375 to £1,875. Delivery is free in the UK on orders over £100, with free click and collect. michaeljonesjeweller
The Jewellers (thejewellers.com) has 9ct gold options, for example a 9ct white gold diamond and 0.46ct sapphire necklace at £1,210. thejewellers

New and pre-owned

Hatton Jewellers sells both. New necklaces come with a certificate of authenticity and a UK hallmark, and there’s 0% finance on gemstone necklaces over £500. hatton-jewellers

Tips for buying

Check that gold pieces are UK hallmarked. It’s a legal requirement above a minimum weight, and it confirms the metal purity.
Ask whether the sapphire is natural or lab-created, and whether it’s been heat-treated. Heat treatment is very common and fine, but it affects value. A certificate for the stone is worth having on pricier pieces.
Look at the returns policy. Most of these retailers offer around 14 days, but check before buying, especially for pre-owned or finance purchases.
For a ballpark, a small natural sapphire pendant in 9ct gold usually starts at a few hundred pounds. Larger stones, 18ct gold and diamond accents push the price up quickly.
