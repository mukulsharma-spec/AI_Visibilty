Here are six to start with, mixing coloured gemstones and diamonds. Prices are as listed when I searched, so check them and stock on the retailer’s site.

If you want colour, the sapphire and ruby bracelets are the strongest matches. If you meant classic diamond tennis bracelets, the Goldsmiths and Taylor & Hart ones fit better.

Metal purity and setting affect both price and wear. 9ct is cheaper and harder wearing, while 18ct is richer in gold. Bezel settings are more snag-resistant than claw settings. Also check the bracelet length (7 to 7.5 inches is typical) against your wrist.
