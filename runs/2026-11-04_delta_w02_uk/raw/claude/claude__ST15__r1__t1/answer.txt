What to look for

Colour: Look for a strong, even pink or peach hue, free from distracting visible inclusions. The pinker and clearer the stone, the more it costs. glamira
Metal: Rose gold amplifies the gem’s warmth, which is why that pairing is so popular. White gold, platinum and yellow gold also work, and white gives a cooler, crisper look. glamira
Setting: Morganite is softer than sapphire or diamond. A halo, bezel or three-stone setting protects the edges, and a plain solitaire is fine if you’re careful with it.
Care: Avoid harsh chemicals and ultrasonic cleaners. Take the ring off for exercise, cleaning and cooking. glamira greenweddingshoes
Certification: Many morganites are heat-treated to improve colour, which is normal and widely accepted. Ask the seller to confirm any treatments, and ask for a gem report if the stone is large or expensive.
Budget: One guide puts morganite at $100-300 per carat, but the setting and any diamonds around it drive most of the final price. diamonds
