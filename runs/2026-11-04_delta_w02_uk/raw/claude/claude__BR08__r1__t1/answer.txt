Brilliant Earth
The pages I found were the US site, so these prices are in dollars and exclude tax:

Lab grown sapphire rings run from about $2,340 for a Freesia solitaire to $4,240 for a Luxe Secret Garden diamond ring.

Loose stones are listed too, for example a 9x7mm blue oval lab grown sapphire at $1,490.

The two aren't a perfect like-for-like comparison. The designs, metals and stone sizes differ, and the US prices exclude tax while Angara's UK prices include VAT. Even so, Angara's solitaires and three-stone styles come in noticeably lower than Brilliant Earth's comparable styles. Angara also advertises free express shipping, free UK returns and 60-day returns.

Before you buy, check Brilliant Earth's UK site directly for the real £ price, including delivery, duties and returns terms for the UK. Then compare the actual stone size, metal and certification on any specific ring, since those drive most of the price difference.
