Several UK-based retailers sell Akoya strands online, at quite different price points.

Pearl specialists

Pearlescence has a dedicated Akoya necklace section. Prices range from about £80 for dark-toned pieces to £550-£650 for blue Akoya strands, and natural white Akoya necklaces appear at £480 and £800. They also list a 7.7-8mm natural white strand at £865. [Akoya Necklaces +2]
Pearls.co.uk sells a classic single strand of creamy white Akoya pearls, and a separate white seed and Akoya “Original” style. [pearls]
Angara UK has Japanese Akoya strands in classic styles, for example an 18" double-strand 6-7mm necklace with a 14ct white gold clasp at £2,399, with free express shipping and free 60-day returns in the UK. [angara]

Fine jewellery and antique dealers

Jewellery Discovery stocks gold-clasped strands. One example is a 7-8mm strand of 53 round Japanese Akoya pearls, knotted between each pearl, with a 9ct yellow gold ball clasp. Their pieces come with free UK delivery and are authenticated. Some listings are marked sold, so check availability. [jewellerydiscovery] [jewellerydiscovery]
Mappin & Webb carries pearl pendants and more formal strand designs. It’s a higher-end option, so check their pearl necklace category for Akoya specifically. [mappinandwebb]

Overseas option
Pearl Paradise (US) is often recommended on pearl forums. One UK buyer noted that import charges would apply when buying from them, so factor in customs and VAT. That thread is quite old, so treat it as a lead rather than current advice. [pearl-guide]

What to check before buying

Pearl size in mm. 7-8mm is the classic Akoya range, and larger pearls cost significantly more.
Whether the pearls are individually knotted. This protects them and makes a break less costly.
Whether the listing says natural colour or treated, and what the clasp metal is (hallmarked gold versus silver).
The returns policy. Pearls are hard to judge from photos, so a generous return window is valuable.
