Tips

Garnet is reasonably durable but not as hard as diamond or sapphire. It’s fine for everyday wear, but a solitaire in a protective setting suits a ring she’ll wear daily.
Check the metal is hallmarked. Anything sold as gold or silver in the UK should be.
Find her ring size discreetly, for example by borrowing a ring she already wears on that finger. Retailers with easy returns or free resizing make sizing mistakes less stressful.
Look at the garnet in daylight if you can. Colours range from deep wine-red to brighter orange-red, and she may prefer one.
