Choosing a stone

Sapphire is the best all-rounder. It’s very hard, so it’s good for daily wear, and it comes in blue and other colours.
Onyx is black and understated, and it suits a classic signet.
Emerald looks striking but is more brittle, so it’s better for occasional wear.
Ruby is durable and bold.
Garnet, blue topaz and lapis are cheaper and softer-toned. Lapis and garnet scratch more easily.

Whatever you choose, look for a UK hallmark on any gold or silver piece. It confirms the metal purity.
