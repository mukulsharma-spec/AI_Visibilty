Vintage, handmade and one-off pieces
- 1stDibs has some UK-based sellers, for example one in Chesham, GB, offering London blue topaz solitaire rings in 10K and 14K white gold with customisation. Many other listings there are from US or other overseas sellers, so check the “located in” line.
- Etsy and eBay have plenty of silver and gold-plated rings, but the results I saw were priced in US dollars. Filter for UK sellers to avoid customs charges and long delivery times.

Buying tips
- Natural blue topaz is almost always treated (irradiated and heated) to get the deep London blue colour. That’s standard and fine, but a good seller should say so.
- Check the metal is hallmarked, especially with gold, and check the returns policy before ordering. Ring sizing can be tricky online.
- At a physical jeweller, such as an independent shop or a Hatton Garden dealer if you’re near London, you can see the stone’s colour in person. London blue varies a lot, from inky and dark to a brighter steel blue.
