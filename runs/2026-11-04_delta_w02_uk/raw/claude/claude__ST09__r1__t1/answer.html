One caution: the Shining Diamonds page that comes up for “yellow sapphire rings” actually lists yellow diamonds, not sapphires.

Before buying, it’s worth checking:

Treatment: most yellow sapphires are heat-treated, which is normal and affects price. Ask the seller to state it, and expect to pay a premium for unheated stones. Certification: for stones over about a carat, a lab report (GIA, SSEF, Gübelin and similar) confirms origin and treatment. Returns and hallmarking: a decent returns window, and a UK hallmark on the metal, are good signs.
