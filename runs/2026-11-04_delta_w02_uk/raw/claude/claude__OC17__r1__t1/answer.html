Yes, several UK jewellers offer free gift wrapping on gemstone necklaces. Only one of them explicitly mentions a gift receipt in what I found.

Gift wrapping and a gift receipt

Astrid & Miyu: All orders come with a gift receipt, and they also offer a gift bag, a free gift message, and gift wrap and ribbon. They sell gemstone necklaces, such as the Ironstone Gemstone Necklace in 9k gold at £210. [astridandmiyu]

Free gift wrapping (gift receipt not mentioned)

Berry’s Jewellers: Every purchase, large or small, is eligible for free gift wrapping, and it can include a personalised message. You can add it at checkout or ask in store. They stock many gemstone necklaces, including sapphire, tourmaline and Marco Bicego designs. [berrysjewellers]

Winterson: They offer complimentary gift wrapping, with their signature zigzag paper sealed with a Winterson Flower. [winterson]

CW Sellors: Free luxury gift wrapping is available when you select it at checkout. They have gemstone pieces such as Whitby Jet and opal necklaces. [awin]

Alison Moore: You can choose to have items wrapped in hand screen-printed paper. The wrapping is optional, and they have birthstone pendants. [alisonmoore]

Thistledown Wishes: Jewellery is delivered gift wrapped in a branded eco-friendly gift box tied with a biodegradable ribbon. It is sold through The British Craft House. [thebritishcrafthouse]

For the retailers that don’t mention a gift receipt, it’s worth emailing or asking in store before you buy. Many will leave the price off the paperwork if you ask. Also check each retailer’s returns policy, since it can differ for gemstone or bespoke pieces.
