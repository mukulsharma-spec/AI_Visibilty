I’d skip the 18ct pieces at places like Allum & Sidaway, which will likely be well beyond your budget. Vintage sellers can be good value, but the one vintage ring I found was £795 for a very small stone (0.25ct).

A few things to check before buying:

Durability: Tanzanite is relatively soft and scratches or chips more easily than sapphire or diamond. For an everyday ring, look for a protective setting like a bezel or halo, or save it for occasional wear.
Treatment and grade: Most tanzanite is heat-treated, which is normal, but retailers should say so. Ask about the grade (AA or AAA is better colour and clarity) and whether it’s a natural stone.
Returns: Check the return policy, since colour looks different in photos than in person. Stores with physical branches, like David Christopher, let you compare in person.
