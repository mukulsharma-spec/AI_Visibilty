Assuming your budget is about £1,100 (roughly $1,500 at current exchange rates), you have plenty of choice. Here are some good options.

Best value for a quality gold setting

  • Lloyds Family Jewellery has natural ruby half eternity rings in solid gold from about £238 to £454, and a ruby and diamond emerald-cut ring at £539. These are 9k gold and use small stones, so they suit stacking or everyday wear. lloydsfamilyjewellery
  • The Fine Jewellery Company has a 9ct white gold round natural ruby single stone ring, with a 5mm ruby (0.70ct total), for £699. Its 9ct white gold oval claw-set ruby ring (7x5mm) is £779. thefinejewellerycompanythefinejewellerycompany

Bigger or more detailed rings, near the top of your budget

  • A 9ct white gold oval natural ruby and diamond cluster ring (7x5mm) is £1,119. That’s just over £1,100, so check the exchange rate on the day. thefinejewellerycompany
  • Shining Diamonds lists a 0.75ct marquise ruby and diamond halo ring at £1,029, and a 0.50ct oval ruby and diamond halo cluster ring at £539. That site also lists 18k options, including a 0.37ct round ruby ring in 18k gold at £749. shiningdiamondsshiningdiamonds
  • Diamond Heaven has a 0.40ct round ruby ring in 18k yellow gold at about £885. diamond-heaven

Things to watch for

  • Almost all rubies at this price are heat-treated. That’s normal and accepted, but “unheated” rubies cost far more, so don’t expect them under £1,100.
  • Very large, cheap stones are a warning sign. TJC lists a 13.6ct natural ruby ring in 9k gold at £599.99, but at that price the stone is likely to be low-grade, heavily treated or included, so look closely at the grading and reviews. tjc
  • Check that it says “natural” rather than lab-created or glass-filled. Ask for a gemstone report or treatment disclosure on anything over about £800, and check the UK return policy (14 days is the legal minimum for online purchases).
  • Choose 18ct over 9ct if you want richer colour and more durability against skin reactions, but expect to pay a bit more for the same stone size.
