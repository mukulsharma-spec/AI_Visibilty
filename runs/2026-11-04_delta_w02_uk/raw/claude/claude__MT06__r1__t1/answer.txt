Things to watch for

Almost all rubies at this price are heat-treated. That’s normal and accepted, but “unheated” rubies cost far more, so don’t expect them under £1,100.
Very large, cheap stones are a warning sign. TJC lists a 13.6ct natural ruby ring in 9k gold at £599.99, but at that price the stone is likely to be low-grade, heavily treated or included, so look closely at the grading and reviews. 
tjc

Check that it says “natural” rather than lab-created or glass-filled. Ask for a gemstone report or treatment disclosure on anything over about £800, and check the UK return policy (14 days is the legal minimum for online purchases).
Choose 18ct over 9ct if you want richer colour and more durability against skin reactions, but expect to pay a bit more for the same stone size.
