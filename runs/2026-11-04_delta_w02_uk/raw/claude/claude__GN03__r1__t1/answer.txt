Here are some well-regarded options in the UK, grouped by what you might be after.

Everyday and fashion-forward fine jewellery


Monica Vinader: Well-made gold vermeil and solid gold pieces, with a good reputation for quality and design.

Missoma: Trendy, layerable pieces at accessible prices, with a lot of gold-plated and sterling silver.

Astrid & Miyu: Delicate, stackable pieces, popular for earrings and ear stacks.

Mejuri: Minimalist fine jewellery that ships to the UK, with a solid gold range at mid-level prices.



Diamonds, engagement rings and bespoke


Taylor & Hart: A UK brand known for customisable engagement rings and good transparency on diamond quality.

Ingle & Rhode: A British brand with an ethical sourcing focus.

Blue Nile UK: A big selection of loose diamonds and settings, with detailed grading information.

Hatton Garden jewellers: London’s jewellery quarter, where many independents sell online and often offer better value on diamonds.



High street and big retailers


Beaverbrooks, Goldsmiths and Ernest Jones: Broad selections, regular sales, and physical stores for returns, resizing and servicing.

Pandora: Charms and bracelets, good for gifts.



Luxury


Boodles, Tiffany & Co. and Cartier: Prestige pieces with high-end service and packaging.



Handmade and unique


Etsy and Not On The High Street: Independent UK makers, good for personalised or one-off pieces. Check seller reviews carefully.



Tips for buying online


Look for hallmarked gold and silver (UK law requires hallmarking for precious metals above a certain weight).

Check the return policy, since UK distance-selling rules generally give you 14 days, though personalised or bespoke items are often excluded.

For diamonds, ask for certification from GIA, IGI or a similar lab.

Know the difference between gold-plated, gold vermeil (a thicker layer of gold over sterling silver) and solid gold, as prices vary widely.
