Congratulations to your daughter! Here are some good places to look in the UK, grouped by what you might want.

High-street jewellers (easy to see in person, good returns)
Beaverbrooks, Goldsmiths, Ernest Jones, and Mappin & Webb are all reliable for real gold and gemstone pieces. They’re worth visiting if you’d like to see the stones before buying. H.Samuel and Argos are more budget-friendly.

Fashion-fine jewellery brands (modern, giftable, often online-first)
Monica Vinader, Missoma, and Astrid & Miyu make delicate gemstone pendants in gold vermeil or solid gold at mid-range prices. Pandora and Swarovski are also easy, popular choices.

Something more personal or unique
Not On The High Street and Etsy UK are good for independent designers, and many will personalise or engrave. If you’re in London, Hatton Garden has dozens of independent jewellers who can sell you something special or even customise it.

Specialist gemstone retailers
Online specialists like Angara offer a wide range of stones with detailed quality information, which helps if you want a specific gem.

A few tips

In the UK, gold, silver, and platinum items above a small weight should carry a hallmark, so check for one.
Ask whether the stone is natural, lab-grown, or treated (many sapphires and emeralds are). Lab-grown stones can give you a bigger, better stone for the money, as long as it’s clearly disclosed.
Check the return policy, since sizes and chain lengths can matter. Gifts are easier when the retailer offers exchanges.
Birthstones or her favourite colour make a meaningful choice, and you could tuck the stone’s meaning into the card.
