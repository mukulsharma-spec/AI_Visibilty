Retailers with pay-later or pay-in-instalments options

Hatton Jewellers has the widest range. It offers Klarna, Clearpay (pay in 4 instalments across 6 weeks), and OMNI Capital Finance, which runs 12 to 48 months at 0% to 16.9% APR depending on order value.

Beaverbrooks offers Klarna’s pay 30 days after delivery or pay in three interest-free instalments. The 30-day option is available on online orders between £50 and £350.

Diamond Rocks UK offers 0% finance via Klarna on purchases up to £5,000. This is the best fit of these for a bigger purchase like an engagement ring.

Daisy Jewellery offers Klarna, with payment up to 30 days after shipment or in three interest-free instalments.

Antique Jewellers offers Klarna pay in 30 days and pay in 3 monthly interest-free payments.

Jewlr lets you split the cost into three payments with Klarna on orders over £35.

EverWith (memorial jewellery) offers Klarna “buy now, pay later” with payment in 30 days.

Ramsdens offers introductions to third-party funders so jewellery can be bought on finance terms.

Which type to choose

Pay in 3 or pay in 4 (Klarna, Clearpay): these are good for smaller purchases. They’re usually interest-free if you pay on time, but late fees can apply.
Longer finance (Klarna Finance, OMNI Capital): this suits larger purchases. It can carry interest, so compare the APR and total cost.

Regulation change: BNPL borrowers get stronger protections from 15 July 2026. Some retailers now note that FCA regulation may change the terms, protections and disclosures of these products. Since this took effect recently, check the current terms at checkout.
