Deals

Wowcher UK collects emerald stud deals from various retailers, including both natural and lab-created stones. Check the product description carefully, because the stone type makes a big difference to price and value. wowcher

Before buying, check whether the emeralds are natural or lab-created, and whether the piece is hallmarked. Also look at the return window, since gemstones can look quite different in person than in photos. Emeralds are usually treated with oils or resins, and good sellers will say so.
