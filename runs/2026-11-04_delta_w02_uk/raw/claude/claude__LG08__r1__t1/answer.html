Things to check

These are mostly diamond-focused. If you want coloured gemstones (sapphire, ruby, emerald) in both natural and lab-grown versions, look closely at each product page, since the choice may only apply to diamonds. Sunshine Diamonds and The Diamond Store look the most promising for gemstones.
Look for independent certification (IGI or GIA) on both natural and lab-grown stones, and check return policies.
Lab-grown diamond ads have recently drawn regulatory attention. A trade headline reports that the ASA upheld complaints about using “diamond” without qualification in lab-grown jewellery advertising, so look for clear “lab-grown” labelling.
