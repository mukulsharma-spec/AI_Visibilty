I didn’t name specific shops, so I’ll go by the types of option I listed.

Fastest: an independent jeweller with an in-house workshop. Simple resizing and engraving often take 1 to 3 days, and some will do it while you wait or the same day, especially laser engraving. Workshops in Hatton Garden and the Jewellery Quarter are usually quickest because the bench is on site.

Middle: a retailer with an in-house or nearby workshop. Expect about a week, depending on their queue.

Slowest: a shop that sends rings off, or a mail-in service. Allow 1 to 3 weeks, since you add postage both ways and the shop’s own handling time. If you do post it, Royal Mail Special Delivery Guaranteed is the fastest insured option.

Heat-sensitive stones, platinum, and complex settings can add days, because the jeweller may need to remove the stone first. Ask for the turnaround up front and whether they can prioritise it, as some charge a rush fee.
