There isn’t one single best ring, but under about £370-400 (roughly $500), three routes give the best value, depending on what you want.

1. A 9ct gold ring with a small natural sapphire and diamond accents. This is the most practical option for everyday wear. At this budget, expect a small stone, typically heat-treated. A large share of the blue sapphire sold in UK fine jewellery is heat-treated Thai sapphire rather than untreated stone. That’s normal and fine, as long as the seller discloses it. Sapphire is also very durable: it rates 9 on the Mohs hardness scale, nearly as hard as diamond.

2. A lab-grown sapphire. If you want a bigger, cleaner stone, this stretches the budget furthest. Lab-grown sapphires have the same composition as mined ones and offer exceptional value under £500. They’re chemically real sapphire, just not natural, so they won’t hold resale value the way a natural stone might. 
juvetti


3. An antique or vintage ring from a UK auction house. This can give you the most character per pound. Recent regional auction listings show an 18ct gold sapphire and diamond cluster ring estimated at £340-£440, and a platinum sapphire and pear-shape diamond ring at £200-£300. Buyer’s premium and fees are usually added on top of the hammer price, so budget for those. It’s also worth requesting a condition report, since auction catalogues often note things like small chips or a slightly loose stone.

Things to check before buying:

Hallmark. UK gold jewellery over a certain weight must carry an Assay Office hallmark. If a listing doesn’t mention one, be suspicious of the metal claim. 
gemondo

Treatment and origin. Look for “natural, heat-treated” or “lab-grown” stated clearly in the listing.
Returns policy. A 14-day return window is standard for online purchases and lets you judge the colour in person.
Colour in real light. Sapphire ranges from pale cornflower to inky navy, so check photos in daylight and ask for a video if you can.

My general pick for most people at this price is a 9ct gold ring with a natural, heat-treated sapphire of around 0.5-0.75 carat, or a lab-grown stone if size matters more to you than origin.
