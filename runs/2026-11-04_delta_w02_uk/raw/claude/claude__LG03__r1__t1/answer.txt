Things to check before buying

Confirm the listing says clearly that the ruby is lab-grown (or “created”), not just “ruby”.
Ask for a grading report if you’re spending four figures. Lab-grown rubies are less commonly graded than diamonds, so it’s worth asking.
Look for a UK hallmark on gold or platinum, and check the returns policy. Online purchases generally carry a 14-day cancellation right in the UK, but retailers’ own terms for resizing and returns on custom pieces differ.
Compare stone size and colour in photos and videos. Lab rubies are generally vivid and clean, so differences between sellers usually come down to cut and setting quality.
