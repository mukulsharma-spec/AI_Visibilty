For a ring over £5,000, the stone’s quality, origin and treatment matter more than the brand. Here is where I’d look, from my own knowledge plus a quick search, which returned little detail on specific retailers.

London specialists and fine jewellers

Hancocks (Burlington Arcade) is known for antique and vintage coloured-stone pieces and for sapphires, including Burmese and Ceylon stones.
Bentley & Skinner and Wartski are long-established Mayfair dealers in antique and period jewellery with good stones.
Blowers Jewellers (Mayfair and Hull) lists natural sapphire and emerald rings online at roughly £4,400 to £8,900, so it’s a useful price benchmark.
Garrard, Boodles and David Morris sit at the higher end, with strong coloured-stone work but a brand premium.
Hatton Garden and St Cross Street have independent gemstone specialists and bespoke makers, often at better value than Mayfair. Many show by appointment only, so contact them first.

Auctions
Bonhams, Christie’s, Sotheby’s and Fellows (Birmingham) sell fine coloured-stone rings, often at lower prices than retail. Lots usually come with lab reports. Expect buyer’s premium and VAT on top of the hammer price, and no returns.

What to insist on at this price

An independent lab report from GIA, SSEF, Gübelin or AGL. It should state the origin (Burma, Colombia, Sri Lanka and Kashmir command premiums) and any treatment.
Treatment disclosure. Heat on sapphire and ruby is standard and accepted. Unheated stones cost significantly more, and for emerald, minor oil is normal but heavy filling is not.
A written return policy, ideally 14 days or more, and an insurance valuation.
Buy the stone, not the setting. A good stone in a plain setting beats a mediocre stone in a flashy one.

Another route
You can buy a certified loose stone from a reputable dealer and have it set by a bespoke jeweller. This often gives you better quality for the money than ready-made rings.

If you tell me which stone you want (sapphire, emerald, ruby or something else), your budget ceiling, and whether it’s an engagement ring, I can narrow this down and suggest what quality to target.
