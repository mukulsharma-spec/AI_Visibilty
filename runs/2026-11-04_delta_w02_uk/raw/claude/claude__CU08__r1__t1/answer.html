Virtual try-on for rings is fairly common in the UK, but my search turned up little that is specific to gemstone rings, so treat this as a starting point.

Confirmed to offer ring try-on

H.Samuel has a dedicated virtual try-on tool for rings and watches, covering fashion, wedding and engagement rings. It’s a high-street chain with a lot of coloured-stone rings, so it’s probably your best bet for gemstones. 
hsamuel

Goldsmiths is a UK retailer that a Tangiblee (try-on software vendor) blog post lists among retailers offering virtual try-on of engagement rings. That post is from 2024, so check the feature is still on the product pages. 
tangiblee

Get-Diamonds launched an AR tool called “Virtual Hand” in 2022, which lets you try on a variety of diamond rings in different sizes, cuts and settings. It’s diamond-focused, so it may not help with coloured stones. 
jewelleryfocus


Worth checking, but I couldn’t confirm AR try-on

Angara UK specialises in sapphires, emeralds and other coloured stones. Its ring pages show hand views with a light or dark skin tone toggle, plus a ruler image for scale. That helps you judge size and proportion, but it looks like pre-rendered imagery rather than live camera try-on. I couldn’t find anything saying it has a true AR feature. 
angara


Tips

Look for a “Try On” button on individual product pages. Retailers often enable it only for part of their range, and gemstone styles are sometimes left out.
Virtual try-on shows size and proportion well but is weaker at showing colour and sparkle. Gemstone colour in particular can look different on screen, so check the return policy before buying. Angara’s UK pages mention free 60-day returns.
