Tips when buying

Ask whether the stone is natural or lab-grown, and whether it has been treated. Oil treatment is common with emeralds.
Check for a hallmark on gold pieces.
Emeralds are fairly delicate, so a pendant or earrings will wear better than a ring if she’d wear it daily.
