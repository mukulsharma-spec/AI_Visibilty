Two things matter a lot with ruby rings. First, ask whether the ruby is heated or untreated and whether a lab report comes with it. Most rubies are heat-treated, and untreated stones cost much more. Second, ruby carat weight and diamond carat weight aren’t comparable, so look at the ruby’s colour and clarity rather than just the size.
