Most UK jewellers offer free resizing as a one-off within a set window, not for life. A lifetime warranty is common, but it usually covers manufacturing defects rather than accidental damage. These are the ones that came up, from most to least generous on resizing:

London Diamonds is the standout. Its lifetime warranty advertises no charges for resizing, servicing or repairs, ever. One reviewer said they posted a ring in and got it back resized and cleaned a week later, free under the warranty. It mainly sells engagement rings. trustpilottrustpilot

Queensmith gives one free resize within 60 days of collection or delivery. Later resizes cost a small fee. It also includes unlimited free professional cleaning and a lifetime manufacturer’s warranty. The warranty’s validity is tied to regular servicing. Hearts of London customers can still get free cleans and resizing and the lifetime guarantee through Queensmith. lifetime care package +2

Cullen offers one free resize within 12 months of completion, depending on the style and metal. All its gold and platinum jewellery has a lifetime manufacturing warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. cullenjewellerycullenjewellery

Pruden & Smith is a weaker fit. It offers one free resize within three months of delivery, and its guarantee runs from 1 year to 20 years depending on the piece, rather than for life. It does give lifetime free re-polishing. prudenandsmithprudenandsmith

The Diamond Store only has a 5-year warranty against defects. It does offer free exchange or resizing on custom sizes (smaller than J or larger than Q) if the fit isn’t right. thediamondstorethediamondstore

Before buying, check three things:

  • Whether the resize is unlimited or one-off, and how long you have to use it.
  • Whether the lifetime warranty covers accidental damage or only defects.
  • Whether the warranty requires regular servicing to stay valid.

Policies change, and the sources above are the retailers’ own pages and customer reviews. Confirm the current terms on the product page before ordering.
