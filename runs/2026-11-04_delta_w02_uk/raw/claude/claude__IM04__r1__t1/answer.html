Here are six tanzanite pendants from UK retailers, ranging from budget silver to solid gold. A few things to keep in mind as you compare:

  • Metal matters for price. Silver with gold or rhodium plating (Otis Jaxon, TJC) keeps costs low but the plating wears over time. Solid 14ct or 18ct gold (C W Sellors, Angara, Leibish) costs far more but lasts.
  • Stone size varies a lot. The pieces here run from about 0.25ct up to 1.50ct total weight, so check the carat figure rather than judging by the photo.
  • Prices and stock change. The V&A piece showed as out of stock when I looked, and I couldn’t confirm current prices for Otis Jaxon, Angara or Leibish, so check each page.
