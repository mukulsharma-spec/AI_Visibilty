Several UK retailers advertise free 30-day returns on gemstone rings, but the fine print differs between them. These come from the retailers’ own pages and recent roundups, so confirm the current terms at checkout.

Angara (UK site). Its product pages list Free 30-Day Returns, with free return shipping on all orders, plus free express shipping within the UK. It specialises in coloured gemstone rings such as peridot, sapphire and emerald, so it is probably the closest match to what you asked for.

The Diamond Store. It offers free 30-day returns for refunds and 60-day exchanges across the UK and Northern Ireland. It also sells gemstone rings. Things to check before you buy:

Custom and personalised items. These are usually excluded. Beaverbrooks, for example, excludes items made or ordered to your specific requirements and personalised items. Engraved or made-to-order rings are the likeliest to be non-returnable.

Condition. Rings generally need to be unworn and in their original packaging.
Timing. Seasonal extensions exist. John Lewis offered extended festive returns last year, which may be relevant if you’re buying now.
