Several UK retailers advertise free 30-day returns on gemstone rings, but the fine print differs between them. These come from the retailers’ own pages and recent roundups, so confirm the current terms at checkout.

Angara (UK site). Its product pages list Free 30-Day Returns, with free return shipping on all orders, plus free express shipping within the UK. It specialises in coloured gemstone rings such as peridot, sapphire and emerald, so it is probably the closest match to what you asked for. angara

The Diamond Store. It offers free 30-day returns for refunds and 60-day exchanges across the UK and Northern Ireland. It also sells gemstone rings. Rings smaller than size J or larger than size Q count as custom sizes, but it still offers a free exchange or resizing if the fit is wrong. That suggests those sizes may not be refundable, so check before buying if you’re outside the J to Q range. thediamondstorethediamondstore

Ernest Jones. You can return items bought online by post free of charge within 30 days and get a refund to your original payment method. It also takes returns in UK stores. ernestjones

Monica Vinader (direct, and via John Lewis). Its gemstone rings, such as the Rio Pink Quartz and the Siren range, are listed with free returns on John Lewis. John Lewis allows returns or exchanges within 30 days of receiving the item. A Hello! Magazine roundup also lists free UK and US returns on Monica Vinader’s own site. Beaverbrooks. It has a 30-day return window starting from the date of purchase, with refunds to the original payment method. Its page says that, without a pre-paid returns label, the Post Office will charge you to send the package back. Check that you get a label when you order. jewellerymonthly.co.uk +2

Things to check before you buy:

  • Custom and personalised items. These are usually excluded. Beaverbrooks, for example, excludes items made or ordered to your specific requirements and personalised items. Engraved or made-to-order rings are the likeliest to be non-returnable. beaverbrooks
  • Condition. Rings generally need to be unworn and in their original packaging.
  • Timing. Seasonal extensions exist. John Lewis offered extended festive returns last year, which may be relevant if you’re buying now.
