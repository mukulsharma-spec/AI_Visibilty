I'd go with Angara. It's the more practical choice for a UK buyer.

Kay is a US chain, with over a thousand stores there, and its free shipping applies only to US addresses. I couldn't find any sign that it delivers to the UK, so you'd probably need a package forwarder, which adds cost and hassle. Check kay.com's shipping page before you consider it.

Angara has a UK site with prices in pounds that include VAT. It offers free express shipping and free return shipping within the UK, and returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. Their pages also mention a lifetime warranty. One thing to plan around is that every Angara item is made to order after you place the order. You pick a delivery date at checkout, so don't leave it to the last minute.

UK Mothering Sunday is 7 March 2027, not May, so you have a few months. Ordering by early to mid February should leave plenty of buffer.

For a mum's birthstone, you could do a single solitaire pendant in her stone, or a pendant with stones for each of her children. Angara's pendants have some gemstone-specific options, and the 14K gold versions will last better than sterling silver if you can stretch the budget.
