Tips

Durability: Aquamarine is hard (about 7.5 to 8 on the Mohs scale), but it can chip more easily than sapphire or diamond. A halo, bezel or low-profile claw setting protects it better for everyday wear.
Colour and clarity: Look for a stone that’s eye-clean, meaning no visible flaws. Aquamarine is often heated to improve its colour, which is normal, but ask the seller to confirm any treatment in writing.
Hallmark: Check that gold and platinum pieces carry a UK hallmark.
Returns and resizing: Check the returns window and whether resizing is free, since you may not be able to confirm the ring size in advance.
Be wary of marketplaces: I’d avoid eBay and similar sites unless the seller is well established. One listing I came across had a buyer review complaining the seller misrepresented where the ring was made.
