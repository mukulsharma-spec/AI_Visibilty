Higher-end (18ct gold, diamonds)
- Angara’s UK site sells pear-shaped peridot drops with a diamond halo in 18ct rose gold at £1,979 including VAT. It also offers free express shipping, free UK returns and 30-day returns.

Prices and stock change often, so confirm them on each site. For anything pricier, look for a clear returns policy and a statement of whether the stones are natural or lab-created. Peridot is a fairly soft gem, so studs and drops suit it better than much-knocked styles like rings.
