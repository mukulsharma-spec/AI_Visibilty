There are quite a few UK options. Which fits best depends on your budget and whether you want a coloured gemstone specifically.

Coloured gemstone specialists

Angara (UK site): You choose a gemstone, design the ring, then place the order. It’s built around coloured stones such as garnet, moonstone, London blue topaz and green sapphire. You pick the shape and carat weight, then the setting and any accent stones. This is probably the closest match for a gemstone-focused design. 
angara

angara

Diamonds by Me: Their configurator lets you pick a centre stone such as garnet, plus the metal and stone size. They also offer a bespoke service if you have a specific design in mind. The garnet example I saw was priced at roughly £1,434 to £1,931. 
diamondsbyme

Yorkshire Jewellery Company: Their Ring Builder lets you choose the ring style, metal, gemstone and design step by step. 
yorkshirejewellerycompany

Austen Blake: They make gemstone rings to order in their workshop, in silver, white, yellow or rose gold, or platinum. You can also talk to their jewellery experts by live chat, email or phone. 
austenblake


High-street names with online builders

Ernest Jones: Their ring builder covers engagement and wedding rings, and they say the finished ring is ready in about four weeks. It leans towards diamond rings. 
ernestjones

H.Samuel: You choose the stone, setting and budget, and the ring is handcrafted within four weeks of ordering. 
hsamuel

Hancocks: You can design online or visit their Manchester showroom for an in-person consultation. 
hancocksjewellers


Bespoke or lower-budget options

MOH London: They have an online ring builder, and you can also work with their designers in person or by video call. The process asks for a deposit up front and the remaining 50% before crafting starts. 
mohlondon

Alison Moore: You can mix and match gemstone stacking rings in sterling silver or solid gold. A single stone stacking ring starts from £22, and a “Create Your Own Nebula” ring is £129. 
alisonmoore

Not On The High Street: They sell made-to-order pieces like a “Design Your Own Birthstone Eternity Ring” from £275. 
notonthehighstreet


If you tell me your budget, the stone you have in mind and whether it’s an engagement ring or a general-wear piece, I can narrow this down. Whichever you pick, check the return policy for made-to-order pieces, since custom rings are often non-refundable.
