January’s birthstone is garnet, usually a deep red, though it also comes in orange, green, and other colours. Here are some good places to look in the UK:

High street and established jewellers

Goldsmiths, Beaverbrooks, Ernest Jones, and H.Samuel carry garnet rings, earrings, and pendants at mid-range prices, and often have gift-ready options.
Pandora and Swarovski sell birthstone charms and pendants if you want something more fashion-led and affordable.

Contemporary and mid-range brands

Monica Vinader, Missoma, and Astrid & Miyu do delicate, wearable garnet pieces, often in gold vermeil or sterling silver.

Handmade and personalised

Etsy UK and Not On The High Street have lots of independent makers, including custom and personalised birthstone jewellery, which is good for something unique.

Higher-end or more choice

Hatton Garden in London has independent jewellers where you can see stones in person, compare quality, and sometimes commission a bespoke piece.
Gemporia and similar online gem specialists sell loose or set garnets, often at good value.

Look for stone quality (rich, even colour with few visible flaws), the metal (sterling silver, gold vermeil, or solid gold, which affects price and durability), and the return policy if you’re buying online as a gift.
