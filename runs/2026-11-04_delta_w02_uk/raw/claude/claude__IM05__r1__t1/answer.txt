Things to check before buying

Natural or lab-grown: Lab-grown stones cost far less and look the same. Natural stones hold more rarity and resale value.
Heat treatment: Most natural pink sapphires are heated. “No heat” stones with a certificate command a premium.
Colour: Pink sapphires range from pale blush to vivid hot pink and purplish pink. Deeper colour usually costs more, so view it in person if you can.
Durability: Sapphire is very hard (9 on the Mohs scale), so it’s a good choice for daily wear.
