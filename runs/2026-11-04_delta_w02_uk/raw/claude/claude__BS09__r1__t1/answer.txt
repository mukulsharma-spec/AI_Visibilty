A few things to check before buying:

Full or half eternity: half eternity bands are easier to resize, and the plain section of the band is more comfortable day to day.
Metal: 18ct gold and platinum hold up better than 9ct over years of wear.
Hallmarking: in the UK, gold and platinum should be hallmarked, so confirm that before paying.
Sapphire quality: sapphires are often heat-treated, which is normal, but ask whether stones are natural and treated, and about colour consistency across the band.
Returns and resizing: some pieces are made to order and can’t be returned, so confirm the policy and resizing costs first.
