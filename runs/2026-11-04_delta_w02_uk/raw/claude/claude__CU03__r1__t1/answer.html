Here are the UK options I found that fit what you’re after.

DiamondsByMe (diamondsbyme.co.uk) is probably the closest match to a “pick everything yourself” experience. It lets you adjust the stone size, and you can choose between standard SI G diamond quality or VS E. You can also choose the precious metal, including gold or platinum. The price updates as you change options. 
diamondsbyme


Pruden & Smith is a UK jeweller with customisable pendants. On its Simple Diamond Pendant, you can choose your diamond size and a metal colour such as 9ct white, rose or yellow gold, and you use the “Customise” form to enquire about 18ct and platinum. It’s less of a live configurator and more of an enquiry-based service. 
prudenandsmith


Jewlr UK is a personalisation-focused site. You choose your style, metal, stones and engraving, and the piece is then made to order. Some pendants, like its Small Gemstone Cross Pendant, are listed with size options, but this varies by design, so check each product page. It leans toward birthstone and personalised pieces rather than fine diamond work. 
jewlr


Jewellery Studio Online says everything on its website can be customised, including alternate gemstones or metal colour. This is a request-based approach, so you’d contact them with your preferred stone size. 
jewellerystudioonline


MoH London has a “Build Your Own Pendant” flow where you choose your setting, then your diamond, then complete the pendant. I couldn’t confirm which metals are offered, so check that on the page. 
mohlondon


Two US sites, Diamond Studs and Earth’s Treasury, also have pendant builders with a good range of metals. I’d only consider them if you’re comfortable with international shipping, duties and VAT. The UK sites above avoid that.

Before ordering, check whether the stone size is given in carats or millimetres, since the same carat weight looks different across shapes. Also confirm the hallmarking of the metal and the returns policy for made-to-order pieces, as many sites don’t accept returns on custom items.
