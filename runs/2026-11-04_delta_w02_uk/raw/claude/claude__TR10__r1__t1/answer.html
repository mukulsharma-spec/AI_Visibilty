Based on Trustpilot scores (the main review data I could find), these are the strongest options for gemstone jewellery in the UK. I couldn’t check other platforms like Google Reviews, and scores shift over time, so look at the live pages before buying.

Highest ratings with a solid number of reviews

Lily Arkwright: TrustScore 5.0 from 935 reviews. It’s UK-made and UK hallmarked, with Assay Assured membership. It leans towards engagement rings (lab diamonds, moissanite, coloured gemstones), so it suits bridal and ring shopping best.

Lloyds Family Jewellery: 4.9 from 211 reviews, with customers praising quality, presentation and service. It sells natural diamond and gemstone designs.

Large retailers with high ratings

Gemporia: around 4.5-4.6 across roughly 19,000 reviews. That volume makes the score very reliable, and it has the widest gemstone range of the group.

Angara: 4.5-4.7 from nearly 3,000 reviews. Customers like being able to choose gemstone quality, karat weight and metal. It’s a US-based brand, so check UK delivery times, duties and returns before ordering. One reviewer also complained about its lifetime warranty.

Love Amber X: about 4.5-4.6 from over 3,000 reviews. It’s a specialist in Baltic amber rather than a general gemstone shop.

Gemondo: around 4.4-4.5 from 306 reviews. It has accessible pricing and free UK standard delivery. It replies to only 60% of negative reviews.

Well liked, but fewer reviews

Gem Bazaar: about 51 reviews, with customers praising its customer service.

Jian London: 519 reviews, and customers describe a reassuring first-time online purchase.

Great British Gems (antique and vintage pieces) and Berlin Gems (loose certified stones) both score around 4.3-4.4, but with only 7 and 10 reviews. That’s too few to judge confidently.

Tips for choosing

A 5.0 from a few hundred reviews can mean less than a 4.5 from thousands, so weigh the review count too. Check the 1-3 star reviews for patterns around returns, resizing and certificates. For precious metals, look for UK hallmarking and Assay Assured membership. For higher-value stones, ask whether a gemological certificate comes with the piece.
