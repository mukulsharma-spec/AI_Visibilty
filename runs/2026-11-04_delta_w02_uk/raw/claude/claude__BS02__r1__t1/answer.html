What to check before buying

  • Ruby treatment: most rubies are heat-treated, which is normal and accepted, but a good seller should say so. Untreated rubies cost much more.
  • Ruby weight: compare the carat weight of the ruby itself, not just the total. Some listings only give diamond weight.
  • Metal and hallmark: 9ct is cheaper and more durable, while 18ct has richer colour but costs more. All UK gold should be hallmarked.
  • Returns policy: this matters for earrings, since fit and colour can look different from the photos.
