Which online jewelry stores offer financing or buy now pay later? I'm shopping in the UK.
There are several online jewellery retailers in the UK that offer buy now, pay later (BNPL), interest-free instalments, or longer-term finance.
The main payment providers to look out for are:
Klarna – Pay in 3 instalments or other available payment options.
Clearpay – Usually splits purchases into 4 instalments.
PayPal Pay in 3 – Available on eligible purchases.
Humm – Finance options, depending on retailer and eligibility.
Retailer-specific finance – Often offers 0% APR for a set period, sometimes with longer repayment terms.
