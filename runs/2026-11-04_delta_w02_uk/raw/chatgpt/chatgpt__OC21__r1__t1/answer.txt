Which gemstone would I choose?
Aquamarine
Pale blue, elegant and delicate. A lovely choice for a new-mother gift.
Emerald
Rich green, luxurious and timeless. Particularly beautiful in yellow gold.
Pink sapphire
Romantic, feminine and a little less conventional.
Blue sapphire
Classic, prestigious and very durable.
One thought: aquamarine is the traditional March birthstone, emerald May, ruby July and sapphire September. You could make the baby's birthstone part of the present.
A few things I'd check before buying
Natural versus lab-grown: Both are real gemstones, but make sure you know which you're buying.
Treatments: Ask whether the stone has been heated, filled, dyed or otherwise treated.
Metal: I'd favour solid 14ct or 18ct gold if budget permits.
Certification: For a significant gemstone, ask for an independent gemmological report.
Design: A pendant or necklace is a safer bet than a ring if you don't know her current ring size.
