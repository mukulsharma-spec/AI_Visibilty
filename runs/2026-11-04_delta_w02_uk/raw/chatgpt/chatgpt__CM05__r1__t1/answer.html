My advice before buying

One important thing: don't compare prices without checking the gemstone specifications.

Natural sapphires: Generally more expensive, but mined gemstones.

Lab-grown sapphires: Real sapphire chemically, but created in a laboratory.

Cubic zirconia: An inexpensive diamond simulant, not a diamond.

Metal: 925 sterling silver, 9ct gold and 18ct gold make a substantial difference to price.

If you want something that looks like a Blue Nile fine-jewellery bracelet, I'd prioritise 9ct white gold or good-quality sterling silver with proper sapphire settings.
