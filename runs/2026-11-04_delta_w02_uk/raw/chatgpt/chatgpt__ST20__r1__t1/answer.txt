How to make sure it doesn't look fake
This is actually the most important part.
1. Choose a sensible size
I'd look at 1–1.5 carats equivalent for a natural-looking everyday ring.
2. Keep the setting simple
A delicate band and four or six claws look timeless.
3. Look at the stone's fire
Moissanite has more rainbow flashes than diamond. Some cuts show this more strongly.
4. Consider an oval or round
Both can look elegant. Prioritise good proportions and a well-cut stone.
One thing worth knowing: moissanite is not diamond. Even the best quality has a different optical appearance, particularly in direct sunlight. That's not necessarily a bad thing!
What I'd personally look for
Colour: D–F (colourless).
Clarity: eye-clean.
Metal: solid 14ct or 18ct gold, or platinum.
Cut: excellent-quality round brilliant or oval.
Setting: simple solitaire, petite hidden halo or delicate pavé.
Certification: proper stone identification and a written warranty.
I'd also avoid enormous 3–5 carat equivalent stones if your goal is to have people assume it's a natural-looking diamond ring.
