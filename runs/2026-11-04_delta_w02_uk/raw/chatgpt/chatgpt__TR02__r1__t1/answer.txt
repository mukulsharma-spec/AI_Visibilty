Independent laboratories to look for
These are worth knowing because the laboratory named on the report matters considerably.
GIA = Gem identification, natural/synthetic determination and treatments
Gübelin = High-end coloured gemstone analysis and origin
SSEF = Ruby, sapphire and emerald origin and treatments
GRS = Coloured gemstone origin and treatment
AGL = Coloured gemstone identification and enhancement analysis
You can also independently arrange testing in the UK through:
Gemmological Certification Services (GCS) – GCS gemstone reports .
Gem & Pearl Laboratory – The Gem & Pearl Lab .
GIB Gemmological Laboratory – Reports and pricing . Full reports for major coloured gemstones start at £149 for stones up to 2.99ct, according to its published pricing. [GCS +2 — citation chip, no href]
What should the certificate actually tell you?
When shopping, I'd look for these five things:
Natural or synthetic: Does the report establish that the gemstone is naturally occurring?
Treatment disclosure: Is it heated, unheated, fracture-filled, diffusion-treated or otherwise enhanced?
Geographical origin: Particularly relevant for valuable rubies, sapphires and emeralds.
Identification: Does it confirm the actual gemstone species and variety?
Verifiable report number: Can you check the report with the issuing laboratory?
One important distinction: coloured gemstones don't have a universally standardised grading system equivalent to diamond grading. A certificate confirming identity and treatments isn't necessarily a comprehensive quality grade.
