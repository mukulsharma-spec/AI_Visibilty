A very important point about tanzanite engagement rings

I love tanzanite aesthetically, but there's something worth knowing before spending a substantial amount.

Tanzanite has a Mohs hardness of approximately 6–7, compared with 9 for sapphire and 10 for diamond. It can chip or fracture if knocked.

For an engagement ring worn every day, I'd recommend:

A protective halo or bezel setting rather than an exposed solitaire.

A lower-profile setting to reduce knocks.

Platinum or 18ct gold.

A substantial, well-made band.

Asking about servicing, repairs and insurance.

I'd also insist on confirmation that the stone is natural tanzanite, rather than a synthetic or simulated stone, and request clear photographs and video of the actual gemstone.
