What I'd look for with your budget
For a ring intended to last, I'd prioritise:
Stone: Natural sapphire, if that's important to you. Lab-created sapphire is a perfectly legitimate alternative and can offer more size for the money.
Treatment: Heated sapphires are common and generally accepted. Ask about diffusion treatment and fracture filling.
Metal: 14ct gold (375 hallmark) is a sensible choice. Sterling silver is another option if you want a larger stone.
Setting: A four- or six-prong setting, with a secure basket, is a good everyday choice.
Documentation: Ask for the sapphire's dimensions, carat weight, origin if known, treatment disclosure and an independent gemmological report where appropriate.
One important point: don't pay a premium just because a sapphire is labelled AAA or AAAA. These are retailer grading scales, not universal independent standards.
