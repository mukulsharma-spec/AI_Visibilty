A few important buying tips:
Ask whether the sapphire is natural or laboratory-grown.
Ask whether it has been heat-treated. This is common and not necessarily a disadvantage.
For a significant purchase, request an independent gemmological report, ideally from a recognised laboratory such as GIA, SSEF or Gübelin.
View the sapphire in daylight as well as shop lighting. A very dark blue stone can look almost black indoors.
