Which online jewelers have virtual try-on for gemstone rings? I'm shopping in the UK.

My recommendation
If you're specifically shopping for a coloured gemstone ring, I would start with Brilliant Earth and Mikimoto for their actual try-on technology, but verify that the particular gemstone design you want is supported.
One thing worth remembering: virtual try-on is useful for judging the overall appearance, proportions and setting, but it won't accurately reproduce the sparkle, colour saturation or transparency of a real gemstone.
