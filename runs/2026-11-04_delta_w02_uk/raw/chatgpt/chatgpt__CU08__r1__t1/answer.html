Which online jewelers have virtual try-on for gemstone rings? I'm shopping in the UK.

There are a few online jewellers serving the UK with virtual ring try-on technology. However, there's an important distinction: some let you try on engagement rings digitally, while fewer support coloured gemstone rings featuring sapphires, emeralds, rubies, aquamarines or other precious stones.

Here are the best options I've found.

UK online jewellers with virtual try-on

1. Brilliant Earth

Confirmed virtual try-on

UK-facing online store.

Upload a photograph of your hand and overlay a ring.

Adjust ring position, stone shape, carat weight and metal.

Also offers virtual consultations.

Gemstone suitability: The tool is documented primarily for engagement-ring designs and diamond customisation. Coloured gemstone compatibility isn't confirmed.

2. Mikimoto

Confirmed virtual try-on

Has augmented-reality jewellery try-on.

Uses your smartphone camera to show rings on your hand.

Supports ring stacking.

Gemstone suitability: Worth exploring for pearl and gemstone-accented designs, although not every ring is supported.

Confirmed virtual try-on

Online ring builder.

My recommendation

If you're specifically shopping for a coloured gemstone ring, I would start with Brilliant Earth and Mikimoto for their actual try-on technology, but verify that the particular gemstone design you want is supported.

One thing worth remembering: virtual try-on is useful for judging the overall appearance, proportions and setting, but it won't accurately reproduce the sparkle, colour saturation or transparency of a real gemstone.
