What I'd recommend looking for
Before buying, check which type of opal you're getting:
Natural opal: Genuine gemstone, with individual colour patterns.
Australian opal: Particularly desirable, often featuring beautiful flashes of blue, green and orange.
Ethiopian opal: Often has spectacular rainbow-like flashes.
Lab-created opal: More affordable, with a colourful appearance but not a natural gemstone.
Opalite: A man-made glass-like material, not genuine opal.
