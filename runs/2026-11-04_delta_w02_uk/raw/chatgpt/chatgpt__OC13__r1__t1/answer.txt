A little buying advice: make sure the stones are genuine
Since real gemstones matter to you, I'd check these things before ordering:
Ask whether the stone is natural, lab-grown or simulated.
Check whether it has been treated or colour-enhanced.
Look for a gemstone description and, for a more expensive purchase, an independent gemological certificate.
Check the metal too: solid gold and gold plating are very different.
For example, Pandora's birthstone-inspired pieces aren't necessarily natural gemstones, so don't assume that a birthstone design means a genuine stone.
