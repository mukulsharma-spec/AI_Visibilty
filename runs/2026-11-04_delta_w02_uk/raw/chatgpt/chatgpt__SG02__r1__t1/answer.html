What I'd look for

For a wedding ring that you'll wear every day, I'd consider:

Metal: 18ct or 9ct gold, platinum or palladium.

Sapphire setting: Flush-set sapphires are particularly practical because the stones sit inside the band rather than protruding.

Width: 5–7mm is a good starting point for a traditional men's band.

Stone: Natural versus lab-created sapphire – ask which is being supplied.

Comfort: A comfort-fit interior is worth considering.
