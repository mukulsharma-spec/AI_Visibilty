What I'd look for before buying
A few important things can make a big difference:
Natural vs lab-grown: Both are real sapphire, but natural stones generally cost more.
Treatment: Ask whether the sapphire is heat-treated. This is common and accepted, but should be disclosed.
Metal: Solid 9ct gold is preferable for a ring you'll wear regularly.
Certification: Ask for documentation identifying the gemstone and any treatments.
Returns: Check the resizing and return policy, particularly for made-to-order rings.
