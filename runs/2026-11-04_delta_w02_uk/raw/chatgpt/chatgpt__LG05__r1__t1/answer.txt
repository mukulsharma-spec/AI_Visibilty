1. Are lab-grown sapphires actually real sapphires?
8
Yes. Lab-grown sapphires are genuine synthetic corundum, with essentially the same:
Chemical composition as natural sapphires.
Hardness (9 on the Mohs scale).
Colour and optical properties.
Durability for everyday jewellery.
They're not glass, cubic zirconia or sapphire-coloured imitations.
The difference is how they're made: natural sapphires form underground over geological periods, while lab-grown sapphires are created in controlled laboratory conditions.

4. What to check before buying
I'd pay attention to these five things:
Colour: Look for rich, vivid blue rather than excessively dark or almost black blue.
Cut: Oval, cushion and emerald cuts are particularly attractive.
Transparency: Look for a clear, lively stone without distracting imperfections.
Metal: Solid 9K, 14K or 18K gold is preferable to gold-plated jewellery if longevity matters.
Certification: Check that the seller clearly identifies the stone as lab-grown and discloses any treatments.
