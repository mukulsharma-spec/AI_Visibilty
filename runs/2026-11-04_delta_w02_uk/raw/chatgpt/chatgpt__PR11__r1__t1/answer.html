One important distinction:

Natural sapphire: Mined from the earth. This is what I'd prioritise for you.

Lab-grown sapphire: Genuine sapphire chemically, but manufactured in a laboratory.

Simulated sapphire: Glass, cubic zirconia or another stone. Not genuine sapphire.

Also, heat-treated natural sapphire is still real sapphire. Heat treatment is common and should be disclosed. Sapphire Stone – GIA +2

How I'd make sure they're genuinely natural

Before paying, ask the jeweller:

Are both sapphires natural earth-mined stones, not lab-grown?

Have they been heat-treated, diffusion-treated or fracture-filled?

Can you confirm natural origin in writing?

Is there an independent gemmological report, or can one be obtained?
