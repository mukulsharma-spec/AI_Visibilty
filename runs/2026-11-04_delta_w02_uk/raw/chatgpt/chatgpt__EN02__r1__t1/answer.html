What to look for before buying

A few things I'd strongly recommend checking:

Diamond certification: Look for an independent grading report, ideally GIA for natural diamonds or IGI for lab-grown diamonds. Check the report number.

Cut quality: For a round brilliant diamond, prioritise Excellent/Ideal cut. Cut has a significant effect on sparkle.

Return policy: Check the actual refund terms, particularly for bespoke and made-to-order rings.

Resizing: Ideally, get at least one complimentary resize.

Warranty: Look for lifetime manufacturing coverage and ongoing cleaning and inspection.

Total price: Check whether VAT, the setting and delivery are included.

One important distinction: lab-grown and natural diamonds are both real diamonds, but lab-grown diamonds generally cost considerably less. They also have very different resale economics.
