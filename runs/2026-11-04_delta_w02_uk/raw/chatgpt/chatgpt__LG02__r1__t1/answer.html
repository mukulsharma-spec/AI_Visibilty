What I'd look for

A few things worth knowing before buying:

Make sure it's a genuine lab-created emerald, not green cubic zirconia, glass or a simulated emerald.

Ask for details of the stone's origin and a certificate or written authenticity guarantee.

Choose solid 9ct, 14ct or 18ct gold if you want a ring to last.

Emeralds are relatively prone to chipping, so a bezel or protective setting is worth considering for everyday wear.
