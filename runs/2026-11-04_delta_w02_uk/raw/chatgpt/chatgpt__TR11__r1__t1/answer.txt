I would be very cautious about spending £2,000 on a gemstone ring from an unfamiliar website, especially if it's an independent online jeweller you've never heard of.
That doesn't automatically mean the site is a scam. There are legitimate small jewellery businesses in the UK. However, at this price point, you should do some checks before paying.
The good news is that there are ways to protect yourself if something goes wrong.
1. Five things to check before ordering
Company registration: Check whether the business has a genuine UK company registration number on Companies House. Check how long it has been trading and whether its details match the website.
Independent reviews: Look beyond testimonials on its own website. Check Trustpilot, Google reviews and Reddit. Look for complaints about non-delivery, refunds, misleading gemstones and poor-quality settings.
Gemstone certification: For a £2,000 ring, ask for an independent gemological report where appropriate. Make sure the report identifies the stone and any treatments. A certificate from the seller itself isn't the same as independent verification.
Returns policy: Check whether you can return the ring for a full refund. UK online purchases generally have a 14-day cancellation period, but personalised or bespoke jewellery can be exempt.
Contact details: Look for a genuine UK address, working telephone number and clear customer service arrangements.
2. How to pay safely

Credit card
If you're buying in the UK, paying by credit card can give you valuable protection under Section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act.
For a £2,000 purchase:
Section 75 generally applies when the cash price of the item is more than £100 and no more than £30,000.
It can make your credit card provider jointly liable if the seller breaches the contract or misrepresents the item.
PayPal or certain third-party payment arrangements can complicate Section 75 protection.
Debit cards may offer chargeback protection, but this isn't the same statutory protection.
Avoid bank transfers, cryptocurrency and payments outside the website's secure checkout.
