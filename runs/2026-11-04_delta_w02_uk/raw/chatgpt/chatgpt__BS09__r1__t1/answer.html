A few things I'd consider before buying

Full vs half eternity: Full eternity has stones all the way around, while half eternity has stones across the top. Half eternity is generally easier to resize.

Natural vs lab-grown: Check that the sapphires and diamonds are natural if that matters to you.

Gold vs platinum: 18ct gold and platinum are excellent choices for a substantial fine-jewellery band.

Setting: Channel settings are smooth and practical; claw settings show off more of the stones.

Hallmarking: Look for UK hallmarking and a clear returns policy.
