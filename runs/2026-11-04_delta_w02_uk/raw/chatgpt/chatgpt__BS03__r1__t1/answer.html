A few things I'd look for

When comparing these, I'd pay particular attention to:

Sapphire size: 6 × 4mm or larger gives a more noticeable centre stone.

Sapphire colour: Look for a rich, vivid royal blue rather than a very dark navy.

Diamond halo: Check the total diamond carat weight.

Metal: 18ct white gold is particularly beautiful with blue sapphire, although yellow gold gives a warmer, more traditional appearance.

Natural vs lab-grown: Check that the sapphire and diamonds are natural if that matters to you.
