Where can I buy dainty everyday gemstone jewelry in solid gold? I'm shopping in the UK.
If you're looking for dainty, delicate gemstone jewellery in genuine solid gold, I'd focus on independent fine-jewellery brands and smaller British designers rather than the usual demi-fine brands.
There are some lovely UK options, particularly for tiny sapphires, emeralds, opals, birthstones and delicate gemstone pendants.
One important distinction: look for 9ct, 14ct or 18ct solid gold, not 18ct gold vermeil or gold-plated sterling silver. The latter can look identical in photographs but isn't solid gold.
