The most important thing is to distinguish between:

Natural ruby: Mined from the earth. This is what I'd prioritise.

Lab-grown ruby: Real ruby chemically, but created in a laboratory.

Synthetic-looking red stones: Often red glass, garnet or imitation ruby, rather than ruby.

For your budget, I'd look for a small, genuine natural ruby with a good setting rather than a large, inexpensive red gemstone.

What I'd check before buying

A few things matter enormously when buying rubies:

Natural origin: Ask whether the ruby is mined or lab-grown.

Treatment: Heat treatment is common and generally acceptable. Avoid undisclosed fracture filling or glass filling.

Certification: Ask for an independent gemmological report, ideally from GIA, Gem-A or an equivalent laboratory.

Gold: Look for a genuine 375 hallmark for 9ct gold.

Chain: Check whether the chain is solid gold or merely gold-plated.
