Yes! Several UK jewellers offer gift-wrapping services, and some also provide a gift receipt or gift-friendly paperwork so the recipient doesn't see how much you spent.
For a gemstone necklace, I'd look particularly at jewellers that offer:
Gift wrapping: A presentation box, ribbon or branded gift packaging.
Gift receipt: A receipt without the purchase price, ideally with an exchange option.
Discreet delivery: No invoice showing the price inside the parcel.
Gemstone details: A card or certificate explaining the stone, where relevant.
One thing to check: a gift box isn't necessarily the same as complimentary gift wrapping, and a gift receipt isn't always automatically included.
