My advice before buying
If authenticity matters, check these details:
Natural gemstone: Ask whether the stone is mined/natural or lab-created.
Treatments: Many genuine gemstones are heat-treated, irradiated or fracture-filled. This isn't necessarily bad, but it should be disclosed.
Metal: Look for 925 sterling silver, 9ct gold or 14ct gold.
Certification: For more expensive ruby, sapphire and emerald earrings, ask for a gemstone identification report.
Avoid ambiguity: "Crystal", "simulated gemstone" and cubic zirconia are not the same as natural gemstones.
