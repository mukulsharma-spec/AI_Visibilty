What to look for when buying a ruby ring

A few things are worth checking before purchasing:

Natural vs lab-grown: Make sure the listing explicitly identifies which type of ruby you're buying.

Treatments: Many natural rubies are heat-treated. Ask whether the stone has been treated.

Gold purity: 9ct is more affordable, while 18ct contains more gold.

Certification: For an expensive ruby, request an independent gemstone report.

Returns: Check resizing, returns and refund policies before ordering.
