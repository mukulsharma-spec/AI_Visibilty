A sapphire engagement ring is a beautiful choice. It's timeless, a little less conventional than a diamond, and offers some gorgeous possibilities in terms of colour and design.

For the UK market, I'd look at independent fine jewellers as well as established engagement-ring retailers, particularly if you're considering a natural sapphire.

A little buying advice

Sapphire is a particularly good engagement-ring gemstone because it scores 9/10 on the Mohs hardness scale, making it suitable for everyday wear.

A few things to check:

Natural versus lab-grown sapphire.

Whether the stone is heat-treated.

Whether a reputable gem laboratory certificate is available, particularly for stones over 1 carat.

Whether the ring can accommodate a wedding band sitting flush alongside it.

UK prices vary considerably, but a useful initial budget guide is around £1,000–£5,000 for many natural sapphire designs.
