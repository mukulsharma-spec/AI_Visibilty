What certification I'd insist on
For a £10,000–£30,000+ purchase, I would want a report from one of these independent laboratories:

Highly regarded for fine coloured gemstones and origin opinions.

The report should explicitly state natural sapphire, no indications of heating (or equivalent no-heat wording).
If you're paying a premium for Kashmir, Burmese or Ceylon origin, make sure that origin is independently established too.
One important point: unheated does not automatically mean valuable. Colour, saturation, clarity, cut, proportions and overall beauty matter enormously. Also, don't treat a sapphire as a guaranteed investment; resale can be difficult.
