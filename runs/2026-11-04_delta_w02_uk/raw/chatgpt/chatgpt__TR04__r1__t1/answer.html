Important things to check before ordering

A 30-day return policy doesn't always mean every ring qualifies. Check:

Whether the 30 days start from dispatch, purchase or delivery.

Whether return postage is genuinely prepaid and insured.

Whether made-to-order or resized rings are excluded.

Whether natural and lab-grown gemstones are both eligible.

Whether the ring must be unworn, with certificates intact.
