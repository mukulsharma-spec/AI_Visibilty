What I'd look for at this budget
A few things are particularly important with tanzanite:
Colour: Look for a rich violet-blue or deep blue-purple. Avoid stones that look very pale or grey.
Stone quality: Ask whether it's natural tanzanite and whether the colour grading is independently assessed.
Gold: 9ct solid gold is a good choice for your budget.
Setting: A bezel or protective halo is worth considering because tanzanite is relatively delicate (6–7 on the Mohs hardness scale).
Certification: Ask for gemstone details and an independent certificate where possible.
One important point: tanzanite is not as durable as sapphire or diamond, so I'd avoid wearing it during exercise, gardening or heavy household work.
