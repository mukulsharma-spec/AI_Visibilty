A few things I'd look for when buying citrine
Natural citrine: Ask whether the gemstone is naturally occurring or heat-treated amethyst. Both are sold as citrine, but the distinction matters if you specifically want natural citrine.
Metal: 9ct or 18ct gold for a long-lasting fine-jewellery piece; sterling silver for a more affordable option.
Colour: Pale champagne citrine is subtle and elegant; deep golden-orange or honey citrine makes more of a statement.
Returns: Check the returns policy and whether the retailer provides gemstone details.
