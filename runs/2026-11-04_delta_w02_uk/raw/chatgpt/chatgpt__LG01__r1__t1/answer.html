A few things to check before buying

Lab-grown vs simulated: Lab-grown sapphire is actual sapphire (corundum), whereas glass or cubic zirconia marketed as sapphire-look stones is different.

Metal quality: Check whether the piece is solid 9ct/18ct gold, gold vermeil or gold-plated silver.

Certification: For expensive pieces, ask for an independent gemstone report, ideally IGI or another recognised gemmological laboratory.

Returns: Check UK returns periods, resizing policies and warranties.
