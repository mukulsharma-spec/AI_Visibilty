What styles to consider
Here are three particularly lovely directions for a pear-shaped emerald engagement ring.
Classic solitaire – Elegant, understated and lets the emerald take centre stage.
Diamond halo – More sparkle and a glamorous vintage feel.
Diamond shoulders – A beautiful balance of green colour and brilliance.
Minimalist bezel – Modern, distinctive and offers extra protection around the emerald.
