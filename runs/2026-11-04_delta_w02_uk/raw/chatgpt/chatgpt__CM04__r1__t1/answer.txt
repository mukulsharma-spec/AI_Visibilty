One important thing about ruby quality
I'd pay particular attention to these distinctions:
Natural, heat-treated ruby: A perfectly reasonable choice. Heat treatment is common and accepted.
Natural, unheated ruby: Rarer and considerably more expensive.
Lab-created ruby: Chemically and physically ruby, but not mined. Usually much more affordable.
Glass-filled ruby: I'd avoid this for a quality ring. It's a lower-grade material with significant durability concerns.
For a substantial natural ruby, ask for an independent gemmological report.
