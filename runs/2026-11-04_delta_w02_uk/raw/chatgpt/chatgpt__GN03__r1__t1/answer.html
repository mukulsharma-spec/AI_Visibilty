A quick tip when buying jewellery online in the UK

Pay attention to the actual metal:

Solid gold: 9ct, 14ct or 18ct. Best for long-term wear.

Gold vermeil: Gold plating over sterling silver; a good middle ground.

Gold-plated: Usually more affordable, but the finish can wear away.

Sterling silver: Look for 925 silver.

For precious-metal purchases, check the UK hallmarking requirements and consider retailers carrying the Assay Assured trustmark.
