What I'd check before buying

A few important things when comparing natural and lab-grown gemstones:

Origin: Make sure the retailer explicitly identifies whether the stone is natural or laboratory-created.

Treatments: Natural sapphires, rubies and emeralds can be heat-treated, oiled or otherwise enhanced. Check that this is disclosed.

Certification: For expensive coloured stones, look for independent reports from GIA, SSEF, Gübelin or AGL where appropriate.

Photographs: Prefer actual photographs or videos of the exact stone rather than stock images.

Returns: Check the returns policy, particularly for bespoke or commissioned jewellery.

The National Association of Jewellers also has guidance on gemstone descriptions and the distinction between natural and artificial materials.
