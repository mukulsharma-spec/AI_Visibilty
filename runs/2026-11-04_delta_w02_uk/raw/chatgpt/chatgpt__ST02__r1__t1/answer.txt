My buying advice
If you specifically want genuine peridot rather than green cubic zirconia, look for:
Natural peridot explicitly stated in the product description.
925 sterling silver, 9ct gold or gold vermeil settings.
Stone dimensions and total carat weight.
A returns policy and clear UK delivery terms.
