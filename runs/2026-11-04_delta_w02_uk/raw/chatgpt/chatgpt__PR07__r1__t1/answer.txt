What to look for when spending £5,000+
A few things are particularly important with coloured gemstones:
Natural versus lab-grown: Establish which you're buying. At this budget, I'd investigate natural stones.
Treatments: Ask whether the gemstone is heated, unheated, fracture-filled, or otherwise treated. Treatments can substantially affect value.
Origin: For important sapphires, rubies and emeralds, ask about geographical origin and whether it has been independently established.
Independent certification: Request a report from a reputable gemological laboratory, such as SSEF, Gübelin, GIA or AGL where appropriate.
Return and buyback policies: Check these before paying, particularly for bespoke commissions.
Insurance valuation: Make sure you receive a proper valuation and UK hallmarking documentation.
One important distinction: a beautiful gemstone isn't automatically a valuable investment. Buy primarily for its beauty, rarity and craftsmanship.
