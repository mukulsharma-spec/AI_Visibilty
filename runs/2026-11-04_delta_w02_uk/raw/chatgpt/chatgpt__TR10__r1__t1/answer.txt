What I'd check before ordering
Especially when buying expensive gemstone jewellery online:
Gemstone authenticity: Does the listing explicitly state natural, lab-created or treated?
Metal quality: Check whether the setting is solid 9ct/14ct/18ct gold, sterling silver or gold vermeil.
Gemstone certification: For expensive rubies, emeralds, sapphires and tanzanite, ask about independent grading or certification.
Returns: Check the return window and whether refunds are available on sale items.
Recent reviews: Look at negative reviews as well as positive ones, particularly concerning stone quality, sizing and returns.
