A few things to check before buying
With sapphires, I'd pay particular attention to:
Natural vs lab-grown: Make sure the listing explicitly says natural if that's important to you.
Treatment: Heat treatment is common and generally accepted. Ask about diffusion or other treatments.
Total carat weight: Check whether the stated weight is for both earrings combined.
Colour: Look for a rich royal or cornflower blue, rather than very dark navy.
Certification: Ask for gemstone details and any available independent grading documentation.
