A few things I'd look for

10

Aquamarine is a beautiful engagement stone, but there are a few things worth knowing:

Colour: Look for a medium to deep blue rather than a very pale, almost colourless stone.

Stone quality: Ask for natural aquamarine and whether it has been heat-treated.

Durability: Aquamarine is 7.5–8 on the Mohs hardness scale. It's suitable for everyday wear with reasonable care, but isn't as hard as sapphire or diamond.

Setting: A protective setting with good stone security is sensible for a ring worn every day.

Certification: Ask for a gemstone description and an independent valuation for insurance.
