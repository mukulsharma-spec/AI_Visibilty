Where the real difference lies: emerald quality

This is the most important part of the decision.

Neither retailer should get an automatic pass simply because it advertises a higher quality grade. Emerald grading isn't as standardised as diamond grading.

One thing I wouldn't compromise on: certification

For an engagement ring, I'd want:

A report from an independent gemmological laboratory, ideally GIA, SSEF, Gübelin or AGL.

Written disclosure of oiling, resin filling or other treatments.

Confirmation that the emerald is natural rather than laboratory-grown.

Actual photographs or video of the specific stone, not just a representative product photograph.

A retailer's own Certificate of Authenticity is useful, but it isn't equivalent to an independent laboratory report.

Also, emeralds are relatively brittle despite their reasonable hardness. I'd favour a bezel or protective halo setting, or at least a well-protected four- or six-prong setting, for everyday wear.
