What should you look for?

Before ordering, I'd check:

Natural vs lab-grown: Make sure the listing explicitly identifies which it is.

Treatment: Ask whether the sapphire is heat-treated or unheated.

Certification: For a more expensive natural sapphire, request an independent gemmological laboratory report.

Gold: Look for 9ct, 14ct or 18ct solid gold rather than gold-plated metal.

Returns: Check the return policy, particularly for bespoke rings.

Yellow sapphire is a durable gemstone (9 on the Mohs hardness scale), making it a good choice for everyday jewellery.
