Which online jewelers let me pick the metal and gemstone size for a pendant? I'm shopping in the UK.
If you want to design a pendant where you can choose both the precious metal (yellow, white or rose gold, platinum, etc.) and the gemstone size, there are several UK jewellers worth looking at.
I'd divide them into two categories: jewellers with online customisation tools and jewellers who will make a pendant to your exact specifications.
Best UK online jewellers to try
1. Juvetti
Best for choosing stone size
Choose yellow, white or rose gold in 9k, 14k or 18k, or platinum.
Pendant setting accommodates stones up to 5 carats.
Select your diamond or lab-grown gemstone separately, then add the pendant setting.
Made to order, with an estimated dispatch of around six weeks.
Website: Juvetti solitaire pendant setting .
2. Pruden & Smith
Best for bespoke gemstone pendants
Sterling silver, 9ct and 18ct gold, platinum.
Gemstones include emerald, sapphire, ruby, aquamarine, opal and more.
Many designs can be personalised with your chosen gemstone.
Can make a fully bespoke pendant around a stone you select, including larger or unusual sizes.
Website: Gemstone pendants .
3. Austen & Blake
Choose metal colour and chain type.
Gemstone choices include sapphire, emerald, ruby, amethyst and citrine.
Made-to-order pendants with customisation of gemstone and diamond specifications.
Particularly good for classic solitaire and halo designs.
Website: Gemstone pendants and necklaces .
4. H.K. Jewellery
Extensive gemstone, cut, colour and metal choices.
Yellow, white and rose gold, platinum, palladium and sterling silver.
Offers online consultations and bespoke design.
A good option if you want control over the exact gemstone cut and dimensions.
Website: Pendants and necklaces .
5. ASD Jewellers
Sterling silver, 9ct and 18ct yellow, white and rose gold.
Choose from a selection of gemstones.
Offers online metal and stone selection.
Some designs have fixed stone dimensions, so check before ordering.
