Understanding gemstone quality grades
One important distinction: unlike diamonds, coloured gemstones don't have one universally accepted grading system.
You should be able to compare the following characteristics:
Quality factor | What to look for
Colour | Hue, tone and saturation
Clarity | Eye-clean, slightly included, included
Cut | Excellent proportions and symmetry
Carat | Weight of the gemstone
Treatment | Heated, untreated, oiled, etc.
Origin | Country of origin, where documented
For example, you might want to compare three sapphires:
Commercial grade
Lighter colour, visible inclusions
Fine grade
Vivid colour, good clarity
Premium grade
Exceptional colour and appearance
These are illustrative descriptions, not official universal grades.
