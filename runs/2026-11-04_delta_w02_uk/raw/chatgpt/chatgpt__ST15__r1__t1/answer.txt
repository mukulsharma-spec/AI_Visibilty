A few things to know before buying
Look for natural morganite rather than simulated morganite.
Morganite is often heat-treated to improve its pink colour. This is a normal treatment, but ask the jeweller to disclose it.
Look for a lovely pink or peach colour rather than something so pale it looks almost colourless.
Morganite has a hardness of 7.5–8, so it's suitable for rings with reasonable care. A protective setting is a good idea.
