Which gemstone should you choose?

Here are four I'd particularly consider for a promise ring.

Blue sapphire

Loyalty, trust and faithfulness. Excellent durability for everyday wear.

Emerald

Ruby

Passion, devotion and romance. A lovely choice for a relationship.

Aquamarine

Calmness, harmony and lasting connection. Soft, elegant blue.

One important buying tip: ask whether the gemstone is natural, lab-grown or imitation, and whether it has been treated. Treatments aren't necessarily bad, but they should be disclosed. The National Association of Jewellers has guidance on gemstone descriptions. National Association of Jewellers
