What to look for when buying Akoya pearls
A few things make a considerable difference to quality:
Pearl size: 6–7mm is classic and elegant; 7–8mm makes more of a statement.
Lustre: Look for bright, sharp reflections rather than a dull, chalky appearance.
Surface: Ideally, pearls should have very few visible blemishes.
Colour: White with pink or rose overtones is particularly desirable.
Construction: Look for individually knotted pearls and a solid gold clasp.
Provenance: Confirm that the pearls are Japanese Akoya cultured pearls, rather than freshwater pearls marketed simply as Akoya-style.
