What I'd recommend

Before buying, check whether the ruby is:

Natural ruby: A genuine mined gemstone.

Lab-grown ruby: Real ruby with the same basic chemical composition, but laboratory-created.

Synthetic or imitation ruby: Check the seller's exact terminology, as these descriptions can be used differently.

Also ask whether the ruby has been heat-treated, whether a certificate is supplied, and whether the chain is included.
