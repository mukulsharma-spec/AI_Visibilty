What I'd look for when buying

Natural ruby: Make sure it isn't synthetic or lab-created unless that's what you want.

Treatment: Ask whether it's heated, unheated or fracture-filled.

Certification: For a significant purchase, request an independent gem laboratory report.

Gold: 18ct yellow gold is particularly beautiful with deep red rubies.

Colour: Look for a rich, vibrant red rather than a dull or overly dark stone.
