Natural or lab-grown emerald?
This is an important distinction when shopping.
Natural emerald
Mined from the earth
Naturally occurring inclusions
Generally more expensive
Better if natural origin matters to you
Lab-grown emerald
Created in a laboratory
Same basic chemical composition
Often clearer and more affordable
Great for getting a larger-looking stone
Both are genuine emeralds, but lab-grown stones should always be disclosed as such. TJC UK +1
What I'd recommend checking before buying
Metal: 9ct or 18ct solid gold if you want something long-lasting.
Stone treatment: Ask whether a natural emerald has been oiled or resin-treated.
Certification: Ask for a gemstone report, particularly for a more expensive natural emerald.
Setting: A bezel or protective setting helps prevent chips.
Returns: Check resizing, returns and warranty policies.
Emeralds are relatively hard but can be brittle, so they need a little more care than sapphires or diamonds. The Hatton Garden Guide +1
