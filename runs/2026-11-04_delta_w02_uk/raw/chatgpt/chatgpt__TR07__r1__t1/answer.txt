I care about ethics. Where can I buy colored gemstones that are ethically sourced? I'm shopping in the UK.
If ethical sourcing is a priority, I'd recommend buying from specialist gemstone dealers who can explain the stone's journey from mine to market, rather than relying on a jewellery brand simply describing its products as ethical.
There are some particularly interesting UK suppliers, including businesses that work directly with artisanal mining communities in Sri Lanka, Tanzania and other gemstone-producing countries.
One important distinction: a gemstone being conflict-free doesn't automatically mean it was mined with fair wages, safe working conditions or minimal environmental damage.

What actually makes a gemstone ethically sourced?
I would look at five things before purchasing.
Factor | What I'd want to see
Mine origin | Country and ideally the specific mine or mining community
Labour | No child labour, forced labour or exploitative wages
Mining conditions | Worker safety and responsible environmental practices
Cutting | Where the gemstone was cut and under what conditions
Traceability | Documented supply chain, not just a verbal assurance
A particularly important point: GIA certification is not ethical sourcing certification. It can help establish gemstone identity, origin (where determinable) and treatments, but it doesn't establish fair labour conditions.
There is currently no universally recognised independent certification covering every type of coloured gemstone from mine to market. The Curious Gem +1

Question about gemstone sourcing and provenance
Hello,
I'm interested in purchasing some coloured gemstones and ethical sourcing is particularly important to me.
Before purchasing, could you please tell me:
What country and, where possible, which mine or mining community the gemstones originate from?
Are the gemstones traceable from the mine through to cutting and polishing?
What assurances do you have regarding fair wages, worker safety and the absence of child or forced labour?
Are your suppliers independently audited or involved in recognised responsible mining initiatives?
Can you provide documentation relating to the gemstone's origin and sourcing?
I'm particularly interested in supporting responsible artisanal mining communities and ensuring that the people involved in producing these gemstones are treated fairly.
Many thanks!
