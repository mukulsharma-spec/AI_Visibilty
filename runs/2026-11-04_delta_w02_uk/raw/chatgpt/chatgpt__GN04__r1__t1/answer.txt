A few things to check before ordering
For custom jewellery, I'd pay particular attention to:
Metal: Solid 9ct, 14ct or 18ct gold versus gold vermeil or gold plating.
Design approval: Can you see a sketch or 3D CAD rendering before production?
Stone quality: Are diamonds certified, and are gemstones natural or lab-grown?
Returns: Bespoke and personalised pieces often have different cancellation and return policies.
Hallmarking: Check that precious-metal pieces meet UK hallmarking requirements.
