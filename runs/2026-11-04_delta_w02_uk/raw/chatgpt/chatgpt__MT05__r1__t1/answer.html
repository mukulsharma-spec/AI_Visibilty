For a gemstone ring, I'd recommend finding a jeweller who offers both professional ring resizing and engraving, ideally with experience working with precious and semi-precious stones.

There are a few things worth checking before handing over your ring:

Stone safety: Some gemstones are sensitive to heat, so resizing should be assessed based on the stone and setting.

Setting security: Ask them to check the claws/prongs and stone stability after resizing.

Engraving: Check whether they offer internal (inside the band) or external engraving.

Insurance: Look for insured handling while the ring is being worked on.
