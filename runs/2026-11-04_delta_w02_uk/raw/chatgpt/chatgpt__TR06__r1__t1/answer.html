Which online jewelers disclose gemstone treatments like heating or filling? I'm shopping in the UK.

Yes. There are several UK-based gemstone specialists and jewellers that are notably more transparent about gemstone treatments than mainstream jewellery retailers.

I'd particularly look for sellers who disclose heat treatment, oiling, fracture filling, glass filling, diffusion and dyeing separately. Simply describing a stone as "natural" is not enough: a natural ruby or sapphire can still have undergone substantial treatment.

Here are the retailers I'd put on your shortlist.

UK retailers worth considering

1. Apsara Gems

Strong disclosure policy

apsara.co.uk

Based in Hatton Garden, London.

Sells both heated and unheated rubies and sapphires.

States that it has a policy of full disclosure of gemstone treatments.

Does not sell glass-filled or dyed gemstones.

Can arrange independent laboratory testing.

Its treatment terminology includes:

H – standard heat treatment.

NEHT – no evidence of heat treatment.

H-D-Ti – titanium diffusion.

H-D-Be – beryllium diffusion.

I particularly like that it distinguishes ordinary heating from diffusion treatment rather than grouping everything under "heated". apsara.co.uk +2

2. Vivaaz Gems & Jewellery

Product-level disclosure

Vivaaz – gemstone treatments policy

This is particularly interesting because it says that every product page has a specific Treatment line under the stone details.

Its terminology includes:

None – natural and untreated.

Heated – heat-treated.

Oiled – oil in fractures, commonly used for emeralds.

Irradiated.

Dyed or coated.

Glass-filled – says it does not sell glass-filled stones as natural.

It also says it will use "treatment not tested" rather than guessing when it cannot establish the treatment.

This is exactly the kind of product-level transparency I'd want when shopping online. I'd check UK delivery availability before ordering. vivaazgems.com

3. Topgem (Apsara)

Detailed treatment explanations

Topgem – gemstone treatments

Topgem provides a particularly detailed explanation of gemstone enhancement.

It explicitly discusses:

Heat treatment.

Emerald oiling.

Titanium and beryllium diffusion.

Glass filling.

Dyeing and irradiation.

It says it sells heated rubies and sapphires but does not sell lead-glass-filled rubies or sapphires, beryllium-treated stones, dyed or composite stones.

It also offers independent laboratory reports for a fee. Note that Topgem and Apsara are associated businesses, so I wouldn't count them as two independent endorsements. TopGem +1

5. R.M. Shah Ltd

R.M. Shah

This retailer publishes a gemstone treatment policy.

It discusses:

One caveat: its policy says treatment information is provided to the best of its knowledge and that undiscovered treatments are possible. I'd therefore ask for confirmation about the particular stone you're considering. R. M. Shah Ltd.

What I'd insist on before buying

Different treatments have very different implications for value, durability and care.

Treatment | What I'd want disclosed

Heat treatment | Whether heated or unheated

Emerald oiling | Type and degree of oiling

Resin filling | Whether fractures have been resin-filled

Glass filling | Whether lead-glass or other glass is present

Diffusion | Whether titanium or beryllium diffusion was used

Dyeing | Whether colour has been artificially added

Irradiation | Whether the stone has been irradiated

One particularly important distinction:

Heated sapphire: A widely accepted and generally stable treatment.

Unheated sapphire: Often considerably more valuable, all else being equal.

Glass-filled ruby: A substantially different commercial proposition from an ordinary heated ruby. It should be explicitly identified as glass-filled or treated ruby.

The UK's National Association of Jewellers has published useful guidance on this. It says that material treatment information should be disclosed online before purchase. Certain treatments, particularly lead-glass filling, require specific and prominent disclosure. National Association of Jewellers

My recommendation

I'd start with Apsara for loose coloured gemstones, and Vivaaz for its product-by-product treatment disclosures. For a finished bespoke ring, I'd also consider Angels.

Before paying, I'd send the seller this message:

Writing

Hello,

I'm interested in purchasing one of your natural coloured gemstones, but gemstone treatment disclosure is particularly important to me.

Could you please confirm:

Whether the stone has been heat-treated.

Whether it has undergone any fracture filling, glass filling, resin filling or oiling.

Whether it has undergone diffusion, irradiation, dyeing or coating.

Whether its treatment status has been independently verified.

Whether an independent gemmological laboratory report is available.

I'd also appreciate confirmation that the treatment information will be included on the invoice.

I'm particularly interested in stones with minimal treatment or completely untreated stones.

Many thanks!
