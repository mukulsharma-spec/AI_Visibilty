Which gemstones are worth considering?

Emerald

Rich, sophisticated, timeless.

Sapphire

Elegant, versatile and durable.

Tourmaline

Distinctive colours and personality.

Aquamarine

Soft, delicate and understated.

A little buying advice: prioritise solid 9ct, 14ct or 18ct gold, ask whether the stone is natural or lab-created, and get written disclosure of any treatments. Sapphires and rubies are particularly good for frequent wear; emeralds need a little more care.
