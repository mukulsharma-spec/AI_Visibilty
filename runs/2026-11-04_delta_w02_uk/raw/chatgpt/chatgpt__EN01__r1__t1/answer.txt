What should you expect to pay?
Indicative UK prices for a 1-carat solitaire:
Lab-grown: £1,000–£2,500
Natural/mined: £3,000–£6,000+
A recent UK market price comparison puts the median at approximately £1,595 for lab-grown and £3,348 for natural 1-carat solitaire rings.
A few things I'd recommend checking
Cut: Excellent or Ideal cut for maximum sparkle.
Colour: G or better is a good starting point.
Clarity: VS1 or VS2 offers a good balance.
Certification: Prefer an independent GIA or IGI grading report.
Metal: 18ct gold or platinum for a premium setting.
