Styles I'd consider
Here are four particularly lovely directions:
Oval sapphire solitaire – Elegant, timeless and understated.
Sapphire halo – A sapphire surrounded by tiny diamonds.
Three-stone sapphire – Sapphire with diamonds on either side.
Modern bezel setting – Minimalist, contemporary and protective.
A few things to check before buying
Ask whether the sapphire is natural or lab-created.
Ask whether it has been heat-treated.
Request a gemstone certificate, particularly for a larger or expensive stone.
Sapphire is 9 on the Mohs hardness scale, making it a good choice for everyday wear.
See the actual stone in daylight, as deep blue sapphires can look almost black under some lighting.
