A few things to check before buying
If you're buying jewellery intended to last for years, I'd prioritise:
Metal: Solid 9ct, 14ct or 18ct gold, or platinum, rather than gold-plated jewellery.
Hallmarking: Check for the appropriate UK hallmark on precious-metal pieces.
Diamonds: Check whether they're natural or lab-grown and whether independent certification is provided for significant stones.
Returns: Look for clear returns, resizing and repair policies.
