My advice before buying
If you want a genuine sapphire necklace, check these details before ordering:
Natural or lab-created: Make sure the listing clearly specifies which it is.
Metal: 9ct or 18ct solid gold is a good choice for long-term wear.
Treatments: Natural sapphires are commonly heat-treated. Check whether this is disclosed.
Certification: For a more expensive sapphire, ask for an independent gemstone report.
Returns: Check the UK returns policy before purchasing.
