3. Important if you're buying in the UK

I'd pay attention to these things before ordering:

Currency: Make sure you're seeing GBP rather than USD.

Import charges: Check whether UK VAT and any applicable import charges are included at checkout.

Returns: Check the UK return address and whether return postage is covered.

Metal: Compare 9ct, 14ct and 18ct gold like-for-like.

Stone dimensions: Compare the sapphire's actual measurements in millimetres, not just carat weight.
