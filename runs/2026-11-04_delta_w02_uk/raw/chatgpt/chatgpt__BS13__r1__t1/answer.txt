If you're shopping for a sapphire engagement ring, I'd recommend looking for a jeweller who can make a matching or shaped wedding band, rather than buying a standard straight wedding ring.
Sapphire engagement rings can be a little trickier to match, particularly if the stone is set in a raised setting, has a halo, or sits in a vintage-style mount.
The best options are usually:
Contoured wedding bands: Curved to fit snugly around the sapphire engagement ring.
Custom-made bands: Made to match the exact metal, finish and design of your engagement ring.
Diamond-set bands: Particularly lovely alongside a blue sapphire, especially in white gold or platinum.
Plain shaped bands: Elegant if you want the sapphire to remain the focal point.
