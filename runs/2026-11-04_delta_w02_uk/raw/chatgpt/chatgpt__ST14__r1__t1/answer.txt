What I'd personally look for
A few things to avoid overpaying:
Natural emerald: Check that the listing explicitly says natural emerald, not green crystal or cubic zirconia.
Gold: 9ct solid gold is a good starting point. Gold-plated silver is cheaper but less durable.
Treatments: Most natural emeralds are oil-treated. This is normal, but ask about the treatment level.
Certification: Look for details about gemstone authenticity and treatments.
