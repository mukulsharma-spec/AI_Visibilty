One important thing about opal

Opal is softer and more delicate than diamonds or sapphires (around 5.5–6.5 on the Mohs hardness scale). It can scratch or crack, so if it's going to be an engagement ring worn regularly, I'd recommend:

A protective bezel setting rather than exposed prongs.

Solid gold or platinum.

Asking whether the stone is solid opal, a doublet or a triplet.

Checking the care requirements and insurance coverage.

A beautiful opal can absolutely make a meaningful engagement ring, but it does need a little more care.
