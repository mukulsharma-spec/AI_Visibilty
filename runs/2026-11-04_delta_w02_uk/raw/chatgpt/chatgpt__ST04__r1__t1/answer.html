A few things to look for

Make sure the listing specifically says London Blue Topaz, rather than simply blue topaz. London Blue is the darker, richer, inky blue variety.

I'd also check:

Whether the gemstone is natural.

Whether the metal is solid gold or gold-plated.

Whether the ring comes with a gemstone certificate or independent appraisal.
