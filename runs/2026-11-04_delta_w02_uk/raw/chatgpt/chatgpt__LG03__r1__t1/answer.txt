What I'd look for before buying
A lab-grown ruby is a real ruby: it has the same chemical composition and crystal structure as a mined ruby. It isn't the same as a red glass or synthetic spinel imitation.
I'd check these five things:
Gemstone: Make sure the description explicitly says lab-grown or lab-created ruby, not simulated ruby.
Metal: Ideally 9ct, 14ct or 18ct solid gold, or platinum.
Documentation: Ask for confirmation of the gemstone identity and metal purity.
Colour: Look for a rich, vivid red rather than a pinkish or overly dark stone.
Returns: Check the return and resizing policies, particularly for made-to-order rings.
