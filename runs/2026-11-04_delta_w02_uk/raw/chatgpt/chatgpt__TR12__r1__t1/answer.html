What sort of laboratory report should you look for?

Not all gemstone certificates are equally useful. I'd prioritise reports from:

Laboratory

Particularly relevant for

Gübelin Gem Lab

High-end coloured gemstones

SSEF

Rubies, sapphires and emeralds

GIA

Broad range of gemstones

GRS

Rubies and sapphires

Gem-A

Gemstone identification and gemmological assessment

Ideally, the report should establish:

Whether the gemstone is natural or laboratory-grown.

Whether it has been treated (for example, heat treatment in sapphires or oiling in emeralds).

Geographic origin, if determinable and relevant.

Gemstone identity and weight.

One important point: a retailer's own authenticity certificate is not the same as an independent laboratory report.
