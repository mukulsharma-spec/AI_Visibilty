My buying advice
For a pair you'll treasure for years, I'd prioritise:
18ct gold rather than 9ct, particularly for a luxury purchase.
Natural rubies, if that's important to you. Check that they are not lab-grown or synthetic.
Ruby colour: look for a rich, vivid red rather than brownish or overly dark stones.
Diamond quality: ideally G–H colour and VS–SI clarity.
Secure settings and good butterfly or screw-back fittings.
One important distinction: some retailers advertise the combined carat weight of both earrings, rather than the weight of each individual stone.
