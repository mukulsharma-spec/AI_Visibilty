What to check before buying
One important distinction: make sure you know whether you're buying:
Natural emerald: Earth-mined gemstone, generally more expensive.
Lab-grown emerald: Real emerald with essentially the same chemical and physical properties, usually considerably less expensive.
Emerald-coloured stone: Could be glass, cubic zirconia or another imitation rather than emerald.
For natural emeralds, ask about oil or resin treatments and request an independent gem laboratory report for substantial purchases.
