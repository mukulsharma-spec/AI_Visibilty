A few things I'd insist on
For the necklace you're describing, I'd look for:
Solid 14ct gold, not gold vermeil or plating.
585 gold fineness.
A single natural gemstone, if that's your preference.
A secure four- or six-claw setting, or a bezel setting.
A chain included in the price.
Clear gemstone treatment and origin disclosures.
