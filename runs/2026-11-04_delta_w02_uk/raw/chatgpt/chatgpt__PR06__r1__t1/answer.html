Fine jewelry gifts with real gemstones under $200 I'm shopping in the UK.

For a gift under £200 in the UK, I'd focus on genuine gemstones set in sterling silver, 9ct gold or 18ct gold vermeil.

The sweet spot is a beautiful natural gemstone rather than a tiny diamond or an expensive designer logo. Think sapphire, amethyst, garnet, peridot, moonstone, topaz or emerald.

One important distinction: real gemstones don't necessarily mean solid gold. You can get lovely genuine-stone jewellery in sterling silver or gold vermeil within this budget.
