What I'd look for when buying
A few things are particularly important with coloured gemstones:
Natural or lab-created: Check that the listing explicitly identifies which you're buying.
Treatments: Heat treatment, dyeing and fracture filling should be disclosed where applicable.
Metal quality: Look for sterling silver, 9ct, 14ct or 18ct gold, depending on budget.
Hallmarking: Check UK hallmarking requirements for precious-metal jewellery.
Gemstone photographs: Look for close-up photographs and ideally videos of the actual piece.
