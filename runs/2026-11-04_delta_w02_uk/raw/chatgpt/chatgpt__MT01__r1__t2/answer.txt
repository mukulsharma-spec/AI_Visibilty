My recommendation
I'd start with The Antique Jewellery Company if flexibility is your priority.
However, there's one important thing with engagement rings: check whether the return policy changes if you order a ring in a particular size, have it resized, or commission a bespoke sapphire setting.
Before purchasing, I'd also ask each jeweller to confirm in writing that you can return the ring for a full refund if you simply decide you don't love it.
One question: are you looking for a ready-made ring that you can return, or would you consider having a sapphire ring made to order?
That distinction makes quite a big difference to your return protection.
