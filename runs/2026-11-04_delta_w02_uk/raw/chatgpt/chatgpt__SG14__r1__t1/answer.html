For a nurse who uses her hands all day, I'd look for a low-profile, bezel-set sapphire ring with a smooth, rounded setting. It's a lovely choice because the bezel surrounds the sapphire rather than leaving exposed claws (prongs) that can catch on gloves, clothing or equipment.

I'd prioritise:

Full bezel setting: metal surrounds the entire stone.

Low-set stone: sits close to the finger, rather than raised.

Oval, round or cushion sapphire: ideally without sharp corners.

Comfort-fit band: smooth inside and not too wide.

18ct gold or platinum: durable options for everyday wear.

I'd avoid halo settings, cathedral shoulders and high-set solitaires for her particular job.
