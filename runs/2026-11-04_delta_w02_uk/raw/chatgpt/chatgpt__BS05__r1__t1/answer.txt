What I'd look for in your ring
My favourite combination would be:
Centre stone: 1.5–2ct emerald-cut blue sapphire.
Side stones: tapered baguette or trapezoid diamonds.
Setting: platinum or 18ct white gold.
Band: slim, plain band to keep the centre stone prominent.
Setting protection: secure claws around the sapphire's corners.
One important consideration: Ask whether the sapphire is natural or lab-grown, whether it has been heat-treated, and for an independent gemstone report. Also check the returns policy, especially for bespoke orders.
