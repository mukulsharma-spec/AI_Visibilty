A few things to consider before buying

For everyday wear: Sapphire and ruby are excellent choices because they're relatively hard-wearing.

For emeralds: Look for protective settings, as emeralds can be more vulnerable to damage.

For authenticity: Ask whether the ring is genuinely antique or newly made in an antique-inspired design.

For peace of mind: Check gemstone treatments, certification, resizing options and the jeweller's return policy.
