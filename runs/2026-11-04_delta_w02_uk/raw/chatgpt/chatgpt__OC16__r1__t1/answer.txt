My shopping advice
For something timeless: choose a natural opal pendant in 9ct yellow gold.
For something feminine and delicate: choose a pink tourmaline necklace.
For a meaningful everyday gift: sterling silver opal earrings are a lovely choice.
One thing to check before buying: whether the opal is natural, laboratory-created or simulated. These are different materials and can have quite different prices.
